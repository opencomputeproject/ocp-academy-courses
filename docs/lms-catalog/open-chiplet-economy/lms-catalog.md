**Title**\
Open Chiplet Economy: Business and Technology of Chiplet-based Systems

**Short Description**  
Explore chiplet architecture, packaging, interfaces, testing, economics, and the standards enabling an open chiplet ecosystem.

**Long Description**

This series connects the business case for chiplets with the engineering decisions needed to build viable systems. Explore architecture, packaging, communication, test, cost, and interoperability, then examine supplier qualification and opportunities to contribute to the Open Chiplet Economy. Narrated explanations, technical videos, and knowledge checks connect component choices to complete system outcomes.

**Overall duration: 150 minutes**

**Module 1: Introduction to Chiplet-based Systems and Technology — 20 minutes.**\
Explore the motivations for chiplets, including process selection, yield, reuse, and product flexibility. Understand how open interfaces, standards, and integration tools support the ecosystem.

**Module 2: Chiplet Architecture: Breaking the Limits of Monolithic Chips — 15 minutes.**\
Compare aggregation and disaggregation, evaluate partition boundaries, and identify the traffic, latency, and interface overhead that must be considered during architectural planning.

**Module 3: Chiplet Advanced Packaging — 20 minutes.**\
Compare substrates, fan-out, bridges, interposers, and hybrid bonding. Connect packaging choices to interconnect density, manufacturing yield, test access, power delivery, and thermal design.

**Module 4: Chiplet Die-to-Die Interfaces — 15 minutes.**\
Distinguish physical signaling, link behavior, and protocol meaning. Compare interface metrics and examine how standards, versions, profiles, and implementation evidence affect compatibility.

**Module 5: Chiplet Testability — 15 minutes.**\
Examine testing at die, package, and system stages. Explore controllability, observability, test access, and the information suppliers and integrators need for coverage and diagnosis.

**Module 6: Understanding Chiplet Cost Structure — 15 minutes.**\
Evaluate engineering investment and recurring production costs. Consider yield, packaging, assembly, screening, reuse, and supply assumptions when comparing cost per shipped good system.

**Module 7: Chiplet Standardization and Interoperability — 15 minutes.**\
Explore models, exchange formats, integration workflows, and the 3DK framework. Understand the roles of assembly, material, and test design kits in supporting interoperability.

**Module 8: Chiplets in a Marketplace — 15 minutes.**\
Follow a chiplet from catalog discovery to system qualification. Examine the technical collateral, support, supply commitments, and lifecycle responsibilities needed for successful procurement and integration.

**Module 9: What's Next for Chiplets and How to Get Involved — 20 minutes.**\
Examine emerging ecosystem directions, distinguish published contributions from proposals, and identify practical ways to participate. Bring architectural, commercial, and trust considerations into a system decision brief.
