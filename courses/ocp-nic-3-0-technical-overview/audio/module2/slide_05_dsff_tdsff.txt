# pronunciation: DSFF -> D S F F; TDSFF -> T D S F F; SFF -> S F F; 4C+ -> four C plus; PCIe -> P C I E

D S F F and T D S F F use the dual-width mechanical path. In a chassis designed for conversion, two adjacent S F F positions include a removable middle guide rail. Removing that rail creates the space for one D S F F card. This is a mechanical comparison, not an instruction to remove a powered card.

The D S F F card aligns with Primary and Secondary four C plus connectors. The connector pitch is eighty-one point five five millimeters, which is smaller than the spacing of two normal adjacent S F F slots. This means faceplates with thumb-screw implementations are not supported when that convertible position is used as two S F F slots. Mechanical compatibility must be designed into the chassis.

Across both connectors, D S F F supports up to thirty-two P C I E lanes and up to one hundred sixty watts delivered at the card edge. Cooling capacity remains dependent on the operating conditions. T D S F F keeps the same board outline and electrical model but permits a taller application. The bottom-side insulator or insulative backing plate belongs to the card construction. It is not a loose installation spacer between a finished card and the baseboard.
