**Title**\
OCP NIC 3.0 Technical Overview

**Short Description**\
Review OCP NIC 3.0 form factors, interfaces, management, thermal design, and implementation compliance.

**Long Description**

Build a technical overview of OCP NIC 3.0 across mechanical, electrical, management, thermal, and compliance boundaries. Compare form factors and connector arrangements, trace slot ownership and power behavior, and review the firmware, signal-integrity, and environmental evidence needed for integration. Connect the specification to ecosystem resources and implementation planning.

**Overall duration: 50 minutes**

**Module 1: Introduction to OCP NIC 3.0 — 7 minutes.**\
Introduce the open specification, its design scope, active form factors, supported applications, and multi-vendor ecosystem. Establish the relationship to earlier OCP NIC designs.

**Module 2: Form Factors & Mechanical Design — 8 minutes.**\
Compare card dimensions, connector mating, faceplates, retention, and assembly requirements. Examine how single- and double-width cards relate to baseboard slot layouts.

**Module 3: Electrical Interface — 10 minutes.**\
Map connector signals and card/baseboard ownership. Trace discovery, lane bifurcation, mandatory power states, sequencing, and the division of hot-swap responsibilities.

**Module 4: Management & Firmware — 9 minutes.**\
Review management interfaces, NC-SI traffic, MCTP transport, FRU EEPROM records, temperature and power reporting, and firmware-update requirements.

**Module 5: Signal Integrity, Thermal & Environmental — 9 minutes.**\
Connect shared PCIe channel budgets and compliance fixtures to thermal, airflow, cooling-tier, shock, and vibration requirements. Review evidence at the card and platform boundaries.

**Module 6: Compliance, Ecosystem & Future — 7 minutes.**\
Organize regulatory and safety compliance, identify implementation changes in specification v1.6.0, and use current subproject, specification, and marketplace resources for follow-on work.
