Begin with the workload and the problem you want to improve. Record the site constraints and what success would look like. Next, discover candidate designs, products, and partners, then qualify the interfaces and responsibilities. Plan a bounded pilot with measurements and a way to stop or recover if requirements are not met. During the pilot, compare results with the agreed criteria. Use that evidence to expand, revise, or stop. Share useful findings with the community where appropriate. Starting small is valuable because it makes learning manageable, not because a small installation needs no engineering review.
