# pronunciation: OCP = O C P; AI = A I; ORv3 = Open Rack version three; CDU = C D U
O C P Accepted identifies product recognition tied to relevant approved O C P contributions and program requirements. It does not guarantee a particular deployment’s efficiency or that every recognized product works with every other one. O C P Ready is a facility program using self-certification against its requirements; O C P’s current guidance says it does not conduct an on-site audit. Read the scope and supporting information, then ask what your workload and configuration still need to establish. Product recognition, facility information, and your deployment acceptance serve different purposes.
