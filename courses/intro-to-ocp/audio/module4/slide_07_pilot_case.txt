Suppose an operations team wants to reduce service effort while accommodating a new workload. A useful pilot might test one supported configuration under agreed conditions. The team records service steps, workload performance, power and thermal behavior, and support issues. Before starting, it decides who owns each interface and what results would justify expansion. If the evidence misses the criteria, revise the design or stop. That result can still be valuable if it prevents a larger unsuitable deployment.
