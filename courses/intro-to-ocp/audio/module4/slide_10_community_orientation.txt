Alongside the technical courses, O C P Community Orientation can help you decide how to take part. How to Build Your Brand in the O C P Community explains how useful participation earns trust, and how events and communications can extend the reach of that work. The O C P Contribution Process follows a submission through review, approval and publication.

The O C P Ready Data Center Recognition Program focuses on facility requirements, assessment and recognition. Introduction to the O C P Solution Provider Program explains the Marketplace, recognition programs, agreements and the path for bringing solutions to market. O C P's Volunteer Leadership Guide covers project roles, onboarding, collaboration tools and contribution workflows. Choose the course that fits your next responsibility. These are optional follow-on courses, not extra requirements for completing this introduction.
