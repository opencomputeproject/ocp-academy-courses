Suppose your team has learned how to improve a cooling interface. First, discuss the problem with the relevant project and submit the required proposal information. Agree the contribution type, scope, and licensing route. Next, develop the draft using the applicable guidance and invite community review. Review can send you back to revise the proposal; that is useful work, not a failed process. Follow the required project and Steering Committee approvals, including any applicable notice period. Publication makes the accepted contribution available, and later experience can lead to a new revision. A vendor’s product-recognition application is a separate path, not an automatic result of publishing a design.
