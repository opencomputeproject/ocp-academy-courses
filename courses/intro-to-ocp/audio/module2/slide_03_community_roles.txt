The Foundation provides the organizational framework, programs, and support for the community. Its Board provides governance. The Steering Committee supports technical direction and contribution review. Project leads organize work within their domains, while contributors bring proposals, analysis, testing, documentation, and feedback. These roles connect rather than form a simple production line. A useful proposal often crosses project boundaries, so start with the relevant project leads and follow the current process.
