# pronunciation: OCP = O C P; AI = A I; ORv3 = Open Rack version three; CDU = C D U
A contribution is shared work published through O C P’s process. It might be a specification, design, guideline, or another supported document type. A product is a particular implementation supplied and supported by an organization. Product recognition relates that product to the relevant O C P contribution and program requirements. Neither publication nor recognition establishes that your complete installation is ready. Keep the documents, the product revision, and the deployment evidence connected, but do not treat them as the same approval.
