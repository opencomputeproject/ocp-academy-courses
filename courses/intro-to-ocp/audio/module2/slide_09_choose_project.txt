Your team finds that a cooling connector is awkward to service in a populated rack. Start with the cooling community, and involve Rack and Power where the mechanical arrangement matters. Bring the interface description, service conditions, and what you observed. A vague request to improve cooling is hard to act on; a bounded problem with evidence gives the community something to review. If the work also affects monitoring, involve the relevant management expertise.
