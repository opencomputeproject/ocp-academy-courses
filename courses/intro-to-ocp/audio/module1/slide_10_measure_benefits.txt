Open designs can support better choices, but outcomes depend on the implementation. A claim about energy use, cost, deployment speed, or reuse should identify what was measured and under what conditions. Circularity means keeping products, components, and materials useful for longer through approaches such as repair and reuse. A modular design may help, but a real circularity plan also needs spare parts, support, and a viable next use. Treat broad benefit claims as starting questions, not universal guarantees.
