An architect can compare documented interfaces before selecting a platform. A developer can reuse a contributed design instead of starting every detail from scratch. An operations team can share service experience that makes the next design easier to maintain. Open hardware makes useful design information available under stated terms. Interoperability is a separate question: whether the selected parts work together as required. Shared interfaces help, but a common label alone does not establish compatibility, supplier support, or a safe installation.
