# pronunciation: OCP = O C P; AI = A I; ORv3 = Open Rack version three; CDU = C D U
The Open Compute Project, or O C P, is a community that shares designs and knowledge to improve computing infrastructure. It brings people who use technology together with people who build it, so they can solve common problems. In this course, you’ll discover how that collaboration works, recognize a few important technologies, and choose a practical first step for your own work.
