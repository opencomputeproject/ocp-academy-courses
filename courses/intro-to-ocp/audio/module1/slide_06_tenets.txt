# pronunciation: OCP = O C P; AI = A I; ORv3 = Open Rack version three; CDU = C D U
O C P describes its work through five tenets: efficiency, impact, openness, scale, and sustainability. Think of them as questions to ask about a proposal. Does it use resources well? Does it address a meaningful shared need? Can others understand and use the contribution under its terms? Can the approach be deployed at a useful scale? And how does it affect environmental outcomes over its life? These ideas guide the community, while the current contribution process defines the specific submission requirements.
