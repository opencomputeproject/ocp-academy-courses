# pronunciation: OCP = O C P; AI = A I; ORv3 = Open Rack version three; CDU = C D U
Begin with computing equipment: servers, storage, and network devices perform useful work. The rack supports and organizes that equipment and its interfaces. Power travels from the facility through the selected conversion and distribution systems to the equipment. A busbar is a conductor used to distribute electrical power; in an Open Rack implementation it forms part of that path. Most electrical energy used by the equipment becomes heat that must leave through the intended cooling system. Some systems use air, some bring liquid close to selected components, and some use both. Management information helps operators observe the system, but measurement does not replace the physical capacity of power and cooling.
