For the same delivered electrical power, increasing voltage reduces current. In an ideal comparison, moving from twelve volts to forty-eight volts makes the current one quarter as large. Resistive loss follows current squared times resistance, so with the same conductor resistance, that particular loss becomes one sixteenth as large. This is not a claim that a complete rack becomes sixteen times more efficient. Real systems include conversion losses, connections, controls, operating limits, and many other design choices. The relationship explains a motivation, not a guaranteed system result.
