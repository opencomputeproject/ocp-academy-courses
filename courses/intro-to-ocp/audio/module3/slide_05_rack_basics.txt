# pronunciation: OCP = O C P; AI = A I; ORv3 = Open Rack version three; CDU = C D U
Open Rack describes shared approaches to arranging equipment and its supporting interfaces. Earlier versions established important mechanical and rack-level power ideas. Open Rack version three develops those interfaces for newer needs. High-power work addresses electrical capacity, while Open Rack Wide addresses the physical envelope. The names are not interchangeable ratings. A wider frame does not by itself provide more usable electrical power, and a power upgrade does not prove cooling readiness. Use the relevant specification and implementation documents to understand what a particular assembly supports.
