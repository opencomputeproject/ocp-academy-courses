There is no single rack-power number that determines the right cooling technology for every deployment. Start with the equipment’s requirements and the site’s available conditions. Then consider reliability, maintenance skills, water and energy use, material compatibility, and the full life cycle. A cooling method that works well in one configuration can be unsuitable in another. Compare evidence for the same workload and operating conditions, and identify who is responsible for each interface.
