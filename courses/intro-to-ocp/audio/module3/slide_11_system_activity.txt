A team proposes new liquid-cooled servers because the rack footprint fits. Before treating that as a deployment plan, ask what electrical supply the equipment needs, what cooling conditions must be delivered, and how the remaining heat is removed. Ask how technicians will install and service it, and how alarms and operating limits are monitored. The useful habit is to connect the interfaces. A fit check is one input, not the whole decision.
