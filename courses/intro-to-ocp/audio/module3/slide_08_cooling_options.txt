# pronunciation: OCP = O C P; AI = A I; ORv3 = Open Rack version three; CDU = C D U
Air cooling carries heat away in airflow to the room or facility cooling system. A rear-door heat exchanger transfers heat from the exhaust air into a liquid loop. Direct-to-chip cooling brings coolant through cold plates attached to selected components; other components may still need air. A coolant distribution unit, or C D U, manages a cooling-loop interface, depending on the design. Immersion places compatible equipment in a dielectric fluid designed not to conduct electricity under its specified conditions. These approaches are alternatives and combinations, not a mandatory ladder of progress. Each needs suitable equipment, materials, service procedures, and a complete path for rejecting heat.
