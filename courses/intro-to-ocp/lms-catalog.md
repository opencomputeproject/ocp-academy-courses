**Title**\
Introduction to the Open Compute Project

**Short Description**\
Discover the OCP Community, explore open infrastructure, and plan your next step in adoption or participation.

**Long Description**

Discover how the Open Compute Project (OCP) brings people and organizations together to develop and share open infrastructure. This introductory course explores OCP's origins, Community, governance and contribution process, then connects Open Rack and cooling concepts to practical adoption decisions. Expert videos, narrated explanations, animations and knowledge checks help you find relevant resources and choose a useful next step. No engineering prerequisites are required.

**Overall duration: 45 minutes**

**Module 1: The OCP Origins — 12 minutes.**\
Explore the practical problems that inspired OCP, its five tenets, and the value of open collaboration. Consider how shared designs can support different roles and why evidence matters when evaluating their benefits.

**Module 2: The OCP Ecosystem & Governance — 10 minutes.**\
Understand the roles of the OCP Foundation, governance bodies, project leaders and contributors. Follow the contribution path from a shared problem to review and publication, and identify appropriate ways to participate in the OCP Community.

**Module 3: OCP Technologies — Open Rack & Cooling — 14 minutes.**\
Connect rack design, power distribution, heat removal and facility requirements. Explore Open Rack and cooling approaches, recognize important interfaces and constraints, and find specialist Academy courses for deeper study.

**Module 4: Today and Tomorrow — The Journey — 9 minutes.**\
Use OCP recognition programs, the Marketplace and contribution resources within their stated scope. Define a bounded pilot with clear responsibilities and decision criteria, then choose technical or OCP Community Orientation courses that match your next step.
