Welcome to Thermal Boundaries in A I Infrastructure. Cooling now helps determine which compute can be deployed, where it can run, and what the deployment will cost. This course gives you a framework for making that decision. We begin by separating three different limits: efficiency, economics, and physics. Then we'll examine why a better cooling method can remain unadopted, and what the industry can prepare before a transition becomes unavoidable.
