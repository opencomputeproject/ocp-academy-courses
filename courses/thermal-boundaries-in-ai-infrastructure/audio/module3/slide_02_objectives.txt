By the end of this module, you'll be able to test a cooling proposal beyond its ability to hold temperature.

Begin with full ownership cost. You'll identify the capital and operating consequences that land in different budgets and on different lifecycles. A quoted rack-side price can be only a small part of that comparison.

Next, separate component reliability from system availability. Fewer individual failures can coexist with a shared dependency that affects many devices at once. The architecture and the failure domain determine what the evidence means.

You'll also distinguish a model assumption, a modeled result, and a reported observation. That lets you use the paper's comparisons without treating them as guaranteed results for your facility.

Finally, you'll evaluate readiness across owners and suppliers: compatible interfaces, visible controls, realistic load and fault tests, and agreed records witnessed before production acceptance.
