By the end of this module, you'll be able to separate an example configuration from a mandatory interface condition. You'll connect a source page, an applicable requirement and an observed fact in one review record.

You'll also distinguish a demonstrated mismatch from a missing measurement or conflicting source statement. Keep the scope of each disposition narrow. A sound finding about a connector or keep out area doesn't establish that the complete system is compliant, thermally suitable, secure or ready for deployment.
