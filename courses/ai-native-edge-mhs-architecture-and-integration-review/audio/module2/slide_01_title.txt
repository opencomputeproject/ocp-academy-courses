Module two follows the interfaces that make a reusable board useful in a real system. You'll start at the datum, inspect the mounting and height restrictions, and connect the selected peripheral interfaces to their electrical and power requirements.

The aim is a repeatable review sequence. You won't memorize every dimension. You'll learn where a condition applies, where its evidence belongs, and when a source conflict needs clarification before you can close a finding.
