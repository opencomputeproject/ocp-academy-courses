**Title**\
AI Native Edge-MHS: Architecture and Integration Review

**Short Description**\
Review Class N/L form factors, mechanical interfaces and integration evidence using the AI Native Edge-MHS specification.

**Long Description**

Build a practical method for reviewing AI Native Edge-MHS Host Processor Modules. Compare Class N and Class L, examine critical mechanical and power interfaces, and apply source-linked requirements to two guided integration cases. The course distinguishes requirements, recommendations, examples and excluded scope, while identifying the evidence that companion specifications and complete system designs must supply.

**Overall duration: 55 minutes**

**Module 1: Architecture and Class N/L — 16 minutes.**\
Compare Class N/L form factors and depth choices, follow fixed and floating references, and distinguish architecture intent from complete system interoperability.

**Module 2: Mechanical interfaces and compatibility — 20 minutes.**\
Follow a datum-led interface review through mounting, height restrictions, peripheral placement, HSIO and power. Preserve conditional requirements and identify source conflicts.

**Module 3: Integration review exercises — 18 minutes.**\
Review two constructed integration cases and connect requirements, observations, dispositions and next actions. Separate demonstrated mismatches, evidence gaps and conflicting source text.
