**Title**\
Open Cluster Designs for AI

**Short Description**\
Read OCP cluster designs, compare AI fabric choices and assemble the evidence for deployment.

**Long Description**

This four-module course applies OCP Open Cluster Designs for AI contributions to practical design review. Learners trace pod groups into complete clusters, compare training and inference fabric choices, and connect the selected configuration to physical, validation and operating evidence.

**Overall duration: 55 minutes**

**Module 1: Read the Open Cluster Architecture — 12 minutes.**\
Locate the design hierarchy, network roles and the scope of each published contribution.

**Module 2: Trace a Building Block into a Cluster — 15 minutes.**\
Reconcile equipment and connection records, compose pod groups and review shared capacity.

**Module 3: Match the Fabric to the Workload — 15 minutes.**\
Compare wiring, network-interface and plane choices with training and inference requirements.

**Module 4: Turn a Reference Design into Deployment Evidence — 13 minutes.**\
Assemble physical and operating evidence, interpret validation results and make a readiness decision.
