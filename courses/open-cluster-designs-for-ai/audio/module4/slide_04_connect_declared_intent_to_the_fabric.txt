Connect the intended design to the operating fabric. The April references use Hedgehog as a Kubernetes-native controller and sonic on the switches. This is a contributed implementation example. The wiring definitions describe switches, servers, and connections. Separate tenant definitions describe network membership and permitted external connectivity. Kubernetes custom resource definitions, or C R Ds, make that intent reviewable and versioned. The controller reconciles the intended state through agents on the switches. Observed state and telemetry then help operators check what is actually running. Preserve an independent out-of-band management path. The implementation deliberately avoids making that recovery network depend on the controller-managed network it may need to repair. Reusable logical intent still depends on supported hardware and verified platform behavior.
