Begin the physical review with the actual site and selected equipment. Confirm the power and cooling envelope, rack loads and dimensions, cable routes, connector compatibility, measured reach, and service access. Check the supporting equipment as well as the compute. A logical design that remains valid at a different density can still need a different physical installation. The January power and cooling estimates belong to its stated example; they are not universal facility limits or a substitute for equipment ratings and qualified engineering. Use the Open Data Center for "A I" course for a deeper facility discussion. Here, your task is to identify the interface, the evidence required, and the team responsible for accepting it.
