Choose a test that can establish the claim. A document review can identify missing requirements. Schema and connectivity checks can establish logical consistency. A virtual lab can help teams practice configuration, reconciliation, and failure procedures. It does not establish physical cable behavior, full-scale throughput, or production latency. Those claims need representative equipment, workloads, load conditions, and failure tests. Separate what you measure from the target you accept. A service-level indicator, or S L I, is a measurement such as a latency percentile or error rate. A service-level objective, or S L O, is the target chosen for that measurement. The reference provides observability guidance; it does not prescribe one universal target. Similarly, enabling a switch's R D M A traffic policy does not validate the host-to-host result. Record the configuration, conditions, result, owner, and remaining limitation.
