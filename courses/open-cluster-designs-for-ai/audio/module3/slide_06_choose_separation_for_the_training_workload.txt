# pronunciation: AI = quoted A I; xPU = ex pee you; OPG = O P G; XOC = X O C; SONiC = sonic; RoCE = rocky; CRD = C R D; RDMA = R D M A

The training reference offers two separation profiles. Minimal Training converges CPU-facing front-end and storage traffic. Full Training separates those functions. Both retain the scale-out back-end used for communication between compute domains, as well as management paths. These names belong to this training reference. Minimal Training does not mean that the scale-out network disappears. Distributed training communication can use remote direct memory access, or R D M A. R D M A over Converged Ethernet is commonly called rocky. In the contributed implementation, switch policy combines traffic classification, congestion signaling, and priority flow control for the designated traffic class. The hosts and network interfaces must be configured consistently too. Profile selection should follow the workload, storage behavior, isolation needs, and operating team, rather than a preference for the fewest network labels.
