Read the bill of materials alongside the rack layout and connection maps. The list tells you what to procure. The layout tells you where it goes. The map tells you how identified endpoints connect. A configuration identifier and source revision should join those records. If a quantity in a summary table disagrees with a detailed topology or map, stop treating the list as a ready-to-order kit. Reconcile the discrepancy for the chosen configuration. Use reconciled entries for the selected configuration, rather than combining quantities from different variants. The transferable skill is to compare records, resolve differences, and preserve the result. An apparently complete document is a starting point for that work.
