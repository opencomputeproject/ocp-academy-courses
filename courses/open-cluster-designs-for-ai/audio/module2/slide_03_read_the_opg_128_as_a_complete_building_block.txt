The January O P G one hundred twenty-eight example contains sixteen servers with eight accelerators each. Sixteen times eight gives one hundred twenty-eight accelerators. The published physical example uses nine racks, with compute, networking, storage, management, and supporting equipment distributed across the group. Its assumptions include air cooling and four-hundred-gigabit scale-out links. Those are properties of this example, not universal limits on an Open Pod Group. Notice what the accelerator count leaves out: the storage and metadata services, network switches, power support, cable management, and the paths used to operate and recover the equipment. The complete building block includes all those responsibilities. A new supplier choice or a different rack density still needs to satisfy the interfaces and site requirements.
