Start with a workload description, not a shopping list. A training service, an independent model-serving service, and a model distributed across several hosts can place very different demands on the same accelerator count. Record the form factor, the communication pattern, the storage demands, the tenant boundaries, and the performance goals. Include the operating team and the facility in that conversation. A published design becomes useful when those assumptions are visible. A different assumption does not make the reference useless. It tells you which part of the design needs another check. The Open Cluster Designs initiative develops practical building blocks and complete cluster architectures to reduce repeated integration work. It works alongside Open Data Centers for "A I" within the wider Open Systems for "A I" effort. The preceding course provides that broader system context.
