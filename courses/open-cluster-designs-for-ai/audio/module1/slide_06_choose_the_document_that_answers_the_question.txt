Use the document set as a map. The Open Cluster Designs white paper explains the purpose, hierarchy, and design objectives. The January O P G M architecture describes the pod-group hardware example. Its companion X O C N architecture adds the shared networks and head-end resources for a complete cluster. The April training and inference fabric references provide network-centric implementation guidance, including topology, declarative management, traffic policy, and operating workflows. They use Hedgehog and sonic as a contributed implementation. That does not make those products a requirement for every open cluster.
