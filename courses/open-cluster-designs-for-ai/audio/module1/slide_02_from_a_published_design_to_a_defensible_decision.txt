The course has four modules and an estimated learner time of fifty-five minutes. First, locate the architecture and its assumptions. Next, trace equipment and connections from a pod group into a cluster. Then compare fabric choices against the workload. Finally, assemble the evidence for an installation and operating handoff. Short activities and two questions in each module help you apply the method. The linked specifications and Summit recordings are optional further study. Their full running times are not part of the course estimate.
