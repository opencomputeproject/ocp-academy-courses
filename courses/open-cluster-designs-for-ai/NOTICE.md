# Source attribution and notices

The course adapts concepts and redraws instructional diagrams from the OCP Community's 2026 Open Cluster Designs for AI contributions. Source references and exact revisions are recorded in `course.json` and `authoring/source-manifest.json`. The course adds original explanations, scenarios, assessments, figures, narration and finite teaching animations; it does not certify a deployment or reproduce a complete procurement kit.

- January white paper: Lowell D. Lamb (Broadcom) and Loren Staley (Celestica).
- January OPG-M and XOC-N architecture contributions: Lowell D. Lamb, Loren Staley, Joshua Mora (System-Stack) and Ashok Raman (Broadcom).
- April training and inference fabric reference architectures: Art Fewell (Hedgehog) and Arun Solleti (Celestica), with the contributors acknowledged in those documents.
- 2026 Summit current-activities presentation: Lowell Lamb and Loren Staley. Only its published direction and illustrative maturity are summarized; its slides/video are linked, not bundled.

The original contributions and their attribution/notice requirements remain applicable; see [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The repository's MIT software license does not replace third-party source licenses or OCP brand/trademark requirements. OCP Academy branding is provided by the course owner. Open Sans retains its bundled OFL notice in `assets/fonts/opensans-OFL.txt`.
