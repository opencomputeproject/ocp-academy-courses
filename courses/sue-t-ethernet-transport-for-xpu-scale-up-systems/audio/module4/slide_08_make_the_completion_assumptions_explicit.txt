An implementation's completion contract connects reported success to a specific event. The four write-completion alternatives cover different portions of the path, from destination memory back to the initiator's local queue.

Earlier completion can reduce what the response establishes about memory visibility and error coverage. A shared A X I identifier does not fill in missing read/write cross-ordering. An encoding choice does not choose the completion event.

The initiator still needs the response or acknowledgement result that cleans up the outstanding request. Treat that obligation separately from any early application-facing success.

Finally, choose the operation set, buffers, retransmission, acknowledgement mode, and connection allocation against the workload's actual requirements. The specification's examples help frame those choices; the implementation needs to make its own contract clear.
