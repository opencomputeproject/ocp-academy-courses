A successful write response can confirm different events depending on the implementation's completion contract. This module makes that choice explicit.

You'll compare completion at memory with earlier queue-acceptance points, then apply the distinction to an aligned write and a following read. We'll also connect the response to error reporting and resource cleanup. The goal is to explain what the application can conclude when success is reported.
