# pronunciation: SUE-T -> "S U E T"; XPU -> "X P U"; ACK -> "ack"; NACK -> "knack"; PDC -> "P D C"; PSN -> "P S N"; AXI -> "A X I"; AxID -> "A X I identifier"; AxUSER -> "A X user"; WSTRB -> "write strobe"; UET -> "U E T"; RODL -> "R O D L"
Scale-Up Ethernet Transport, or S U E T, turns accelerator transactions into packets while balancing latency, wire efficiency, and endpoint state. This module follows that packet path from an eligible transmit queue to ordered delivery at the receiver.

You'll predict when the scheduler should transmit, trace the effect of a missing packet, and compare transport retransmission with the required lossless network service. These distinctions help you evaluate the buffering and resilience choices in an implementation.
