They cannot. R O D L is an ordered service. The receiver expects one hundred one and sends a knack when one hundred two arrives unexpectedly. Subsequent arrivals are dropped until the expected packet arrives. Receiving bytes on the link is different from accepting their commands for delivery.

Now remove transport retransmission from the design. That saves endpoint state and buffering. It also changes the failure consequence: the lost packet is fatal to the connection. The implementation cannot simply deliver later packets out of order to bypass the gap under this protocol.
