# pronunciation: SUE-T -> "S U E T"; XPU -> "X P U"; ACK -> "ack"
This course follows Scale-Up Ethernet Transport, or S U E T, through four connected modules. You'll trace a memory transaction, explain packing and ordered recovery, and evaluate the accelerator interface's completion contract.

The first module establishes the architecture and acknowledgement model. The second follows eligible queues into packets and examines the response to a sequence gap. The third maps interface identity, ordering, and request encoding. The fourth compares write-completion choices and applies them to a concrete integration case.

Allow about seventy-five minutes for the narration, worked cases, knowledge checks, and review. The modules have different time budgets because packet recovery and completion decisions need more practice than the initial architecture model.

Begin with one question: what has happened at the destination when the source receives an acknowledgement? We'll build on that distinction throughout the course.
