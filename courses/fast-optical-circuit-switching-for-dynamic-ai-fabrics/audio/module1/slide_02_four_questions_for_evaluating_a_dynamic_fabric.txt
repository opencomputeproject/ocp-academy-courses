# pronunciation: OCP -> O C P
This course answers four questions. When does faster optical switching matter? What must happen before a new path can carry useful traffic? How do the switching mechanisms differ? And what evidence would show that an AI workload benefits?

We'll move from workload timing to system behavior, then technologies and applications. You need a working knowledge of data center networks, but not a background in photonic device physics. The course is based on the O C P fast-switching white paper and the work of the Optical Circuit Switching subproject.
