The paper groups fast switching into seven technology families: two free-space approaches and five integrated-photonics categories. This is a map of mechanisms, not a ranking of products or a statement that every entry is commercially available.

In free space, a rotor moves a beam through a fixed sequence of connections, while a metasurface controls a phase profile to steer light. Integrated photonics guides light on a chip. Its categories include thermo-optic, mechanical, electro-optic, phase-change, and semiconductor optical amplifier approaches.

Integrated photonics is broader than silicon photonics. Different material platforms can support different physical effects. The platform name alone therefore does not tell you the actuation mechanism, its timing, or the behavior of the complete switch.

Read the timing table with that distinction in mind. A material response, an electrical modulation bandwidth, a switching transition, and an end-to-end reconfiguration are different measurements. A frequency in gigahertz cannot simply be relabelled as a circuit setup time. The source table surveys reported capabilities with different boundaries.

For this course, focus on what changes physically and what must stabilize afterward. That gives you a basis for asking the next engineering questions without treating a small timing number as proof of scale, reliability, or readiness for a particular workload.
