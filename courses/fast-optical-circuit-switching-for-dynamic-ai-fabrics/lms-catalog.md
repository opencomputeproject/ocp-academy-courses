**Title**\
Fast Optical Circuit Switching for Dynamic AI Fabrics

**Short Description**\
Evaluate fast optical switching through AI workload timing, complete reconfiguration latency, and deployment evidence.

**Long Description**

Evaluate when fast optical circuit switching can improve dynamic AI fabrics. Connect workload communication windows to complete path-reconfiguration time, compare switching mechanisms and deployment constraints, and assess application-level evidence. Worked examples and knowledge checks help learners distinguish device speed, usable-path readiness, and demonstrated system benefits when reviewing a proposed architecture.

**Overall duration: 60 minutes**

**Module 1: When Fast Optical Switching Matters — 12 minutes.**\
Match workload timing and predictability to dynamic connectivity opportunities. Distinguish device switching speed from usable-path readiness and examine the roles of hybrid packet and optical fabrics.

**Module 2: Making the Whole Reconfiguration Path Fast — 17 minutes.**\
Trace scheduling, control, switching, receiver recovery, and endpoint readiness along the critical path. Evaluate overlap, precomputation, variability, and measurement boundaries.

**Module 3: Switching Technologies and Deployment Constraints — 14 minutes.**\
Compare switching mechanism families using speed, port count, optical loss, endurance, and integration constraints. Distinguish scheduled rotor access from on-demand circuit behavior.

**Module 4: AI Workloads, Evidence, and Evaluation — 17 minutes.**\
Connect traffic patterns to reconfiguration opportunities and compare production, experimental, and simulated evidence. Define a fair evaluation of readiness, availability, and useful workload outcomes.
