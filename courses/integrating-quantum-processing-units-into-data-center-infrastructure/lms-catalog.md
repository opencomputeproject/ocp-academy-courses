**Title**\
Integrating Quantum Processing Units into Data Center Infrastructure

**Short Description**\
Connect hybrid quantum-classical systems to QPU facility needs, deployment boundaries, and operational readiness.

**Long Description**

Explore what it takes to integrate quantum processing units into data center infrastructure. Connect the logical QPU and its classical support stack to facility readiness, environmental controls, service boundaries, and operational practices. Compare modality-dependent needs and deployment models, then use a practical planning framework to structure conversations among facility operators, integrators, and quantum-system suppliers.

**Overall duration: 45 minutes**

**Module 1: Why Quantum Belongs in the Data Center — 10 minutes.**\
Frame QPUs as part of hybrid quantum-classical infrastructure. Identify why modalities create different power, cooling, control, space, and environmental requirements.

**Module 2: The Logical QPU and Its Classical Stack — 12 minutes.**\
Trace the logical QPU reference stack and its classical compute, control, storage, and networking dependencies. Compare standalone, co-located, and on-node deployment models.

**Module 3: Facility Readiness by Modality — 13 minutes.**\
Assess site-selection factors, service demarcations, environmental disturbances, utilities, maintenance, and consumables. Compare facility requirements across quantum modalities and identify coordination risks.

**Module 4: From Pilot Deployment to OCP Practice — 10 minutes.**\
Move from pilot installations to repeatable operating models. Connect shared infrastructure and existing OCP domains to responsibilities, reliability expectations, and deployment-readiness checks.
