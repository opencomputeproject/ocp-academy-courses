Module three. Three ways to compute across distance.

The IOWN Global Forum proposes three Across models that help explain different reasons to connect distributed infrastructure. Scale-Across expands capacity across regions. Sync-Across keeps data or state aligned across sites. Reach-Across gives users and applications real-time access to remote resources.

These models inform A I C C, but they are not finished O C P product specifications. They are a framework for asking better architecture questions.

In this module, you'll classify each model by its workload need, its communications behavior, and the service objectives that determine whether the design is practical.
