# pronunciation: AI -> "A I"
Choose the Across model by the primary workload need.

Scale-Across is about capacity across regions. It asks where compute and data should run when power, cooling, utilization, or demand varies by location.

Sync-Across is about state consistency. It asks what must be replicated, how quickly, and how the service behaves during delay or disconnection.

Reach-Across is about access. It asks whether a remote A I resource can satisfy a user or application in real time under the required security and governance.

All three require measurable objectives. Interactive inference may emphasize time to first token, token latency, and tail behavior. Batch work may emphasize throughput, energy, and cost. Stateful or physical systems may put availability, correctness, and recovery first.

The models can also overlap. A service may reach remote capacity, synchronize critical state, and redirect demand across regions. Keeping the three purposes distinct makes that combined design easier to reason about.
