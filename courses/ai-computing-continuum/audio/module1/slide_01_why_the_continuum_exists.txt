# pronunciation: AICC -> "A I C C"; AI -> "A I"; OCP -> "O C P"
Module one. Why the continuum exists.

A I is moving beyond centralized hyperscale data centers. Inference services now need to operate near enterprises, networks, factories, offices, remote facilities, and end users. Those environments differ widely in scale, power, cooling, security, and connectivity.

The Open Compute Project's A I Computing Continuum, or A I C C, asks how open infrastructure can support that diversity without creating a unique system for every location.

By the end of this module, you'll understand the change driving A I C C, what hyperscale adjacent means, and why connectivity matters as much as compute scale.
