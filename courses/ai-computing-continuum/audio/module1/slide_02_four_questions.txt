# pronunciation: AICC -> "A I C C"; AI -> "A I"; OCP -> "O C P"; IOWN -> "eye-own"
This course follows four connected questions.

First, why is A I inference spreading across such a wide range of environments? We'll examine the demands that centralized infrastructure can't always meet on its own.

Second, what must be open and interoperable across the continuum? We'll connect servers, racks, facilities, cooling, power, communications, wireless access, and the changing internal structure of an inference service.

Third, why might compute work across distance? We'll distinguish three models proposed by the IOWN Global Forum: Scale-Across, Sync-Across, and Reach-Across.

Finally, how does the architecture become practical ecosystem work? We'll look at the complementary roles of O C P and the IOWN Global Forum, representative use cases, related O C P initiatives, and ways to participate.

The goal isn't to turn you into an implementer of every layer. It's to give you a reliable systems view of A I C C and the vocabulary to engage with the project.
