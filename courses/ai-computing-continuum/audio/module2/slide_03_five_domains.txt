The current A I C C scope organizes the work into five hardware domains.

A I Open Server Architectures covers A I optimized servers and sleds. This includes expansion for heterogeneous accelerators and fabric topologies, plus the mechanical, electrical, and thermal interfaces that make those designs practical.

A I Open Solution Architectures moves to the rack and system level. It includes air, closed-loop liquid, and hybrid cooling, along with power delivery, facility constraints, retrofit needs, and modular deployment models.

A I Building Block Architectures focuses on reusable physical pieces. Examples include enclosures, cooling modules, networking modules, and power conversion or distribution designed for inference loads.

Autonomous A I Wireless Access extends the continuum into radio environments. Current work includes an Autonomous Radio Unit, a Radio Abstraction Interface, and a Radio Unit Security Control Module.

Connectivity addresses the communications needed between systems and sites. The project names A I fabrics and Scale-Across communications as part of this domain.

These domains are deliberately connected. A new server form factor affects rack geometry, power delivery, cooling interfaces, serviceability, and network attachment. A wireless deployment adds its own timing, security, and environmental requirements.

The value of the structure is not separation for its own sake. It gives contributors clear surfaces where open specifications can meet and form complete solutions.
