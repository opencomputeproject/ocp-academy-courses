Module four. From architecture to ecosystem action.

A I C C is an evolving community project, not a finished product or a single end-to-end specification. Its value will come from turning broad infrastructure needs into contributions, reference designs, interfaces, validation, and deployable products.

This module connects the technical ideas to that ecosystem work. We'll distinguish the roles of O C P and the IOWN Global Forum, follow a representative use case, place A I C C alongside related O C P initiatives, and identify how contributors can engage.

The objective is a realistic picture of what exists today, what remains a target, and what the community can build next.
