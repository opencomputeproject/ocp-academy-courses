**Title**\
AI Computing Continuum: Extending AI Beyond the Hyperscale Data Center

**Short Description**\
Explore distributed AI inference, open infrastructure, and Scale-Across, Sync-Across, and Reach-Across architectures.

**Long Description**

Explore why AI inference is extending beyond centralized hyperscale data centers and how open infrastructure can support diverse deployment environments. Connect workload placement, specialized resources, networking, and operational constraints to three models for computing across distance, then examine the complementary roles of OCP and the IOWN Global Forum in building the AI Computing Continuum.

**Overall duration: 45 minutes**

**Module 1: Why the Continuum Exists — 10 minutes.**\
Explain why inference demand spans different environments, compare workload profiles, and connect placement decisions to capacity, connectivity, and service requirements.

**Module 2: Open Infrastructure Across the Continuum — 12 minutes.**\
Identify the five AICC hardware domains, compare site constraints, and trace how open interfaces connect specialized compute, memory, storage, fabrics, and management resources.

**Module 3: Three Ways to Compute Across Distance — 12 minutes.**\
Distinguish Scale-Across, Sync-Across, and Reach-Across by their capacity, state, and access needs. Evaluate each against workload service objectives and infrastructure constraints.

**Module 4: From Architecture to Ecosystem Action — 11 minutes.**\
Connect OCP and IOWN ecosystem roles to practical use cases, adjacent infrastructure projects, and public participation paths. Distinguish evolving targets from demonstrated capabilities.
