Module three: Programming H B F for A I Serving. The hardware provides sixteen parallel paths, but software decides whether a workload can use them in practice. We'll examine the H B F memory map, parallel read and structured write behavior, and the host's role in refresh, wear, errors, thermals, and security. Then we'll apply those rules to model weights, K V cache, single-model serving, multiple models, and Mixture-of-Experts workloads.
