The A I memory wall is a data-delivery problem as much as a compute problem. Larger models, longer contexts, and more simultaneous users increase the amount of weight and K V cache data that must stay close to an accelerator.

H B M supplies the fastest working-memory tier, but capacity is constrained. S S Ds supply durable capacity, but through a storage path. H B F adds a Nand-based, read-optimized tier between them. It sits near the X P U, exposes many parallel channels, and augments rather than replaces the other tiers.
