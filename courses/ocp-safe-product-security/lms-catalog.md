**Title**\
OCP S.A.F.E.: Product Security Reviews and S.O.L.I.D. Requirements

**Short Description**\
Understand S.A.F.E. reviews, S.O.L.I.D. requirements, and the role of AI-assisted analysis in product security.

**Long Description**

Learn how independent product security reviews support data center decisions. Explore review scopes, preparation, remediation, and reporting, then connect applicable S.O.L.I.D. requirements and AI-assisted analysis to the S.A.F.E. process.

**Overall duration: 35 minutes**

**Module 1: S.A.F.E.: Purpose, Scope, and Assurance — 16 minutes.**\
Explain what a S.A.F.E. review establishes and match review depth to the threat model.

**Module 2: Preparing, Completing, and Using a Review — 11 minutes.**\
Prepare a useful review brief and distinguish private findings, public evidence, and customer decisions.

**Module 3: S.O.L.I.D. Requirements and AI-Assisted Reviews — 8 minutes.**\
Explain how applicable S.O.L.I.D. requirements and AI-assisted analysis fit within an independent S.A.F.E. review.
