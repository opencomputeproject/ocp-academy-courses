Three evidence artifacts answer different questions. The scope document explains the assessment boundary: what was included, the areas examined, and the agreed context. It helps you decide whether the work matches the question you're asking.

The detailed report gives the vendor the technical analysis. It can describe methodology, limitations, findings, their impact, and recommended remediation. This material is generally private and may be protected by confidentiality agreements.

The short-form report, or S F R, is the signed machine-readable summary used for publication. It identifies the vendor, device, and reviewed firmware, including a firmware hash, and records the required review results. Use the maintained specification for its exact format and reporting rules.

These artifacts complement each other. A signature helps establish the integrity and origin of a report; it doesn't expand the review scope. To interpret the assurance, connect the identified product and firmware to the scope and the reported outcomes.
