Published Safe evidence is available through the O C P program's repository. Approved products can be promoted and found through the O C P Marketplace. The Marketplace link on this slide opens the O C P Safe listings. Use a listing to identify the product, then follow the published review evidence. Vendors can use the Solution Provider guidance to learn the listing requirements.

The current framework puts the publication submission with the Device Vendor. This gives the vendor control over the timing and messaging around disclosure. A vendor can delay publication or decline it, but then forgoes the associated Safe endorsement.

Publication isn't the end of product security work. When firmware or relevant implementation details change, agree the follow-up assessment needed for the changed state. A change-focused review can avoid repeating irrelevant work, but its effort depends on the changes and the review boundary.

For a customer, the practical habit stays the same: identify the deployed state, find the applicable evidence, and evaluate the findings and limitations for the intended use.
