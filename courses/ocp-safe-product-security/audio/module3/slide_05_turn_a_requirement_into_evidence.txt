Take the storage-drive sanitization requirement as a simple example. The published requirement refers to the governing sanitization guidance and the Safe storage sanitization requirements. Begin with that complete text, not just a marketing statement that the drive can erase data.

The vendor explains how the applicable behavior is implemented and provides evidence the provider can evaluate. Preparation might include design information, supported commands and states, test results, and the limits or conditions of the claimed behavior. These are illustrative evidence examples, rather than a complete prescribed test method.

The independent provider assesses the evidence and implementation within the agreed scope, and records the results and any gaps. Keeping the requirement, the vendor's claim, and the reviewed evidence connected makes it easier for a customer to understand what was established and what still needs attention.
