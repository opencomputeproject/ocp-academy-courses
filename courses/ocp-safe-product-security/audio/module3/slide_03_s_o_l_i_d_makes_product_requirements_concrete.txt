Solid stands for Securing Of Latest Infrastructure Devices. It defines baseline security requirements for products used in data centers and a structure for identifying which requirements apply to different product types.

Think of three connected parts. The product type and its characteristics determine the applicable requirements. The vendor explains how those requirements are met and prepares supporting evidence. The independent provider evaluates the product through the Safe process and records the results and gaps.

This isn't a second, unrelated audit process. The requirements make the review question more concrete: what should this kind of product provide, and what evidence supports that claim? Start with the applicability mapping, then use the full published requirements. The course examples show how to navigate that relationship; they aren't complete compliance checklists.
