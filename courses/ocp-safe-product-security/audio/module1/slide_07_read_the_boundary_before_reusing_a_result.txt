A review result has a boundary. The product identity, firmware version or hash, assessed components, and review scope tell you what the evidence is about. Limitations and findings tell you where interpretation needs care.

Before reusing a result, compare that boundary with what you plan to deploy. A product family name alone doesn't establish that a different firmware build or configuration was examined. A later update may need a follow-up assessment, with the changes and baseline agreed by the vendor and provider.

Also consider the deployment. A remaining issue may have different consequences in different environments, and a documented mitigation still needs to be appropriate and implemented. Safe supports an informed security decision. It doesn't guarantee that every version is vulnerability-free or that every deployment has the same risk.
