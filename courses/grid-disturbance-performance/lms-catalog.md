**Title**\
Grid Disturbance Performance for Hyperscale Data Centers

**Short Description**\
Interpret voltage ride-through, active-power recovery, and implementation commitments at the utility interface.

**Long Description**

Learn to interpret the OCP interface specification for disturbance performance between hyperscale data centers and the utility grid. This narrated course connects voltage ride-through requirements, active-power recovery, and facility coordination through technical diagrams, a teaching video in every module, practical examples, and knowledge checks. Designed for data center electrical engineers, utility planners, and technical project teams, it helps learners use the specification in equipment reviews and implementation discussions while recognizing its scope, qualifications, and proposed implementation approach.

**Overall duration: 64 minutes**

**Module 1: Define the Grid–Data Center Interface — 15 minutes.**\
Explore how a shared voltage disturbance can affect both data centers and the grid. Identify the point of interconnection, distinguish computational load from other facility loads, and separate ride-through obligations from power recovery.

**Module 2: Read and Apply the Voltage Ride-Through Envelope — 17 minutes.**\
Interpret voltage bands, duration limits, and permitted power behavior in Table 1. Compare shallow, moderate, and deep voltage sags, then use worked scenarios to classify disturbances and identify the applicable requirements.

**Module 3: Recover Active Power and Coordinate the Facility — 16 minutes.**\
Apply the active-power recovery trigger, target, and timing through an 80 MW worked example. Examine measurement and reclosing considerations, cooling recovery constraints, and the conditions that limit the recovery commitment.

**Module 4: Choose Mitigations and Plan Implementation — 16 minutes.**\
Compare PSU firmware changes, medium-voltage UPS options, and grid-support capabilities. Apply the proposed implementation timeline and identify the equipment evidence, utility coordination, and project decisions needed for an informed implementation plan.
