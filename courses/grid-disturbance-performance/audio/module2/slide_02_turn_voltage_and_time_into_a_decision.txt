# pronunciation: OCP -> O C P; POI -> point of interconnection; PFAPR -> post-fault active power recovery; p.u. -> per unit; PSU -> P S U; UPS -> U P S; VFD -> V F D; OEM -> O E M; SVC -> S V C; E-STATCOM -> E statcom.

By the end of this module, you'll be able to classify a disturbance using voltage magnitude and elapsed time. You'll distinguish constant-power operation, continued grid consumption with proportional reduction, and the deeper-sag bands that allow power to fall to any value.

You'll also distinguish an allowed action from a required action. The specification allows backup transfer outside the stated envelope. It does not say the facility must transfer the instant a minimum duration is exceeded. Keep that distinction in mind as the example event markers move toward and beyond their limits.
