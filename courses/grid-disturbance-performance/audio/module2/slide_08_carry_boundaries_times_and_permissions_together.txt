# pronunciation: OCP -> O C P; POI -> point of interconnection; PFAPR -> post-fault active power recovery; p.u. -> per unit; PSU -> P S U; UPS -> U P S; VFD -> V F D; OEM -> O E M; SVC -> S V C; E-STATCOM -> E statcom.

The envelope contains more than a set of trip thresholds. It specifies a behavior over time. Normal voltage requires continuous constant power. The high-voltage band through one point two zero per unit requires one second at constant power.

The two shallower low-voltage bands retain active-power consumption while permitting proportional reduction. The deeper bands permit any power value, including zero, subject to the applicable recovery commitment. Outside the envelope, backup transfer is allowed rather than compelled. Keep the exact boundaries beside every time and power statement when you explain a result.
