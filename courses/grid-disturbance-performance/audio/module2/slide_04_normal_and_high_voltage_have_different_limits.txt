# pronunciation: OCP -> O C P; POI -> point of interconnection; PFAPR -> post-fault active power recovery; p.u. -> per unit; PSU -> P S U; UPS -> U P S; VFD -> V F D; OEM -> O E M; SVC -> S V C; E-STATCOM -> E statcom.

Start with the normal band: zero point nine zero through one point one zero per unit, including both endpoints. The specification calls for continuous operation at constant power in that range. There is no short ride-through countdown for normal voltage.

Above one point one zero and up to one point two zero per unit, the minimum ride-through time is one second, with constant power maintained. Notice the endpoints. Exactly one point one zero remains in the normal band. Exactly one point two zero belongs to the high-voltage band that requires one second.

Above one point two zero per unit, instantaneous transfer to backup is permitted. The table lists the load behavior and recovery commitment as not applicable for that row. Permission to transfer is not a command to trip or a requirement to change supply.

Constant power is a statement about power behavior. It should not be casually replaced with constant current, because voltage and current together determine the electrical power. The course does not prescribe the converter control design needed to meet that behavior.

When evaluating a high-voltage event, state the voltage and duration together. For example, one point one five per unit falls in the one-second high-voltage band. The same voltage sustained beyond that required interval raises a different transfer-permission question. The specification's boundary and timing need to remain attached to the claim.
