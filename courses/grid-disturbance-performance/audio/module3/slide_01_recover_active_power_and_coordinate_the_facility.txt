Module three: Recover Active Power and Coordinate the Facility. A disturbance response isn't fully described by surviving the low-voltage interval. The grid also needs the computational demand to return in the specified way.

We'll identify the recovery baseline and timing trigger, follow an eighty-megawatt example, and then examine the measurement, reclosing, and cooling assumptions that a simple power trace cannot resolve on its own.
