By the end of this module, you'll be able to calculate the recovery target and identify when the two-second clock begins. You'll also recognize when an event falls outside the scope of that commitment.

Our example uses computational active power, not total site demand. Keep that distinction visible throughout the calculation. After the numerical example, we'll examine how voltage measurement, protection behavior, and cooling restoration affect the project discussion. The goal is a defensible interpretation with explicit assumptions, rather than a pass or fail judgment based on an unlabeled graph.
