This is a discussion checklist derived from the topics in the specification. It is not a complete commissioning sequence, an official test protocol, or a new certification standard. Those details require the appropriate project and industry processes.

Keep future work visible as a separate category. The document identifies frequency ride-through, rate of change of frequency, phase jump, and counter-based protection schemes as future disturbance-performance topics. It also lists active-power ramp rate, intra-minute variation, and forced oscillations.

The current version does not supply the quantitative thresholds needed to teach those topics as settled commitments. An exact-looking number added from another context could make the course less accurate, not more useful. If a project has additional requirements, bring their own authoritative documents into the review.

The outcome of this course is a disciplined conversation. You can state what the O C P table requires, what the equipment may need to do, what the implementation proposal says, and what still needs agreement. That clarity is more valuable than claiming that every open engineering question has already been resolved.
