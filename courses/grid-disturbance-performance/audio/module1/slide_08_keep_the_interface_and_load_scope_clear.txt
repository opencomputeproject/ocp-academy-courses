# pronunciation: OCP -> O C P; POI -> point of interconnection; PFAPR -> post-fault active power recovery; p.u. -> per unit; PSU -> P S U; UPS -> U P S; VFD -> V F D; OEM -> O E M; SVC -> S V C; E-STATCOM -> E statcom.

Four ideas provide the foundation for the rest of the course. Voltage is assessed at the point of interconnection. The stated commitment applies to computational load. Ride-through and recovery describe different parts of the event. And grid-facing power behavior matters even when computing remains available.

Use those ideas to challenge ambiguous claims. Ask which load is included, which voltage is measured, and which time interval is being discussed. Then connect the O C P baseline to the project's actual utility and equipment arrangements. In the next module, we'll replace broad statements with exact voltage bands and durations.
