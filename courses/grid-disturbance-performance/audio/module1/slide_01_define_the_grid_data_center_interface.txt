Welcome to Grid Disturbance Performance for Hyperscale Data Centers. This course examines the O C P interface specification, version one point zero point zero, effective September seventeenth, twenty twenty-six.

We begin at the boundary between a data center and its utility. You'll learn which load the commitment covers, why a brief electrical disturbance can matter to the wider grid, and why staying connected and recovering power are separate engineering questions.
