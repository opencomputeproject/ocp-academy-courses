The course follows the decisions you would make in a technical review. First, locate the measurement boundary and identify the load covered by the commitment. Next, classify the disturbance using both voltage and time. Then assess how the computational load recovers once voltage returns. Finally, compare implementation options and the proposed treatment of existing and new capacity.

Each module ends with two questions and feedback. The diagrams and animations use the specification's values, while worked examples are clearly identified as illustrations. By the end, you should be able to explain a response decision and name the assumptions that still need agreement with the utility or equipment supplier.
