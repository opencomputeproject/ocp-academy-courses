**Title**\
LVDC Power Distribution for Data Centers

**Short Description**\
Review LVDC architecture, rack interfaces, protection and verification using the OCP white paper v1.1.0.

**Long Description**

Review low-voltage DC power distribution from facility architecture to rack interfaces using the OCP LVDC white paper. Connect voltage compatibility, polarity and earthing, fault protection, conversion, storage, control, EMC, and stability to an integrated design review. Practical scenarios help learners distinguish stated requirements from assumptions and identify the evidence and responsibilities needed to assess a deployment.

**Overall duration: 180 minutes**

**Module 1: LVDC adoption and system architecture — 18 minutes.**\
Compare LVDC adoption stages, locate conversion and isolation, and trace source-to-load paths. Evaluate source and distribution redundancy alongside maintenance and cooling dependencies.

**Module 2: Voltage bands and equipment compatibility — 18 minutes.**\
Distinguish nominal, rated, and operating voltage. Review voltage bands, duration, power, and earth references to identify the evidence needed for source-to-load compatibility.

**Module 3: Polarity earthing and configuration choices — 18 minutes.**\
Trace load and fault-current paths through polarity and earthing choices. Compare TN-S and IT arrangements and use the configuration matrix to identify compatibility questions.

**Module 4: Fault behavior protection and personnel safety — 18 minutes.**\
Examine DC fault-current contributions, interruption, healthy-load tolerance, and recovery. Separate protective switching from verified isolation and assess the limits of generic safety claims.

**Module 5: Power conversion storage and distribution — 18 minutes.**\
Match conversion, storage, and distribution functions to their duties and timescales. Review utility-interface behavior, cooling readiness, and monitoring within the availability claim.

**Module 6: The LVDC rack interface — 18 minutes.**\
Compare LVDC rack interface approaches, assign conversion and protection ownership, and review precharge, inrush, hot-swap, transients, and local energy buffering.

**Module 7: Control sequences and operation — 18 minutes.**\
Align human procedures with automated startup, fault recovery, and maintenance states. Allocate control authority and define permissives, communication-loss behavior, and verification evidence.

**Module 8: EMC and system stability — 18 minutes.**\
Distinguish emissions, immunity, and dynamic stability. Trace noise and current paths through cables, filters, and earth references, then plan representative system-level verification.

**Module 9: Codes standards and verification — 18 minutes.**\
Separate system, product, installation, and utility requirements. Check document scope and status, local applicability, approval responsibilities, and the evidence supporting acceptance claims.

**Module 10: Integrated LVDC design review — 18 minutes.**\
Apply architecture, compatibility, protection, and operating-state reasoning to a deployment proposal. Identify unproven assumptions and assign evidence gaps to owners and acceptance criteria.
