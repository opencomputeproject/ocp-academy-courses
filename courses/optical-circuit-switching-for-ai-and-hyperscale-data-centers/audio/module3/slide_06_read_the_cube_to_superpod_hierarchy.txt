# pronunciation: OCS -> O C S; EPS -> E P S; OEO -> O E O; TPUv4 -> T P U version four; GPU -> G P U; GPUs -> G P Yous; ACTINA -> Actina; MoE -> M O E; OCS-BCube -> O C S B Cube; gNMI -> g N M I
A T P U is a tensor processing unit, an accelerator used in Google's machine-learning systems. In the T P U version four example, a Cube contains sixty-four T P Us arranged in a four-by-four-by-four electrical mesh. The connections inside that Cube remain electrical.

Sixty-four Cubes form a full Superpod of four thousand ninety-six T P Us. The example uses forty-eight MEMS optical switches, each with one hundred twenty-eight ports. Three groups of sixteen switches support the X, Y and Z dimensions. The optical layer connects between Cubes so the system can compose useful larger topologies. These numbers describe this particular architecture. The general lesson is the separation between a reusable electrical building block and configurable optical connections among those blocks.
