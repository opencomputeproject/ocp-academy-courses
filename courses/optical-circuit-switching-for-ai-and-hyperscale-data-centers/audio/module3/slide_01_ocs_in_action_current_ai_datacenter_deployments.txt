Module three connects optical switching to current AI data-center deployments. We'll examine three architectural patterns: replacing an electrical interconnect stage, composing a cluster from reusable compute units, and redirecting connectivity toward healthy or spare resources. Google's Jupiter and T P U version four examples provide concrete context. We'll preserve the conditions around their results and separate what the optical fabric changes from the functions that remain in electrical switches, controllers and workload software.
