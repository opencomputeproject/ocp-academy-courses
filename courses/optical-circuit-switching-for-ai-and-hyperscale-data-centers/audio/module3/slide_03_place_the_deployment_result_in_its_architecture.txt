# pronunciation: OCS -> O C S; EPS -> E P S; OEO -> O E O; TPUv4 -> T P U version four; GPU -> G P U; GPUs -> G P Yous; ACTINA -> Actina; MoE -> M O E; OCS-BCube -> O C S B Cube; gNMI -> g N M I
In the Jupiter evolution described by the OCP white paper, optical circuit switching helps replace a selected electrical interconnect stage. Electrical switching remains elsewhere in the network. The architecture allows connectivity between blocks to evolve and supports incremental upgrades without tying every stage to the same electronic switching generation.

The cited comparison reports a forty-one percent reduction in network power, a thirty percent reduction in capital expenditure, and five times the throughput. These are results associated with that deployment and its comparison conditions. They are not a guarantee from installing an O C S device in any network. Use the example to understand the mechanism, then evaluate your own topology, traffic, transceivers, redundancy and operating requirements.
