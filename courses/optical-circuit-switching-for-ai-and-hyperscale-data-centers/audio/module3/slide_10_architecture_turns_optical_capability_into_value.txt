The three deployment patterns share a principle: the optical switch provides selected connectivity that the larger system can use. In the interconnect case, it changes the role of an electrical stage. In the accelerator case, it composes Cubes into useful topologies. In the resilience case, it can reconnect healthy resources or spares.

Keep the evidence boundaries intact. Deployment statistics belong to their original comparison. A physical path does not guarantee an application's recovery. These qualifications make the examples more useful because they show what an engineering team must reproduce and verify.
