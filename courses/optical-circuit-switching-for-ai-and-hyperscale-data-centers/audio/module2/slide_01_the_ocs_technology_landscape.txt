Module two examines the O C S technology landscape. Optical circuit switching describes a function, but several different physical mechanisms can provide it. Some move fibers or mirrors. Others control polarization or guide light through a chip. We'll compare those mechanisms using the same questions: how quickly must connectivity change, how many ports are needed, and what optical and power constraints apply? These questions help you identify a good physical fit without treating any technology family as a universal winner.
