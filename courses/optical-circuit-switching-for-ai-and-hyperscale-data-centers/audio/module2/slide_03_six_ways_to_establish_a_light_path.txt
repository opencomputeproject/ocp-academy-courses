The six families organize the physical choices. Robotic mechanisms move and connect fibers. Micro-electro-mechanical systems, usually called MEMS, tilt small mirrors to steer light through free space. Liquid-crystal devices alter optical polarization under electrical control. Piezoelectric actuators deform a material to produce precise motion.

Silicon photonics routes light through small waveguides on a chip, using effects such as electro-optic or thermo-optic phase changes. Metasurfaces use tunable sub-wavelength structures to control the optical wavefront. Hybrid designs combine mechanisms to balance their strengths. This classification describes how light is routed. It does not by itself establish a complete system's scale, loss, speed, lifetime or operational maturity.
