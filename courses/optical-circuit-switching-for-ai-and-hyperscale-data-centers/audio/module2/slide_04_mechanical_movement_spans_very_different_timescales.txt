Robotic patching resembles an automated fiber patch panel. It can support large connectivity inventories, and a latched physical connection may hold without actuation power. Reconfiguration can take seconds to minutes. That can be useful for provisioning or infrequent changes, but it does not fit every workload.

MEMS mirrors move much smaller structures and commonly offer millisecond-scale reconfiguration with substantial port counts. Piezoelectric actuation uses voltage-driven deformation for precise positioning. Depending on the device and its settling requirements, implementations can span microsecond-to-millisecond behavior. The original material uses inconsistent timing descriptions for this family, so the responsible comparison is the measured device specification. Include settling, verification and system coordination when deciding whether a mechanism meets the required switching window.
