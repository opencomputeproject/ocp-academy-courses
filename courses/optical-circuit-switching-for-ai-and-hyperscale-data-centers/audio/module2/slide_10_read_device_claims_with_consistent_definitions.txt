A switching-time number may refer to an actuator transition, an optically settled path, or a complete controlled operation. Those are different boundaries. Similarly, a low holding-power claim may exclude the controller and transceivers. Ask what is measured and what remains outside the measurement.

For a comparison to guide a deployment, use the same definition across candidates. Record the wavelength conditions, optical loss, port count and acceptable error or settling threshold. Treat research prototypes, available components and production systems as different evidence levels. None of this diminishes a promising mechanism. It explains the additional work needed to turn a promising component into an operational fabric.
