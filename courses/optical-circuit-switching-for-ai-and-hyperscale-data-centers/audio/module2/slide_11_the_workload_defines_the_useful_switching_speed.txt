You now have a way to read the technology landscape. A robotic connector, a tilting mirror and an on-chip waveguide can all establish optical paths, but they do so under different constraints. Broad timing categories are useful orientation, not a substitute for a device specification.

The key design question is whether the complete change in connectivity fits the workload's available window while meeting the optical, port and operating requirements.
