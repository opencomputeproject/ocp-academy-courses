Welcome to Optical Circuit Switching for AI and Hyperscale Data Centers. We begin with the architectural idea behind an optical circuit: establishing a path for light between selected endpoints. Across five modules, you'll connect that idea to physical switch technologies, deployed networks, emerging applications, and the open ecosystem. This first module gives you the boundary conditions you'll need throughout the course. Keep one question in mind: which functions does the optical path provide, and which functions still belong elsewhere?
