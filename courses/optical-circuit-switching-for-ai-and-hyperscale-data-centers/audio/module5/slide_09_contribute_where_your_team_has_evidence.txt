Participation becomes most useful when it starts with a concrete problem. A supplier can clarify capability limits and provide reproducible measurements. An integrator can identify an ambiguous control behavior. An operator can describe a failure case or a requirement that is missing from a validation plan. A researcher can explain the conditions under which an architecture's benefit holds.

Use the OCP community resources and the white paper to locate the relevant discussions, then bring a clear requirement, example or test. Separate observed behavior from proposed behavior and identify the assumptions others would need to reproduce your result. This helps an open ecosystem improve through evidence that multiple organizations can understand and apply.
