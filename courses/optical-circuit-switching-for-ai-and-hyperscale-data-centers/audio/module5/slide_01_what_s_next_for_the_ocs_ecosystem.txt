Module five asks what's next for the O C S ecosystem. Production deployments have established important use cases, while research continues to explore new ways to use optical connectivity. Broader adoption depends on more than a switching component. It also needs clear capabilities, consistent control, operational verification and shared evidence. We'll connect those needs to the roles of technology suppliers, systems integrators, operators and the open standards community.
