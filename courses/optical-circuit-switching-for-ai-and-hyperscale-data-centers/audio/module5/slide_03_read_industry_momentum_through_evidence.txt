Jupiter and the T P U examples show that optical circuit switching can be part of large operational systems. Emerging architectures show a broader set of possibilities. These are different kinds of evidence, and keeping them separate makes the industry's progress easier to assess.

A production deployment demonstrates that a particular organization has integrated and operated a design under its requirements. A research evaluation explores an approach under specified conditions. An interoperability event can test whether implementations work together under agreed scenarios. A planned demonstration is a future activity until it has occurred and produced evidence. The 2026 Summit discussions are useful for understanding priorities and ongoing work, without treating every proposed interface or event as a completed industry standard.
