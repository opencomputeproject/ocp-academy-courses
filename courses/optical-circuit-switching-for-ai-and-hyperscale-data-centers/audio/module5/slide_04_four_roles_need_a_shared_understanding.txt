Technology suppliers develop the optical mechanisms and describe their supported behavior. Systems integrators combine those devices with controllers, software and an overall architecture. Operators turn that architecture into a service, with requirements for capacity, availability, maintenance and recovery. Standards bodies and open communities provide places to develop common descriptions, specifications and tests.

Researchers contribute new ideas and evidence across these layers. The roles are complementary, and one organization may perform several of them. The important question is what must be communicated at each handoff. A supplier's port capability has to mean the same thing to the integrator's software and the operator's validation process. Otherwise, a functional component can still become difficult to deploy or support.
