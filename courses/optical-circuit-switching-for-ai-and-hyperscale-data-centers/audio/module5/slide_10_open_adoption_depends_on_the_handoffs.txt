The ecosystem story connects everything in the course. A mechanism must meet physical requirements. An architecture must use it in a way that benefits the workload. A control system must express and verify the desired connectivity. An open community can make those requirements and results easier to share.

As you evaluate an O C S proposal, ask where its evidence comes from and which handoff needs further work. Your contribution does not have to be a complete switch or network. A precise requirement, an unambiguous model or a reproducible validation case can help the next organization adopt the technology responsibly.
