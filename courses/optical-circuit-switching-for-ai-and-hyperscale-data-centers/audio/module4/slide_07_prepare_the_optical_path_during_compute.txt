The MixNet idea exploits an overlap opportunity. While accelerators perform computation, the system can prepare regional optical connections for the upcoming communication phase. If the demand prediction is useful and the change completes in time, less of the communication interval is consumed by reconfiguration.

This arrangement depends on coordination. The controller needs a sufficiently accurate view of demand, the optical fabric needs enough capacity, and the transition must fit the available compute window. A prediction error or a longer-than-expected change can reduce the benefit. Hybrid fabrics provide a way to assign traffic according to these properties, rather than forcing every flow through the same mechanism. The 2026 use-case discussion also frames orchestration and spare-resource pooling as system-level requirements.
