# pronunciation: OCS -> O C S; EPS -> E P S; OEO -> O E O; TPUv4 -> T P U version four; GPU -> G P U; GPUs -> G P Yous; ACTINA -> Actina; MoE -> M O E; OCS-BCube -> O C S B Cube; gNMI -> g N M I
Actina stands for Adaptive Circuit Switching Techniques for AI Networking Architectures. Its O C S B Cube concept explores a multidimensional optical topology for accelerator training, using specialized silicon-photonic, multi-port transceivers. A controller can use workload demand to change wavelength assignments and steer available bandwidth.

Three layers must stay distinct. The physical topology determines possible paths. The workload creates communication demand. The control system chooses how available optical capacity serves that demand. Wavelength reconfiguration changes an allocation supported by the architecture; it does not create arbitrary new fibers. Performance therefore depends on the transceiver design, the prediction or knowledge of traffic, and the overhead of making a change at the right time.
