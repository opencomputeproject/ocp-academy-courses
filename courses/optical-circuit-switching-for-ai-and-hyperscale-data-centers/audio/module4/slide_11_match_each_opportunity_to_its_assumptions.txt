The emerging examples use optical connectivity in different ways. Actina explores adapting an allocation to training demand. MixNet aligns a regional optical fabric with expert-parallel communication and the available compute window. Quantum networking values transparency but requires careful control of the physical path. Resource disaggregation uses connectivity as one part of a broader composition system.

For each proposal, ask what is physically available, what the controller must know, and which measurements support the claimed benefit. Research results are most useful when their workload, hardware and evaluation conditions remain attached to them.
