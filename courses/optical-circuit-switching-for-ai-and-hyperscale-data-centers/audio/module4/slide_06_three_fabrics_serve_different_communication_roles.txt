Mixture-of-experts models activate selected expert components for different inputs. Expert parallelism distributes those experts, creating communication between the resources that dispatch work and the resources that execute it. MixNet studies how that traffic can use a regional optical fabric alongside electrical networks.

Its local electrical fabric serves communication within a server. A global electrical fabric, using technologies such as Ethernet or InfiniBand, supports broader communication including data and pipeline parallelism. The regional optical fabric focuses on expert-parallel traffic. The source describes localized groups of eight to thirty-two accelerators whose communication changes gradually enough to support prediction. That locality is a workload observation for the design, not a universal property of every mixture-of-experts model.
