Module four explores emerging O C S use cases. The focus shifts from configuring a deployment or a job to matching connectivity more closely to changing communication demand. We'll examine Actina and O C S B Cube, MixNet for mixture-of-experts training, quantum networking and resource disaggregation. These examples have different maturity levels and different physical requirements. Treat them as ways to reason about opportunities, with explicit attention to the assumptions each architecture needs.
