**Title**\
Optical Circuit Switching for AI and Hyperscale Data Centers

**Short Description**\
Explore OCS mechanisms, deployment architectures, emerging AI uses and the open ecosystem behind optical networking.

**Long Description**

Learn how optical circuit switching connects endpoints, how its physical mechanisms differ, and where it fits in AI and hyperscale data centers. This introductory series combines architectural explanations, original recordings, narrated visualizations and practical knowledge checks to connect technical benefits with their operating conditions.

**Overall duration: 75 minutes**

**Module 1: Introduction to OCS — 12 minutes.**\
Separate the optical path from packet services and system constraints.

**Module 2: The OCS Technology Landscape — 16 minutes.**\
Match the physical switching mechanism to a workload and optical budget.

**Module 3: OCS in Action: Current AI Datacenter Deployments — 18 minutes.**\
Read the architecture behind interconnect efficiency, cluster flexibility and recovery.

**Module 4: Emerging OCS Use Cases — 18 minutes.**\
Explore traffic-aware training, hybrid fabrics, quantum links and resource pools.

**Module 5: What's Next for the OCS Ecosystem? — 11 minutes.**\
Connect deployment evidence to interoperable systems and community participation.
