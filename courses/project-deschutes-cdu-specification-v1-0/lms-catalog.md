**Title**\
OCP Project Deschutes: Coolant Distribution Unit (CDU) v1.0

**Short Description**\
Explore Deschutes CDU architecture, performance, controls, serviceability, deployment, and qualified supply chains.

**Long Description**

Explore OCP Project Deschutes and the Coolant Distribution Unit Specification v1.0. Locate the CDU between facility water and technology cooling loops, interpret its mechanical and thermal performance, and review controls, electrical interfaces, and serviceability. Connect deployment examples and the supplier model to the evidence needed for implementation and ongoing operation.

**Overall duration: 40 minutes**

**Module 1: Why Deschutes, Why Now — 10 minutes.**\
Introduce the cooling challenge and Deschutes architecture. Trace facility water through the CDU to the technology cooling system and place headline performance specifications in context.

**Module 2: CDU Architecture & Performance — 17 minutes.**\
Review physical geometry, fittings, pumps, heat exchangers, sensors, controls, and electrical feeds. Interpret published flow and thermal performance conditions and serviceability provisions.

**Module 3: Deployment, Supply Chain & Call to Action — 13 minutes.**\
Connect containment and rack-power examples to deployment decisions. Examine supplier qualification, field-replaceable units, isolation and service steps, and participation resources.
