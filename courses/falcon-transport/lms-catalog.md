**Title**\
Falcon Transport

**Short Description**\
Trace Falcon transactions, loss recovery, congestion control and multipathing with worked packet flows from v1.2.0.

**Long Description**

Build a transport-level model of Falcon from specification v1.2.0. Follow worked push and pull exchanges, interpret delivery feedback and loss recovery, and connect delay measurements to congestion control and multipathing. Nine animated packet flows and six page-cited knowledge-check questions develop the distinctions between transaction completion, packet acknowledgement and path credit. Examine v1.2 changes and backward compatibility, with references to related OCP Academy networking courses and Summit recordings. Upper-layer protocol mappings and implementation details remain outside scope.

**Overall duration: 60 minutes**

**Module 1: Transaction Architecture — 20 minutes.**\
Follow ordered push and pull transactions, explain independent identities and resources, and recognize v1.2 compatibility conditions.

**Module 2: Reliable Delivery and Loss Recovery — 20 minutes.**\
Interpret receiver windows and EACKs, follow selective and time-based repair, and trace push/pull retries and CIE/Resync recovery.

**Module 3: Congestion Control and Multipathing — 20 minutes.**\
Calculate fabric delay, distinguish fabric and NIC control, interpret compact CSIG, and follow the v1.2 Max-Open-Window scheduler and PLB compatibility limits.
