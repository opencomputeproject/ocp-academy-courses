# pronunciation: RACK -> Rack; EACK -> E ack
Welcome to module two, Reliable Delivery and Loss Recovery. A packet can arrive, wait for acknowledgement, be retransmitted, or complete with an error. Those events mean different things. This module follows the evidence that lets Falcon choose the next recovery action. We will separate the two packet windows, repair one missing packet, compare time-based recovery with quiet-tail probing, and trace retry ownership and error recovery. The examples use Falcon version one point two point zero.
