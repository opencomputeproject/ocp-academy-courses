# pronunciation: RACK -> Rack; EACK -> E ack
By the end of this module, you will be able to read a receiver bitmap without confusing receipt with acknowledgement. You will explain why an internal receive window and a wire bitmap have different widths. You will follow selective repair, distinguish Rack's time-based inference from a tail loss probe, and identify which endpoint owns a receiver-not-ready retry.

Finally, you will explain how Resync restores sequence progress while preserving an error completion. This is the second module in Falcon Transport. Module one established the connection and transaction model. Module three follows the measurement and path-selection loops.

The estimated twenty minutes includes narration, time to inspect the diagrams, and two questions. Numerical timing examples are illustrative, not tuning recommendations. Upper Layer Protocol actions remain boundary events. We do not examine application interfaces, mappings, drivers, or hardware implementation.
