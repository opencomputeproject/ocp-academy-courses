Welcome to Falcon Transport. This course has three modules: transaction architecture, reliable delivery and loss recovery, and congestion control and multipathing. Together, they take about sixty minutes, including diagrams and knowledge checks. We begin with transaction architecture. You'll follow requests, packets, acknowledgements and completions across a connection while keeping the upper-layer protocol at its defined boundary. The course follows Falcon specification version one point two point zero.
