Welcome to Falcon congestion control and multipathing, the third and final module of Falcon Transport. We will follow the feedback loop from measured packet delay to congestion windows, path selection, and protective route changes. The examples use the Falcon version one point two specification. You will see how an acknowledgment can carry both delivery information and congestion evidence, while those two jobs remain distinct. This module takes about twenty minutes including time to inspect the animations and answer two questions.
