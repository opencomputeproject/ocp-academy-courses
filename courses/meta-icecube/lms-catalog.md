**Title**\
Meta IceCube: Anatomy of a 102.4 Tb/s Ethernet Switch

**Short Description**\
Trace IceCube’s ASIC-to-optics path, then explore its Ethernet interfaces, hybrid cooling, and field service.

**Long Description**

Explore Meta’s 102.4 Tb/s IceCube Ethernet switch from its front-panel connectors to its ORv3 HPRv1 power and cooling interfaces. Practice reading the chassis, interpreting port counts, and locating management and service points.

**Overall duration: 24 minutes**

**Module 1: From ASIC to fiber — 11 minutes.**\
Follow an electrical transmit signal to the pluggable optics. Interpret two system configurations and tell physical connectors from logical Ethernet interfaces.

**Module 2: Cooling and servicing IceCube — 12 minutes.**\
Locate the ORv3 HPRv1 rack connections, trace two cooling paths, and diagnose a slow fan using IceCube’s hardware management architecture.
