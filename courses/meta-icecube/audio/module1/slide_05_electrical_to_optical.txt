# pronunciation: ASIC → A sick

In the top view, orient yourself with the front on the left. The switch A sick sits under the large cold plate in the center. Its serializer and deserializer, called SerDes, drive high-speed electrical signals toward the front. The yellow cable assemblies are the electrical flyover path. They carry those signals inside the chassis to the Physical Interface Cards and the O S F P connectors.

The design specification describes the IceCube variant based on Broadcom’s Tomahawk six C. Meta also announced a variant based on Nvidia’s Spectrum six. The signal path here follows the Broadcom-based design described in the O C P specification.

The specification calls the board design PHY-less. In this path, the host signal reaches the pluggable module without going through a separate board-level PHY chip. The optical module changes the outgoing electrical signal into light. In the other direction, the module receives light and converts it back to an electrical signal for the switch.
