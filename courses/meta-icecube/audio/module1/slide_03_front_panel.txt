Start in the center of the front panel. The rows of rectangular openings are the sixty-four O S F P data connectors. Each opening takes one module. The two Physical Interface Cards behind the panel carry thirty-two connectors each.

At the edges you’ll find connections with other jobs. On the right, the Q S F P twenty-eight port is for management. Below it, the E one dot S solid-state drive is accessible at the front. On the left are the R J forty-five connectors and status indicators.

If you were inventorying the equipment, you could count the optical openings by sight. To find the number of Ethernet interfaces, you’d also need to know how the modules and their links are configured.
