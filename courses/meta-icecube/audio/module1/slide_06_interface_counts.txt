A connector is the physical opening where the transceiver plugs in. A logical interface is a link the switch can expose and configure separately. With the dual eight-hundred-gigabit module, one occupied connector can have two Ethernet links, each running at eight hundred gigabits per second. Multiply sixty-four by two and you get a hundred and twenty-eight links. At eight hundred gigabits per second each, that’s a hundred and two point four terabits per second.

The spec also describes five hundred and twelve interfaces operating at two hundred gigabits per second. The total is the same. In the dual-eight-hundred case, the two links in one module can report different states. Where the front panel uses a shared link LED, LED select lets you tell which subport the LED is reporting.
