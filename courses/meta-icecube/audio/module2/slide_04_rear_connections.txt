Face the back of the switch. The blind-mate quick-connect on your right brings coolant into the chassis. The matching connection on your left carries the warmed coolant back to the rack loop. Those directions are always relative to the rear view.

The connector in the center mates to the rack’s direct-current busbar. You might notice that the rear image is marked forty-eight volts D C. The written IceCube requirement gives fifty-one volts nominal and a steady-state range from forty-five to fifty-three volts D C. Use those written values when describing the blade’s rack interface.

The fan grilles sit between the two outer couplings. They belong to the other cooling path.
