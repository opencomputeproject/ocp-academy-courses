These two views show IcePack, Meta’s first liquid-cooled network rack, populated with IceCube switch blades. IcePack is based on Open Rack version three High Power Rack, version one. Each IceCube blade occupies two Open Units and fits into the rack with a canister adapter.

Start at the front to identify the rows of optics and their fiber cabling. That is the side you use to reach the optical modules, management ports, and the E one dot S solid-state drive. The rack brings power and coolant to the rear of the chassis, where the fans also exhaust. We’ll look at those rear connections next.
