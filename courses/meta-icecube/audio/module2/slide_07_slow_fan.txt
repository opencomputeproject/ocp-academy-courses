Look at the rear of IceCube. There are four fan trays. Suppose one fan drops below its expected speed while the switch is running. How does the platform find out?

Programmable logic measures fan speed in revolutions per minute. If the reading is too low, the logic reports a warning to the microserver through a register. The microserver also uses temperature readings to calculate a fan speed command. The warning and the command travel in opposite directions.

IceCube splits platform management between the microserver and the baseboard management controller, or B M C. The microserver handles sensor readings and the fan-speed algorithm. The B M C handles power-on, failure recovery, and system event logging.

The rear fan trays are replaceable in the field, and the front gives access to the E one dot S solid-state drive. Before you move on, name the controller that runs the fan-speed algorithm.
