IceCube has four rear fan trays in a three-plus-one arrangement. The air-cooled components are specified to stay within their operating limits with any one fan tray failed or removed. The front-accessible E one dot S solid-state drive is field replaceable, as are the internal Run B M C module card and memory modules.

A detected tray leak takes a different path. It is reported to the B M C and the rack management controller. The blade’s main power shuts down with standby power retained. The rack controller shuts off the coolant supply to that blade. In the field, match the physical component and the reporting path to the symptom you’re investigating.
