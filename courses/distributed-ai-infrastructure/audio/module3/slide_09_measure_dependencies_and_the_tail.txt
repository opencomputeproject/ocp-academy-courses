Keep timing evidence in three roles. Dependencies establish which work must precede other work. Completion measurements show where required participants become ready. Clock information helps interpret observations made on different hosts.

An average can conceal a late required contribution, and a misleading timestamp can conceal a valid causal sequence. Neither observation alone tells you the root cause.

Use identifiers, timing uncertainty and correlated system signals together. That gives the review team a stronger explanation than either assuming perfect simultaneity or blaming every stall on clock drift.
