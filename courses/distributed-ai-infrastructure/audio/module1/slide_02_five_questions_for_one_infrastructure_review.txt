# pronunciation: AI -> "A I"

We thank Ankur Sharma, Senior Principal Architect at Oracle, for his technical guidance and contribution to developing this course. His explanations will accompany our exploration of distributed A I infrastructure.

We'll begin with coordination boundaries, then trace distributed state, timing and causality, geographic placement, and infrastructure guardrails. Allow about eighty minutes, including the recordings and knowledge checks. You only need a working familiarity with servers and networks. We'll introduce the specialist terms as they become useful. Throughout the course, we'll follow a team reviewing an expansion of its A I environment.
