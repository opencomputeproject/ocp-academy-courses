# pronunciation: AI -> "A I"

Welcome to Distributed A I Infrastructure. Useful A I capacity depends on more than the number of accelerators you install. It depends on which operations need to coordinate, which state they share, and what happens when a required participant is late.

Across five modules, we'll connect those dependencies to timing, distance and controlled recovery. The aim is practical: give you a way to examine an infrastructure proposal and explain what evidence supports it. Start with the workload. Then ask what the surrounding system must provide for that workload to make useful progress.
