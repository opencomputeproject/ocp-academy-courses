# pronunciation: AI -> "A I"

Our example team proposes adding accelerators to an existing site. One group trains a model. Another service answers independent inference requests.

Begin by separating those workloads. For training, identify the participating group and its communication pattern. For serving, identify where requests are routed and which shared services they depend on. Then locate the physical and administrative boundaries around each.

The team should bring measurements of useful progress, the supporting fabric and input paths, and the site's approved capacity envelope. It should also explain which work must stop if a participant becomes unavailable.

An expansion can be reasonable without every component scaling in the same proportion. The point is to make the dependencies visible before spending on the next constraint.

For a broader view of the physical infrastructure, IT building blocks and management responsibilities around these decisions, continue with Open Systems for A I. Here, we'll keep following the behavior of the workload and the evidence needed to explain it.
