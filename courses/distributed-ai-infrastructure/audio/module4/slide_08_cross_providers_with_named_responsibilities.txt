A provider boundary separates responsibilities as well as resources. The example team's review should identify the identities allowed to act, who controls keys, where the relevant data may be stored or processed, and who authorizes recovery changes.

Use an explicit policy for the scenario. For example, the team's dataset is permitted only in Region A. The orchestration and data paths must enforce that requirement. This is a declared design constraint, not a universal statement about the law for every dataset or country.

Ask how policy is checked during ordinary operation and during failure. A backup path or recovery location must not silently violate the same requirement.

Open interfaces can make integration easier, but hardware compatibility alone does not guarantee workload portability. Software behavior, data formats, identity and operating procedures also matter. Document who provides evidence at each boundary so that the deployment can be reviewed as a complete service.
