Match the placement to the required coordination. A dependent remote exchange, a buffered supply path and independent regional serving have different timing behavior.

Private connectivity can improve control, but it cannot erase propagation delay or guarantee that every operating objective is met. Measure the selected service and its failure paths.

Finally, define model-version, session, data and administrative policies explicitly. A useful multi-region review connects those policies to the actual workload and to named responsibilities. It doesn't begin and end with a map of two sites.
