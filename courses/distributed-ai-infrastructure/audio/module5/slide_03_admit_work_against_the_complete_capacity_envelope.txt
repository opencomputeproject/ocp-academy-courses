A device can be idle without being available for this workload. Admission must consider the full resource envelope at the relevant boundary.

The electrical supply needs to support the declared load and operating condition. The cooling system needs to support the equipment's qualified thermal and fluid requirements. Communication, data supply and management paths must also be ready for the work. Maintenance and redundancy conditions can change the available envelope.

Avoid turning one rack-power number into a universal cooling rule. Equipment design, component limits, air and liquid paths, facility conditions and service requirements determine the actual solution. Use the selected system's verified requirements and the site's approved capacity.

The same discipline applies to expansion. If the demand exceeds the approved headroom, available accelerators do not remove that constraint. The request remains pending until the team changes the demand, provides capacity or validates an appropriate alternative.

Admission is where the workload proposal meets the physical system. Make that decision before starting work that the environment cannot support.
