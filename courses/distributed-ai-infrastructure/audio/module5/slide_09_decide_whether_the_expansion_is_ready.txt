# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"

The example team is ready to make its expansion decision. Ask it to answer five connected questions.

Which operations and participants must coordinate? Which state must remain valid? What timing evidence explains the work's completion behavior? Which dependencies and policies cross sites? And how will the system admit and recover the workload within its approved limits?

A complete answer connects the proposed configuration to measurements and a tested operating procedure. If the fabric, checkpoint path or physical envelope is still unverified, the team should name that gap and the evidence needed to close it. More available devices don't close it automatically.

Use the O C P Academy technical catalog when the next question requires specialty depth in cooling, power, optics, transport or management. The purpose of this course is to help you recognize which question matters and how to connect the specialist evidence back to the workload.

A defensible readiness decision can approve the proposal, limit its scope or keep it pending. What matters is that the conclusion follows from the evidence.
