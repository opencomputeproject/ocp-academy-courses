Carry five questions into the next review. Who must coordinate? Which state must be preserved? What timing evidence supports the diagnosis? Which dependencies cross sites? And how is growth or recovery bounded?

Keep correctness, performance, availability and observability distinct while examining how they interact. A slower operation is not automatically corrupt. A restored device is not automatically a ready participant. A private link is not automatically a complete service contract.

The useful outcome is a review that identifies assumptions, tests them at the right boundary, and explains which operating decision the evidence supports.
