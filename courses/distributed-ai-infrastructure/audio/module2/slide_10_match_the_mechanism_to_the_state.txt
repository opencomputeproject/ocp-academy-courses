Distribution does not always mean sharding. Model replicas and model partitions create different exchanges and recovery needs.

A supported membership change can provide elasticity, but a new device must become a valid participant through the framework's process. Finally, define retries at the layer where they operate, with identity, limits and a recovery boundary.
