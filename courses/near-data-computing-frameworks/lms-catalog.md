**Title**\
Near Data Computing: Frameworks and Open Design Questions (Beta)

**Short Description**\
Beta: Explore near data computing frameworks, test draft design ideas, and contribute to the OCP DCC workstream.

**Long Description**

This beta course explores near data computing (NDC) through the working Near Data Computing Framework draft v0.1. Learners compare compute placement across memory, storage, and networking; trace application-to-device integration; distinguish published standards, draft proposals, and proofs of concept; and prepare evidence-based feedback for the OCP Data Centric Computing (DCC) workstream. The course supports community review and helps identify gaps in the draft paper.

**Overall duration: 40 minutes**

**Module 1: Choosing Where Computation Runs — 12 minutes.**\
Explore the data-movement problem, compare NDC device classes and device-first versus accelerator-first architectures, and develop a testable placement hypothesis for a workload.

**Module 2: Connecting Applications to NDC Devices — 14 minutes.**\
Trace a conceptual offload request through frameworks, runtimes, and device support. Identify the data representation, ownership, completion, and visibility contracts needed for correct integration.

**Module 3: Testing the Proposals and Contributing to DCC — 14 minutes.**\
Compare interface starting points, examine inference and vector-search proofs of concept, plan a fair end-to-end evaluation, and prepare a focused contribution to the DCC workstream.
