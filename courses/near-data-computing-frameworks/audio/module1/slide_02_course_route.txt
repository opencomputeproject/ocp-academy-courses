# pronunciation: NDC -> N D C; DCC -> D C C; OCP -> O C P
Data Centric Computing, or D C C, is the O C P workstream bringing together approaches that place useful computation according to where data resides. Near Data Computing, or N D C, names the family of technologies we examine here. Keep that distinction in mind: the community is broader than a particular device or interface.

The first module develops your placement reasoning. The second connects that reasoning to application frameworks, runtimes, data descriptions, and device interfaces. The third examines concrete proposals and proof-of-concept integrations, then helps you prepare a useful contribution.

Allow about forty minutes, including the questions and short reflection activities. You don't need special hardware or a programming environment. Keep one workload in mind as you go. You'll gradually record its data location, a missing integration contract, and evidence that could clarify a design decision. Sharing your note with D C C is optional. Completing the course does not require agreement with an architectural proposal.
