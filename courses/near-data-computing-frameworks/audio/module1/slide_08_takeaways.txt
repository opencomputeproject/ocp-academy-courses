The central question is where useful work should run relative to its data. Memory capacity, bandwidth, latency, and energy describe different parts of that decision. Near data technologies provide several placements across memory, storage, and networking.

The device-first and accelerator-first options help you ask what the host addresses and how it invokes work. They do not settle every access, coherency, or coordination rule. Those contracts depend on the implementation.

Your placement hypothesis should connect a particular workload to a change in the traffic pattern. Test the full path, including preparation and downstream use. A smaller result is a promising reason to investigate; measurements determine whether the complete application benefits.
