Frameworks and compiler paths help select and express useful work. Runtime libraries, adapters, and drivers make it executable on a particular device. A common interface can connect those responsibilities, but it does not replace them.

Follow control messages separately from operand movement. Describe both the bytes and the operation that gives them meaning. Then make ownership, lifetime, completion, and visibility explicit. Shared addressing and coherency are helpful capabilities when supported, rather than substitutes for the full contract.

Your contribution can be small. One precise question about a missing assumption is more useful than a broad request for interoperability. Tie the question to a workload and show what another implementation would need to know.
