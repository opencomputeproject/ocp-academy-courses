By the end of this module, you'll be able to explain what frameworks, compiler paths, runtime libraries, and device support contribute to execution. You'll trace a request while keeping control messages separate from operand movement.

You'll also distinguish a description of data from a description of the operation to perform on it. Finally, you'll identify an ownership, completion, or visibility question that the draft needs to resolve for your chosen workload. We will treat missing definitions as contribution opportunities. Any descriptor you propose here is an illustrative review aid, rather than new specification text.
