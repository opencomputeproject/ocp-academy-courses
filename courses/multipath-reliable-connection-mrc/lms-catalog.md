**Title**\
Multipath Reliable Connection (MRC) Technical Overview

**Short Description**\
Explore MRC multipath RDMA, reliability, congestion control, failure recovery, and AI training fabric deployment.

**Long Description**

Explore Multipath Reliable Connection, an open RDMA transport designed for AI training fabrics. Connect tail latency, incast, and path failures to multipath packet delivery, independent reliability, congestion control, and recovery. Examine topology choices, application and controller interfaces, deployment evidence, and the tradeoffs that shape an MRC implementation.

**Overall duration: 30 minutes**

**Module 1: Why MRC — 8 minutes.**\
Explain the load-balancing, incast, and failure challenges of synchronous AI training. Position MRC relative to conventional reliable-connection transport and the open OCP ecosystem.

**Module 2: Inside MRC — 13 minutes.**\
Trace packet spraying, routing choices, out-of-order placement, reliability, congestion signals, and path-failure bypass. Connect each mechanism to the problem it addresses.

**Module 3: Deploying MRC — 9 minutes.**\
Examine multi-plane topologies, deployment evidence, application and controller APIs, and performance comparisons. Identify transport limitations and implementation tradeoffs relevant to an AI fabric review.
