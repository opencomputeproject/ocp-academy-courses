# title: Welcome to Open Rack
# pronunciation: OCP -> O C P
Welcome to Open Rack, from O C P Academy. Picture a row of servers in a data center. What holds them up? Where do they get their power? The answers start with the rack. Open Rack is the Open Compute Project's approach to designing that shared space. It publishes the dimensions and connections so different teams can design servers, switches, and power equipment for the same rack.

In this course, you'll meet two members of the family: Open Rack version three and Open Rack Wide. We'll start with the frame, then explore what people have built inside it.
