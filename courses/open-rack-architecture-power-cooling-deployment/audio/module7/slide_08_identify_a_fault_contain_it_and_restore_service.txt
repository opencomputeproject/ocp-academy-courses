A cooling fault needs a defined response, not a promise that a coupling cannot leak. Detection may come from pressure, flow, temperature or other qualified monitoring. The meaning of a signal and the required action depend on the configured system and approved operating process.

The animation separates detection, containment and recovery. It is a conceptual response map, not a valve-operating or live-maintenance procedure. Identify which equipment is affected, which protective functions act locally, and who owns the decision to isolate or reduce load.

An alarm clearing doesn't necessarily establish that the cause is resolved or that full operation is supported. Verify the required restoration conditions, inspect or test as the approved procedure requires, and obtain the appropriate authorization before returning to service. Preserve the event and configuration record. This prevents a nominally healthy display from standing in for evidence that the affected mechanical, fluid, electrical and thermal interfaces are ready again.
