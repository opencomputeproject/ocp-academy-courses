The Pivoting Blind Mate Coupling, or P B M C, defines plug and socket interfaces for a blind-mate fluid connection. The specification describes pivoting behavior that supports the permitted alignment relationship. It is not inherently limited to one rack frame.

The animation uses the source drawings and staged highlights. It identifies the plug and socket, the pivoting compliance and the defined mate-distance relationship. It does not invent internal sealing stages, an audible click or a universal self-centering mechanism. The specification leaves relevant internal design choices to suppliers while establishing interface and performance requirements.

Evaluate the selected coupling against its permitted mate distances, misalignment, forces, fluid conditions and qualification. A blind-mate label doesn't promise perfect alignment, zero leakage under every condition or unrestricted live replacement. Those claims require scoped evidence and approved system procedures. The correct mental model is a specified interface operating within demonstrated limits, integrated into a supported mechanical and hydraulic system.
