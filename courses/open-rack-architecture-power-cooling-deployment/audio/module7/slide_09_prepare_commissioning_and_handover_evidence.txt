Commissioning and handover connect design intent to an accepted operating system. Preserve the installed configuration and document revisions, the conditions tested, the acceptance results and any restrictions. Include the limits the operator must maintain at electrical and thermal handoffs.

Identify monitoring and response ownership. The operator needs to know which signals matter, which actions are automatic, and which require an authorized procedure or another team. An exception accepted during deployment must remain visible after the project team leaves.

Also define change triggers. Replacing equipment, changing fluid, rerouting hoses or updating a control revision can affect previously accepted interfaces. Not every change requires the same review, but none should be assumed harmless solely because the rack still powers on. A concise, traceable handover record supports operation, recovery and later integration. It is part of readiness, not an administrative step after readiness has already been declared.
