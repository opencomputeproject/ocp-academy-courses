The evidence record remains useful after commissioning. Equipment generations change, suppliers revise components, and operators adjust workload or cooling conditions. Preserve enough configuration and revision control to recognize when the accepted envelope has changed.

Review the interfaces affected by a proposed change. A new component may fit while changing current demand, heat rejection, alignment or control behavior. A fluid change can affect multiple wetted materials. A revised handling arrangement can change which loaded-route evidence applies.

Shared interfaces create opportunities for reuse and replacement, but substitutions still need a supported basis. Scale the review to the consequence of the change and retain the decision and restrictions. This is how openness becomes a practical operational benefit: more qualified options with traceable boundaries, rather than more combinations whose behavior is assumed. The same method supports future rack architectures even when their dimensions, voltage or equipment packaging differ from today's examples.
