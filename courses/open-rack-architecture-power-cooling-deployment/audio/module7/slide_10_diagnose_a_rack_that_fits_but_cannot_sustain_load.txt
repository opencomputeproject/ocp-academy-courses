Consider a rack that fits its supports and has adequate electrical capacity, but cannot sustain the requested workload because the receiving loop doesn't meet the required thermal conditions. The mechanical and power checks can remain valid while the operating case fails.

Trace the heat path to locate the actual boundary mismatch. Don't immediately blame the coupling or install a larger power shelf. A change to flow, temperature, heat rejection or the equipment envelope must be supported by the responsible design and site teams.

The review decision is to hold the unsupported operating case. A reduced or bounded activity may be possible only with its own justified conditions and restrictions. Record what evidence would close the thermal gap. The linked Project Brazos material offers relevant context for cooling integration in an existing facility, but it doesn't replace this site's commitments. A rack is a system of interfaces precisely because a pass in one domain cannot compensate for a blocking gap in another.
