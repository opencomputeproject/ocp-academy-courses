Follow heat beyond the coupling. Equipment transfers heat to coolant, the rack routes it through its manifold and connections, and the receiving system must carry it to an adequate heat-rejection path. A coolant distribution unit, or C D U, may separate the technology cooling system from the facility water system.

The technology cooling system, often called T C S, serves the equipment side of that boundary. The facility water system, or F W S, serves the receiving side in the illustrated arrangement. These terms locate responsibilities; they do not imply identical fluid chemistry or operating conditions on both sides.

Keep the residual air-side heat visible. A liquid path that meets its own requirement can coexist with insufficient airflow for other components. The system review needs both. Define where temperatures, flows and pressures are guaranteed, and which party demonstrates heat rejection under the agreed operating cases. The linked C D U course provides specialist depth without replacing the configured-rack acceptance evidence.
