The internal assembly and manifold organize equipment and fluid connections, but their roles are distinct. Mechanical support establishes reference positions and permitted movement. The manifold distributes coolant through a defined arrangement. The coupling must work within the relationship those assemblies create.

Review alignment and deflection across the complete tolerance chain, not only at an isolated connection. Then include the space needed for installation and service. A support arrangement can be structurally acceptable while placing a connection outside its permitted mating condition.
