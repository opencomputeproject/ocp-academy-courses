# title: Supermicro: four nodes in less height
# pronunciation: spaced initials are spoken separately; Open U = OpenU; G P Yous = GPUs.
Supermicro offers two chassis for four liquid-cooled Arm server nodes. Start with the twenty-one-inch chassis. It occupies two Open U of rack height. The two drawings share a baseline so you can compare their widths and heights at the same scale.

Now look at the forty-two-inch chassis. The same four nodes occupy one Open U of height. Count the processors as well: two in each node, eight in either chassis. The wider chassis saves one Open U of vertical space while holding the same number of nodes and processors.
