# title: HPE builds AMD Helios on Open Rack Wide
# pronunciation: spaced initials are spoken separately; Open U = OpenU; G P Yous = GPUs.
Start at the frame of H P E’s version of A M D Helios. Its double-wide opening comes from Open Rack Wide. What does that width buy you? A single Helios compute tray carries four graphics processors along with the cold plates that draw heat away from them.

Pull that tray forward and you can see the distinction. The frame provides the supports. A M D’s reference design specifies what rides on them. H P E makes a further choice: Juniper switches connect the accelerators over a scale-up network. Another Helios builder can choose different switches while keeping the same mechanical format.
