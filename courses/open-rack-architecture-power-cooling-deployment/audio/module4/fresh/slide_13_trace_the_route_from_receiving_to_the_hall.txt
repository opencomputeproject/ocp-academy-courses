# title: Trace the route from receiving to the hall
# pronunciation: spaced initials are spoken separately; Open U = OpenU; G P Yous = GPUs.
Mark the inside corner and the two sides of the doorway. Move a scaled rack outline along one possible approach. First check the leading corner as it starts to turn. Then check the trailing corner while the front half moves into the next corridor. A clash can occur halfway through even when the start and finish positions both look clear.

This sketch is a way to find questions for the actual route, not a handling procedure or a clearance certification. The Academy course Open Data Center for A I: Applying the Specification looks at the building side of this same journey, from receiving equipment to providing a usable path into the data hall. If the turn caught your interest, that course is a good place to follow the rack beyond the edge of this drawing.
