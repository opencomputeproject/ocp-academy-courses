# title: Where do the mounting ears land?
# pronunciation: spaced initials are spoken separately; Open U = OpenU; G P Yous = GPUs.
The nineteen inches in a conventional rack-mount instrument are measured across its front panel, from the outside of one mounting ear to the outside of the other. The box behind that panel is narrower. Look at the small bolt holes in the ears. They sit in from the ends. Those holes need rails immediately behind them, not a cabinet wall nineteen inches away.

Google's version-three frame has mounting features inside its walls for add-on kits. With the Open Rack kit, a twenty-one-inch-format tray sits on supports arranged at Open U positions. With the E I A kit, the instrument's ears fasten to rails inside that same frame. Follow the outside posts as the view changes: they stay put. It's the hardware between the posts and the equipment that changes. The ears themselves stay nineteen inches across.
