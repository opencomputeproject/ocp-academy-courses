# title: Two rulers: OpenU and rack units
# pronunciation: spaced initials are spoken separately; Open U = OpenU; G P Yous = GPUs.
Start both rulers at zero. The next mark on the Open Rack ruler is forty-eight millimeters up. That's one Open U. The next mark on the conventional rack-unit ruler is forty-four point four five millimeters up. Those numbers describe the repeat distance between mounting positions. They aren't measurements of the sheet metal that forms a server.

Now follow the fourth marks across. Four Open U pitches reach one hundred ninety-two millimeters. Four conventional rack units reach one hundred seventy-seven point eight. The difference is fourteen point two millimeters, even though the starting positions matched exactly. Align the tops instead and the mismatch moves to the bottoms. When a drawing says a chassis uses four units, knowing which ruler the designer used is part of knowing where the next chassis can go.
