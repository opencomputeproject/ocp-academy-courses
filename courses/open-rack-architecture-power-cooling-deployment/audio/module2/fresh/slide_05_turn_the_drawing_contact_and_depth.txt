# title: Turn the drawing: contact and depth
# pronunciation: spaced initials are spoken separately; Open U = OpenU; G P Yous = GPUs.
Look at the shelf stop from the side. Google marks one reference for the stop and another for the busbar. The stop limits a tray's travel. A tray designed for blind mating has its electrical contact positioned to meet the busbar at that point. The close-up shows the two halves: the clip belongs to the equipment, the bar to the rack. A device with a cord inlet keeps that inlet, regardless of which rails support it.

Depth needs a pair of endpoints, too. Google's table gives standard and extended depths for its E I A rails. The tray dimensions below use different endpoints: six hundred thirteen millimeters to the front panel, six hundred thirty-five to the handle. That extra twenty-two millimeters describes a projecting handle. It doesn't make the supporting rails longer.
