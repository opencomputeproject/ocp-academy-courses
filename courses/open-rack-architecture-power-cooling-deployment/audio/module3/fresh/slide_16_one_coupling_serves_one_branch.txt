# title: One coupling serves one branch
# pronunciation: spaced initials are spoken separately; Open U = OpenU; G P Yous = GPUs.
A manifold and a coupling both carry coolant, but look at how many paths each one serves. The manifold is the common passage down the left. It distributes supply flow to several trays or collects returning flow from them. The small connector in each horizontal line joins just that branch across the rack-to-tray interface.

Now picture one tray moving into the frame. In a blind-mate design, the two connector halves come together with that motion, without someone reaching between their faces to join them. A pivoting blind-mate coupling is one published O C P design for this job. Its plug and socket can accommodate a limited mismatch as they approach. That adjustment belongs at one connection; the entire manifold doesn't have to shift each time a different tray arrives.
