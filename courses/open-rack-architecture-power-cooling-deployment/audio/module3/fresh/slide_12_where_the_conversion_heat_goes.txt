# title: Where the conversion heat goes
# pronunciation: spaced initials are spoken separately; Open U = OpenU; G P Yous = GPUs.
A converter gives off heat where the conversion takes place. Move the shelves to a sidecar and some of that heat moves with them. The processors haven't moved, though. Their heat still has to leave the trays in the I T rack. The two sets of wavy lines belong in different places for a reason.

There's a real example behind this separation. Bosch and Rittal presented a sidecar that uses an Open Rack version-three frame for power equipment. The familiar frame is carrying shelves rather than compute trays. That's one physical implementation, not a requirement that every Mount Diablo design use that frame. To explore how that power rack works, try the Mount Diablo course on O C P Academy.
