# title: From cold plate to facility loop
# pronunciation: spaced initials are spoken separately; Open U = OpenU; G P Yous = GPUs.
Start at the cold plate. It is in thermal contact with the processor. Heat passes through the contact materials and the plate into coolant moving through its internal passages. Follow the warmer fluid away from the tray. A return manifold brings flow from multiple branches together on its way to the coolant distribution unit, or C D U.

At the heat exchanger, follow the heat rather than the fluid. Heat passes into a separate facility circuit. The equipment-side coolant can then circulate back through the supply manifold to the cold plates; it doesn't have to mix with facility water. That's why the drawing has two loops meeting at one box. Follow a parcel of coolant and it stays in its circuit. Follow the processor's heat and its journey continues on the other side.
