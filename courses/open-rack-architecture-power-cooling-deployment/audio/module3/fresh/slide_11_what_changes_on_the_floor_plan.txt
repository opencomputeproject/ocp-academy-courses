# title: What changes on the floor plan?
# pronunciation: spaced initials are spoken separately; Open U = OpenU; G P Yous = GPUs.
Look at the space shaded for conversion equipment. In the first layout, that space is inside the I T rack. A power shelf and a server tray are different pieces of equipment, but both draw on the available space in that frame. In the second layout, the conversion shelves occupy a neighboring floor position. More positions inside the I T frame can now be available for compute equipment.

Which layout makes better use of space? It depends on what space you're counting. Measuring only the height freed up inside one rack leaves out the footprint of the sidecar. Counting only rack footprints leaves out what each populated rack can hold. To compare the layouts fairly, keep the computing workload the same and include the power equipment serving it in both drawings.
