# title: Mt. Diablo moves conversion next door
# pronunciation: spaced initials are spoken separately; Open U = OpenU; G P Yous = GPUs.
Which rack contains the power shelves? So far, we've pictured the shelves in the same frame as the servers. Mount Diablo separates those jobs. The rack on the left holds power equipment. The one on the right holds the computing equipment it serves. The neighboring power rack is often called a sidecar.

Start at the incoming feed, follow it into the conversion shelves, then cross the link between the two racks. That link delivers direct current to the I T rack. Power still has to reach each tray, and local electronics still provide the voltages the processors use. What changed is the address of the upstream converter. If you look only at the photograph of the computing rack, some of the equipment that makes it work is literally out of the picture.
