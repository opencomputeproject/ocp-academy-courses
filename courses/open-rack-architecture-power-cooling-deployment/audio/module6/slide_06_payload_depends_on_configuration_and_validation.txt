Payload is a configured-system claim. The Meta O R W design specification states a minimum equipment payload of four thousand seven hundred kilograms with allowed support bracing or stiffening. The equipment payload excludes the rack frame's own mass.

The document also explains that equipment design and arrangement affect frame capability. Read the permitted support and configuration conditions rather than copying the number into a universal O R W rating. Static support, transport, lifting and relocation are different cases and may require different evidence.

For the proposed assembly, identify the equipment distribution, center of gravity, braces, accessories and handling state. Ask which qualification record supports that configuration and which changes require reassessment. A larger nominal payload doesn't prove that the floor, lift, wheels, attachments or transport route support the loaded assembly. The acceptance decision needs the relevant configured evidence, not a number detached from the structure and test conditions that make it meaningful.
