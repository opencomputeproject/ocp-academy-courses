Read the frame from its reference interfaces, then locate the equipment supports and any internal assembly. A canister is an equipment-supporting assembly within a particular implementation; don't assume the word establishes one universal O R W layout or interface.

The source-derived plate shows where the shared frame geometry ends and the selected arrangement begins. Check rail and support locations, connection references, permitted deflection and the space needed to insert or remove equipment. Larger packaging doesn't remove tolerance accumulation or alignment requirements.

Keep the complete populated configuration in view. An internal assembly can fit within the frame while its hoses or cables obstruct another item's service path. A revised support can change the relationship between equipment and a fixed connection. The review therefore needs both the controlling drawings and evidence that the chosen assembly works within them. Use exact source geometry when dimensions matter; avoid inferring manufacturing details from a simplified rack illustration.
