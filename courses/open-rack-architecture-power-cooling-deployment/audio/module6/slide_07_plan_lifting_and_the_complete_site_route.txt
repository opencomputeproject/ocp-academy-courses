Plan the move as a sequence of supported states. Begin with receiving and the intended loaded configuration. Follow the approved handling arrangement through doors, turns, lifts or ramps to the final position. Include temporary equipment and packaging wherever they affect the envelope or loads.

The animation advances along the route and pauses at each evidence boundary. A frame payload result doesn't establish a lift rating or floor point-load limit. A doorway clearance doesn't prove that a turn can be completed. The handling method must remain within the supplied design's approved conditions.

At the final position, verify access for connection, isolation and replacement, not only the occupied footprint. Qualified site and handling teams must establish the actual procedure and limits. For our review, preserve their acceptance evidence and any restrictions. If a route segment is unresolved, hold the activity that depends on it rather than allowing the rack's structural rating to stand in for site approval.
