# pronunciation: ORv3 -> O R V three; ORW -> O R W; HPR -> H P R; PBMC -> P B M C; PMM -> P M M; CDU -> C D U; PSU -> P S U; OU -> O U; RU -> R U
The O R W base and Meta design documents have different scopes. The base defines shared frame and power interfaces, while leaving external dimensions and maximum payload to the implementation. The Meta document describes a particular rack-frame implementation.

Its nominal dimensions are two thousand three hundred ninety millimeters high, twelve hundred millimeters wide, and twelve hundred nineteen millimeters deep. These are implementation dimensions, not a universal O R W envelope. The controlling drawings and tolerances remain necessary for an engineering comparison.

Keep revisions together. A Summit presentation can show a development configuration or a differently stated nominal value. Don't combine that value with the published drawing to create a new geometry that neither source specifies. For a supplier proposal, identify the actual drawing revision and configured accessories, then compare its complete envelope to the site. The course plate helps locate the relevant interfaces; it is not a substitute for the source drawing or an installation survey.
