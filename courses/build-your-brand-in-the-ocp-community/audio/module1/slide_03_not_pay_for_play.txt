# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"
The first principle is simple: O C P is a Community where engineers come together to solve challenges in a safe and trusted environment.

That trust depends on clear boundaries. O C P isn't a pay-for-play channel, and it isn't a place where spending money buys editorial attention or technical influence. The Foundation also doesn't sell Community contact information. Participants should be able to collaborate without wondering whether every conversation will turn into an unsolicited sales approach.

This changes how an organization should think about brand building. The goal isn't to push a message at the Community. The goal is to become known for helping the Community make progress. A sponsorship can create visibility, and membership can show commitment, but neither one substitutes for useful behavior.

Dirk's phrase captures the point: "You get out of it what you put into it." The return comes from the quality of your participation, not simply the size of a budget. When your team consistently listens, contributes, and treats people with respect, recognition follows the work. That is the foundation for every channel we'll discuss later.
