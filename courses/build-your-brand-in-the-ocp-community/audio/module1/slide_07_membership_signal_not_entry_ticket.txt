# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"
Membership is one visible way for an organization to show commitment to the O C P Community. The current family includes Startup, Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum membership. Each level has its own benefits, eligibility rules, and program options, so use the current membership page when you need the details.

The larger point is more important than memorizing a benefit table. Membership is not required to participate in Community projects and workstreams. An organization can attend public meetings, learn, collaborate, and contribute without treating membership as an entry ticket.

If your organization does become a member, use that status as a promise of continued engagement. The logo can signal support, but the Community will still judge the brand by what people do after joining. Are engineers present? Do they listen? Do they contribute useful work? Do they collaborate responsibly?

Membership and participation work best together. The membership mark shows formal commitment. Consistent Community behavior gives that commitment meaning. The resource link takes you to the current O C P membership page so learners always see the latest tiers and benefits rather than a frozen list in this course.
