Membership is one visible way for an organization to show commitment to the O C P Community. The current family includes Startup, Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum membership. Each level has its own benefits and eligibility rules, so use the current membership page when you need the details.

Membership is not required to participate in public Community projects and workstreams. Your team can listen, learn, collaborate, and contribute without treating membership as an entry ticket.

Formal programs have their own requirements. To list offerings on the Marketplace, an organization needs O C P membership and enrollment in the Solution Provider program. That program is included for Silver, Gold, and Platinum members. Bronze members pay an additional program fee, while Startup arrangements depend on the startup level. Once enrolled, eligible offerings can be listed without a fixed limit on eligible listings, provided they meet the program's requirements.

Exhibit discounts and volunteer leadership eligibility also vary by tier and role. Don't assume that every membership level receives identical benefits or that open participation automatically makes someone eligible for every formal position.

If your organization becomes a member, use that status as a promise of continued engagement. The logo signals support, but the Community will still judge the brand by what people do after joining. Are engineers present? Do they listen? Do they share useful work and collaborate responsibly?

The membership mark shows formal commitment. Consistent Community behavior gives that commitment meaning. Use the current program pages for eligibility, fees, and benefits rather than relying on a frozen table.
