# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"; R&D -> "research and development"
Open participation creates several kinds of value at the same time.

Your engineers can help shape emerging technology roadmaps instead of waiting for those roadmaps to arrive. They can connect with peers across the industry and gain access to a wider pool of engineering and research and development experience. That shared context can help teams identify better approaches sooner.

Collaboration can also accelerate open standards and reduce time to market. When an industry agrees on adaptable interfaces, requirements, or best practices, organizations gain more choices. Those choices can reduce supply-chain risk and make it easier for a broader ecosystem to build compatible solutions.

The brand benefit is real, but it is the result rather than the starting pitch. People learn which teams bring evidence, which teams follow through, and which teams help others succeed. Over time, that behavior becomes technical credibility and trust.

O C P also connects innovation with efficiency, circularity, and sustainability. A contribution that helps the Community build better infrastructure can create value well beyond the organization that first proposed it. That is the power of working in the open.
