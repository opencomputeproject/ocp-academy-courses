# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"; 2025 -> "twenty twenty-five"; dirkv@opencompute.org -> "dirk vee at open compute dot org"
This course grows out of a presentation delivered in April twenty twenty-five by Dirk Van Slyke, Chief Marketing Officer for the O C P Foundation. Dirk has spent years helping organizations understand how to participate well and how to turn meaningful Community work into earned recognition.

We'll follow that practical path in two parts. First, we'll focus on earning trust through listening, participation, contribution, and collaboration. Then we'll look at the channels that can extend the reach of useful work, including the Marketplace, events, communications, and O C P Academy.

The full course takes about twenty-six minutes. You can also watch Dirk's original presentation from the resource link. His contact email appears again at the end if you want to continue the conversation.
