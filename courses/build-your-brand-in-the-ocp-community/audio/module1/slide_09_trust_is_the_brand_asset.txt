# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"
The main brand asset in O C P is trust.

Protect that trust by treating the Community as an engineering collaboration, not an advertising list. Start in the workstreams that match your organization's real expertise. Bring the people who can understand the problem, and give them time to listen before proposing an answer.

When an opportunity to help appears, choose the contribution format that serves the need. It might be a specification, a reference architecture, a tested configuration, a case study, a white paper, a video, or an educational resource. The format matters less than the usefulness and integrity of the work.

Build with other organizations when you can. Shared work is more credible, reaches more people, and reflects the open model itself. Let visibility and leadership follow demonstrated value.

If you remember one sequence from this module, remember this: listen, help, contribute, collaborate, and keep showing up. That is how awareness becomes trust, and how trust becomes a durable brand.
