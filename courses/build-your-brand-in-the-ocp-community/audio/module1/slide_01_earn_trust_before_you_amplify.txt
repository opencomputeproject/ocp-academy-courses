# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"
Module one: Earn Trust Before You Amplify.

Building a brand in the O C P Community starts in a different place from a traditional marketing campaign. It starts with useful participation. People notice the teams that listen carefully, share real expertise, and help the Community solve problems.

In this module, you'll learn how that work becomes credibility. We'll look at the trusted environment O C P protects, how to enter a project workstream thoughtfully, the many forms a contribution can take, and why collaboration creates more value than promotion alone. Let's get started.
