# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"
Module two: Turn Useful Work into Reach.

Trust gives your organization something worth amplifying. The next question is where and how to share it.

In this module, we'll look at the O C P Marketplace, the three major Summit families, regional Tech Days, third-party events, and the communication channels available across the Community. We'll also add O C P Academy as a place where expertise can become durable learning.

The goal is not to use every channel. It is to choose the channel that helps the right people understand and use the work.
