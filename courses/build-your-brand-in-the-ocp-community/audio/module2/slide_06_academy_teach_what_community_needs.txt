# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"
O C P Academy adds another important channel: durable education.

The Academy offers free, on-demand learning across open infrastructure topics. Learners can build knowledge on their own schedule, whether they are new to the Community or exploring a specific technology in greater depth. The catalog also includes Community-oriented learning, not only technical courses.

Course availability in multiple languages helps that knowledge travel across regions. Open-source course materials give the Community another way to participate. People can inspect the editable source, suggest improvements, report issues, and contribute updates as the technology or program evolves.

That makes education a meaningful form of contribution. A strong course can preserve the lessons from a workstream, a specification, a deployment, or a Summit presentation. It can help the next engineer avoid repeating old mistakes and arrive at a Community discussion with better context.

Use the main Academy link to explore the full catalog. The O C P Orientation catalog is the best next resource for learners who want detailed help navigating membership, Solution Provider programs, contributions, recognition, procurement, and volunteer leadership. The open-source repository shows how the course materials themselves can be improved.
