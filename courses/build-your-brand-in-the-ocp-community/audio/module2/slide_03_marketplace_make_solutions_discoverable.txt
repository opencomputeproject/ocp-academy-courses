# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"; 1.68% -> "one point six eight percent"; 0.6% -> "zero point six percent"
The O C P Marketplace helps potential adopters discover products, facilities, services, experience centers, and integrated solutions connected to approved O C P work.

Its multi-vendor structure matters. An adopter isn't limited to one company's catalog. The Marketplace brings together approved Solution Providers and a wide range of offerings, so visitors can compare options and find infrastructure that fits a specific need.

For a durable sense of scale, hundreds of members are O C P Solution Providers, and the Marketplace contains around one thousand listings. Those figures will continue to change, which is why this course uses broad language instead of freezing an exact count.

One statistic from Dirk's presentation remains useful: an average click-through rate of one point six eight percent to provider sites and contact information. Dirk called out why that result is unusually strong. A typical digital-marketing click-through rate is closer to zero point six percent, so the Marketplace rate was about two point eight times that benchmark. That suggests visitors aren't only browsing. They're qualified people actively looking for a solution and choosing to continue the conversation.

O C P doesn't sell the products. It helps interested visitors find the relevant provider. That makes the quality of the listing important. Clear descriptions, useful detail, appropriate images or videos, and a direct landing page can help a serious visitor understand the offering.

Marketplace participation must follow the current Solution Provider rules. Use the linked program information for the latest eligibility and benefits rather than relying on an older presentation.
