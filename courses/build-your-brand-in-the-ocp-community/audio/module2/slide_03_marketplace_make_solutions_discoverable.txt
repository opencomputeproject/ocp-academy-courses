The O C P Marketplace helps potential adopters discover products, facilities, services, experience centers, and integrated solutions connected to approved O C P work.

Its multi-vendor structure matters. An adopter isn't limited to one company's catalog. Visitors can compare offerings and find infrastructure that fits a specific need.

For a broad sense of scale, hundreds of members are O C P Solution Providers, and there are around one thousand Marketplace listings. Counts change, so treat this as an approximate sense of scale, not a live inventory or a promise that a presentation's snapshot remains current.

One statistic from Dirk's presentation remains useful: an average click-through rate of one point six eight percent to provider sites and contact information. In his original explanation, Dirk compared that with a digital-marketing benchmark of roughly zero point six percent. The Marketplace rate was about two point eight times that comparison figure. This is a historical comparison, not a universal benchmark or a guarantee of today's results. It illustrates why qualified visitors who are actively looking for solutions can be valuable.

O C P doesn't sell the products. It helps interested visitors reach the relevant provider. Clear descriptions, useful detail, appropriate images or videos, and a landing page for the specific offering help a serious visitor understand what is available.

Marketplace participation requires O C P membership and Solution Provider enrollment. Fees and benefits depend on the membership tier, and listings must meet current program requirements. Use the linked FAQs for those details. For a guided introduction, take the linked O C P Academy course, Introduction to the O C P Solution Provider Program.
