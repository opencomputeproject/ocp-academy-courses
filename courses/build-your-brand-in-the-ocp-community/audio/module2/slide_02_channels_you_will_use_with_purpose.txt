# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"
By the end of this module, you'll be able to choose an O C P channel with purpose rather than simply asking for more exposure.

You'll understand what the Marketplace does and why qualified discovery can be valuable to Solution Providers. You'll be able to distinguish the major O C P Summits from the smaller regional Tech Days and from third-party industry events.

You'll also compare formats such as the News and Blog, Newsletter, Podcast, Educational Webinars, project workstreams, targeted communications, and O C P Academy.

Most importantly, you'll apply a simple editorial filter. Is the story grounded in real O C P activity? Does it contain evidence, progress, collaboration, or a useful lesson? If so, which channel best serves the people who need it? Those questions will help you turn earned credibility into relevant reach.
