Earned reach lasts because it is attached to work the Community can use.

The Marketplace helps qualified visitors discover O C P-aligned offerings and connect with Solution Providers. Events create opportunities to present, host, speak, sponsor, exhibit, and collaborate. Use the major Summits, regional Tech Days, and third-party events according to the audience and the maturity of the story.

Communication channels each have a job. News and Blog content can document progress. The Newsletter keeps people current. The Podcast adds depth through conversation. Educational Webinars support curated thought leadership. Project communications keep technical work close to the people who own it.

O C P Academy turns expertise into reusable learning and gives the Community a way to improve the course source itself.

Across every channel, apply the same editorial test. Is the content newsworthy? Is it grounded in O C P activity? Does it offer evidence, collaboration, or a useful lesson? If the answer is yes, choose the format that best serves the audience. Let the channel extend the value, not replace it.
