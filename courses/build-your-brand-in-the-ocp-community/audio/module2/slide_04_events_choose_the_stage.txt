Events give useful work a live setting, but the right opportunity depends on the story and the audience.

O C P has three major Summit families: the Global Summit, the E M E A Summit, and the A pack Summit. These are the largest moments for Community announcements, technical presentations, collaboration, exhibits, and sponsorship.

O C P Tech Days are smaller regional events. They are generally a single day and are often delivered in the local language. Current Tech Day locations include Canada, China, Korea, and Singapore, with Singapore serving the broader Southeast Asia community.

O C P also participates in third-party industry events. The Foundation may host a workshop, contribute a track, join a panel, or need a Community speaker who can explain the work with credibility. If your engineers or project leaders can help, make that interest known.

There are several ways to participate. Submit strong content during a call for presentations. Offer to host an event or a facility tour. Speak, sponsor, exhibit, or propose a creative experience that helps people learn and collaborate.

Dirk's rule for speaking opportunities is wonderfully direct: "Best content wins." Start with the audience's need and the quality of the idea. Because schedules constantly change, use the live Upcoming Events and Past Events pages for current opportunities and examples.
