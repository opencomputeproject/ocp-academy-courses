Events give useful work a live setting, but the right opportunity depends on the story and the audience.

O C P has three major Summit families: Global, E M E A, and A pack. They bring the Community together for technical presentations, announcements, exhibits, sponsorship, and collaboration.

O C P Tech Days are smaller regional events, generally built around a main program day and often delivered in the local language. Canada, China, Korea, and Singapore are established locations, with Singapore serving the Southeast Asia community. Locations, dates, and formats can change, so use the live calendar rather than memorizing a map.

For research and emerging ideas, consider the Future Technologies Symposium, or F T S. It connects academic, research, startup, and venture communities with O C P's engineering ecosystem. That can be a better fit for an early research result than a general promotional announcement.

O C P also participates in third-party industry events. The Foundation may host a workshop, contribute a track, join a panel, or need a Community speaker who can explain the work with credibility.

There are several ways to participate. Submit strong content during a call for presentations. Offer to host an engineering workshop, Technology Day, or plugfest at your facility. A plugfest brings participants together to test how implementations work with one another.

For exhibits, Dirk describes a turnkey Summit approach that reduces the work of building, shipping, assembling, and storing a booth. A single sponsor portal and logistics contact can simplify coordination. Check the specific event's packages and pass rules, rather than assuming every edition works identically.

Speaking, hosting, sponsorship, and exhibits should all support useful collaboration. Dirk's rule for speaking opportunities is wonderfully direct: "Best content wins." Start with the audience's need and the quality of the idea. Then use the live Upcoming Events and Past Events pages to find the right current opportunity.
