**Title**\
How to Build Your Brand in the OCP Community

**Short Description**\
Earn trust through participation, contribution, collaboration, and useful communication

**Long Description**

Build a trusted presence in the OCP Community by contributing useful work and choosing the right channels to share it. Learn how participation, contribution, and collaboration create credibility, then use the Marketplace, events, communications, and OCP Academy to extend that value.

**Overall duration: 26 minutes**

**Module 1: Earn Trust Before You Amplify — 12 minutes.**\
Build credibility through listening, participation, contribution, and collaboration. Distinguish open Community participation from membership and formal program eligibility, and turn consistent service into trust.

**Module 2: Turn Useful Work into Reach — 14 minutes.**\
Choose OCP channels that extend the value of credible Community work. Explore the Marketplace, Summits, Tech Days, communications, webinars, and OCP Academy, then match a useful contribution to its audience and purpose.
