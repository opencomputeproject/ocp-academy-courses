# pronunciation: F C S A; input output; nonvolatile memory; chiplet root of trust for attestation.

A topology becomes meaningful when its boxes have architectural responsibilities. This module compares Compute and Hub with Compute Tile, then classifies the chiplets that can extend either system. We will use the accelerator case to show why the word accelerator is insufficient: coherent caching, address translation, and mandatory interfaces determine the useful F C S A classification.
