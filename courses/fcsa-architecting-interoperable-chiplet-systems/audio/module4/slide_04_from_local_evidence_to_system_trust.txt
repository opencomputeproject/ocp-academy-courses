# pronunciation: F C S A; input output; nonvolatile memory; chiplet root of trust for attestation.

A chiplet can report its identity and security state without deciding whether the whole system should trust the composition. Its chiplet root of trust for attestation, or C rot A, supplies local evidence and retains local security responsibilities. The System Root of Trust appraises that evidence against trusted references, the intended composition, and system policy. It checks freshness and the participating chiplets' roles and topology before deciding whether the Security Platform can form or continue. Optional Secondary Chiplet Roots of Trust can provide additional services. They do not make the system-level composition decision. Now change a participating chiplet's debug state. The earlier evidence describes an earlier state. Revalidation is required, just as after a security-relevant update, reset, or recovery. Keep evidence, policy, and response connected throughout operation. A valid identity alone is not a maintained system trust decision.
