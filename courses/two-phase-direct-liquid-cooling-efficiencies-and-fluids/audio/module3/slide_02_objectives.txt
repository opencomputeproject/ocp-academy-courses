# pronunciation: ODP -> "O D P"; GWP -> "G W P"; SDS -> "S D S"; PPE -> "P P E"; ASTM D471 -> "A S T M D four seven one"; AHRI -> "AH-ree"
By the end of this module, you will be able to evaluate the fluid side of a two-phase deployment with more confidence.

You will identify the current OCP Base Specification requirements for boiling behavior, latent heat, density, safety classification, environmental profile, operating temperature, and storage temperature.

You will also distinguish those requirements from the detailed fluid-use guideline. In particular, you will understand why the Base Specification range of minus fifty degrees C to sixty degrees C and the guideline range of minus twenty-six point three degrees C to sixty degrees C serve different document roles.

You will explain why compatibility depends on the exact fluid and wetted-material list. You will connect A S T M D four seven one testing, simulated aging, chemical analysis, environmental validation, and AHRI purity evidence to the detailed Soxhlet, component, and monitored-system path.

Finally, you will connect the S D S, P P E, environmental criteria, annual purity monitoring, and lifecycle records to responsible operation.
