Module three turns the fluid into an engineering decision.

The OCP Base Specification supplies the current compliance baseline. The detailed fluid-use guideline adds selection, qualification, and operating practices.

Keep the boiling-point ranges in context. The Base Specification uses minus fifty degrees C to sixty degrees C at one atmosphere. The detailed guideline uses the corrected minus twenty-six point three degrees C to sixty degrees C selection range.

The baseline also includes latent heat, density, thermal stability, Class A toxicity, Class one or two L flammability, G W P below seven hundred, and defined operating and storage windows.

Compatibility follows the actual wetted-material list. Evidence now spans A S T M D four seven one testing, simulated aging and chemical analysis, environmental validation, AHRI purity checks, Soxhlet screening, component tests, and monitored system proof.

Safety and lifecycle controls remain practical. Review the S D S, use the correct P P E, keep fluid paths clean, control changes, and retain the records.

The theme is evidence. The exact fluid, materials, operating conditions, and lifecycle plan must fit together.
