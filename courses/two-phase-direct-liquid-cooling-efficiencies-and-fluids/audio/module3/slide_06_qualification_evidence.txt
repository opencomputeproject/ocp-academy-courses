# pronunciation: ASTM D471 -> "A S T M D four seven one"; GC-MS -> "G C mass spectrometry"; AHRI -> "AH-ree"; Soxhlet -> "Socks-let"; QD -> "Q D"
The two OCP documents provide complementary qualification paths.

The Base Specification begins with prolonged material-compatibility testing under A S T M D four seven one. It then requires simulated fluid aging for one thousand hours at sixty degrees C, followed by G C mass spectrometry. An accelerated alternative may use one hundred degrees C or higher for seven or fourteen days.

The same compliance path includes oxidation and flammability validation, plus fluid-purity verification according to AHRI Standard seven hundred, twenty twenty-four edition.

The detailed fluid-use guideline adds system-specific engineering evidence. Its seventy-two-hour Soxhlet screen measures extraction and absorption behavior at the fluid boiling point. For seals, weight, stiffness, and volume should remain within the stated change limit.

Positive material results then move into component testing for pumps, Q Ds, cold plates, valves, and other hardware. The path continues through full-system checks and a two-month monitored functional test.

Neither lane replaces the other. The Base Specification establishes the current compliance tests. The detailed guideline shows how to carry compatibility evidence into components and operating systems.

The durable rule is simple: qualification belongs to the exact fluid, exact wetted-material list, and intended operating conditions. When one of those changes, revisit the evidence.
