# pronunciation: SDS -> "S D S"; PPE -> "P P E"
Fluid safety starts before anyone opens a container.

Review the Safety Data Sheet, or S D S, for the selected fluid. It defines the hazards, handling precautions, exposure response, first-aid measures, storage requirements, transport information, and disposal guidance for that material.

Personnel should use the required personal protective equipment, or P P E. Depending on the fluid and task, that can include eye protection, impervious gloves, and protective clothing.

The Base Specification also defines two temperature windows that must not be confused. The fluid operating range is four degrees C to seventy-five degrees C. The storage range is minus twenty degrees C to sixty degrees C. Those are fluid limits, not the room-ambient commissioning check that appears later in the course.

Cleanliness remains a reliability control. Keep containers and fluid paths sealed, keep tools clean, and protect opened components from moisture and particles.

Transport, reuse, refurbishment, recovery, and disposal must follow the selected fluid's documentation, supplier guidance, and applicable requirements.

The fluid may be designed for data-center use, but safe operation still depends on disciplined people, controlled materials, and traceable decisions.
