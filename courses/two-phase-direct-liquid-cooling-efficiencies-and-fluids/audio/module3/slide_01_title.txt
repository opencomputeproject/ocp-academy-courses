Module three: Dielectric Fluids, Selection, and Compatibility.

Two-phase D L C depends on the working fluid. The fluid determines boiling behavior, safety classification, environmental profile, material compatibility, handling rules, monitoring needs, and lifecycle options.

This module now uses two OCP documents together. The Dielectric Coolant Fluid Base Specification, version one point zero, establishes the current compliance baseline. The detailed fluid-use guideline adds selection, qualification, and operating practices.

We will keep those roles distinct while evaluating the fluid as part of a complete engineered system.
