**Title**\
Two-Phase Direct Liquid Cooling Efficiencies and Fluids

**Short Description**\
Explore phase-change cooling efficiency, dielectric-fluid selection, compatibility, deployment, and lifecycle operations.

**Long Description**

Explore two-phase direct liquid cooling from phase-change heat removal to dielectric-fluid stewardship. Trace loop architecture, pressure control, and efficiency boundaries, then connect fluid selection and material compatibility to deployment, monitoring, maintenance, and lifecycle decisions. Knowledge checks help learners assess the engineering and operational evidence needed for high-density infrastructure.

**Overall duration: 60 minutes**

**Module 1: Why Two-Phase DLC Now — 12 minutes.**\
Connect AI cooling constraints to heat-removal choices and efficiency measures. Locate two-phase DLC within the nested cooling loops and identify conditions that can trigger adoption.

**Module 2: Two-Phase DLC Architecture and Efficiency Mechanics — 17 minutes.**\
Compare sensible-heat and latent-heat mechanisms, trace liquid feed and vapor return, and relate pressure control to boiling temperature. Identify facility and loop interfaces that constrain scale.

**Module 3: Dielectric Fluids, Selection, and Compatibility — 16 minutes.**\
Review dielectric-fluid properties, wetted-material compatibility, qualification, safety, handling, purity, and environmental considerations. Distinguish base specification requirements from detailed fluid-use guidance.

**Module 4: Deployment, Operations, Monitoring, and Lifecycle — 15 minutes.**\
Connect startup checks, filling, purging, gas management, and routine monitoring to maintenance, leak response, deployment patterns, and lifecycle records.
