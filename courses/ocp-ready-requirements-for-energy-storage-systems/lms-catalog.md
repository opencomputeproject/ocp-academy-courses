**Title**\
OCP Ready™ Requirements for Energy Storage Systems

**Short Description**\
Connect ESS deployment choices, safety hazards, operating practices, and standards to OCP Ready™ requirements.

**Long Description**

Explore energy storage system requirements for data centers through deployment models, technology choices, safety hazards, operating practices, and compliance. Connect facility and rack-level decisions to stakeholder responsibilities, local authorities, insurer guidance, and international standards. Use the OCP Ready™ ESS parameters as an early planning framework for identifying evidence and coordination needs.

**Overall duration: 45 minutes**

**Module 1: Why ESS Requirements Matter In AI Data Centers — 8 minutes.**\
Explain the shift toward distributed energy storage and why AI changes deployment decisions. Identify the course boundary, stakeholder roles, and early planning questions.

**Module 2: Deployment Models, Architectures, And Technologies — 12 minutes.**\
Compare rack, row, facility, microgrid, and edge deployment models. Connect placement, coupling, control, grid interaction, and storage technology to safety and compliance review.

**Module 3: Safety Hazards, Mitigation, And Operations — 14 minutes.**\
Recognize electrical, thermal, gas, explosion, chemical, and propagation hazards. Connect them to separation, detection, ventilation, suppression, trained personnel, and emergency-response practices.

**Module 4: Codes, Standards, And OCP Ready™ Application — 11 minutes.**\
Navigate North American and international standards alongside local authority and insurer requirements. Apply OCP Ready™ ESS parameters to a coordinated planning and evidence checklist.
