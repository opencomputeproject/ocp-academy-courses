**Title**\
OCP Solid State Transformers: From Medium Voltage to 800 VDC

**Short Description**\
Explore medium-voltage conversion to 800 VDC, OCP SST interfaces, grid response, and integration into LVDC facilities.

**Long Description**

Explore solid-state transformers as a medium-voltage power-conversion interface for high-density data centers. Connect representative SST technology to the OCP v0.3 design envelope, grid disturbance behavior, operating states, energy storage, and LVDC integration. Distinguish requirements, illustrative architectures, and open work while building a practical framework for system verification.

**Overall duration: 80 minutes**

**Module 1: Why SSTs, Why Now — 16 minutes.**\
Connect AI power density and the 800 VDC transition to upstream conversion choices. Position SSTs within the wider facility-to-rack power architecture and OCP ecosystem.

**Module 2: The Technology Inside the SST Black Box — 16 minutes.**\
Compare conventional conversion with an SST, identify product components, and trace a representative isolated power path. Examine semiconductor and magnetic tradeoffs without treating one topology as mandatory.

**Module 3: The OCP SST v0.3 Design Envelope — 16 minutes.**\
Interpret input and output ratings, efficiency, physical and environmental interfaces, auxiliary power, communications, and compliance. Separate fixed requirements from recommendations and unresolved entries.

**Module 4: Grid Interaction, Ride-Through, and State Behavior — 16 minutes.**\
Review grounding, voltage and frequency ride-through, recovery, storage discharge and recharge, and operating states. Apply the state model to coordinated disturbance-response scenarios.

**Module 5: Integrating SSTs into LVDC Data Centers — 16 minutes.**\
Compare redundancy concepts, DC protection, insulation monitoring, and storage placement. Assign responsibilities across timescales and assemble verification evidence for an integrated LVDC system.
