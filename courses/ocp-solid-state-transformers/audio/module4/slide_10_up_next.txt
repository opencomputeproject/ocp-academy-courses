You can now predict the product response during normal and disturbed operation. Module five moves outward to the data center system. We will compare the non-normative redundancy patterns in Appendix A, connect output grounding to insulation monitoring and DC protection, map SST and ESS functions across response timescales, examine how storage placement changes the system boundary, and turn the draft into a verification and collaboration roadmap.

The last module will deliberately cross the black-box boundary. That does not weaken the product specification; it shows where product capabilities must connect to application evidence. A protection device cannot be selected without source-current behavior. A redundancy diagram cannot guarantee availability without capacity and clearing analysis. An ESS cannot promise ride-through without placement, state-of-charge, controls, and recharge policy. System integration turns each of those interfaces into a coordinated, testable scenario.
