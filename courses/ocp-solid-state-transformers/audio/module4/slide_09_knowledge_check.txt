Apply the behavior model to a disturbance. The first question asks what happens in the severe low-voltage region. The second asks which statements correctly describe states and recovery. Look for the difference between a source-of-energy change and a state-machine transition.

In the severe low-voltage question, reject answers that increase SST input current or force a fault transition. The stated response is zero input current with full ESS support, followed by immediate recovery when voltage and frequency return. In the multi-select question, recognize precharge and warning state definitions, and include controlled recharge as part of recovery. The false statement treats every disturbance as a fault, which would defeat the purpose of ride-through.
