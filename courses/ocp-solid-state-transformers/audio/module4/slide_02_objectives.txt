By the end of this module, you should be able to explain the upstream grounding concept, distinguish acceptable, overvoltage, and two low-voltage ride-through regions, interpret the frequency ride-through bands, connect input limits to ESS support and recharge, and identify all six SST states. You should also be able to walk a simple startup, shutdown, warning, or fault scenario and say whether the event should change state or merely change the source of energy supporting the DC bus.

Scenario thinking is the best way to learn the module. Start with normal S one operation. Apply a voltage sag or frequency excursion. Determine whether the SST continues, limits current, stops drawing input, or may trip. Identify how the ESS supplies any missing power. Then restore source quality and determine whether the state changes. Finally, include recharge and any utility ramp constraint. A complete response has an electrical waveform, an energy balance, a state, an alarm, and a recovery path.
