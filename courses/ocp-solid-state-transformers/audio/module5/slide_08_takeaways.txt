Deployment is a system exercise. Appendix A offers non-normative patterns for thinking about spare capacity and fault domains. Grounding, insulation monitoring, converter-limited fault current, and downstream protection must be coordinated. Capacitance, SST controls, and ESS each serve different response timescales. Storage placement changes electrical, operational, fire-safety, maintenance, charging, and approval boundaries. Finally, version zero point three should drive a structured verification plan and community feedback loop—not be mistaken for a complete site design. The best deployment proves the converter and the system around it.

The system boundary can be summarized as five coordinated contracts: grid to SST, SST to DC distribution, distribution to rack, SST to ESS controls, and equipment to operations. Each contract needs ratings, normal behavior, abnormal behavior, telemetry, isolation, and verification. The Diablo 400 course expands the distribution-to-rack contract. The ESS course expands storage and approval. The LVDC white paper expands facility grounding and protection. This course connects them at the medium-voltage converter.
