Use the final knowledge check as a compact design audit. The first question asks how to treat the Appendix A diagrams. The second asks which system-level items must be validated beyond the converter nameplate. Think across product, storage, distribution, protection, controls, and operations.

For the first question, the word non-normative is decisive. Appendix A helps teams compare ideas, but it does not replace a site design or mandate a topology. For the multi-select question, include every coordinated domain: grounding and protection, storage discharge and recharge, communications and states, and auxiliary behavior. Reject the answer that reduces validation to a nameplate. The central lesson of the module is that safe availability emerges from tested interactions, not one component rating.
