Module three is the first direct specification deep dive. The goal is to make the version zero point three tables usable: what voltage and power classes are defined, what the eight-hundred-volt interface must do, what environmental and physical envelope is expected, and which items remain recommendations or TBDs. Read every number with its qualifier. A draft can be precise in one row and intentionally open in the next. Good engineering begins by preserving that distinction.

This module also demonstrates how to work with an emerging specification responsibly. Version zero point three is sufficiently detailed to create common product discussions, yet it is not version one point zero. Procurement language, test plans, and design assumptions should point to the exact revision and record agreed clarifications. Where a row appears internally unusual—such as the frequency entry—raise a question rather than silently correcting the source. The course will call out those cases so learners can recognize draft interpretation as an engineering activity.
