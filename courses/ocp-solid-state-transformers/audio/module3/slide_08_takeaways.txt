The v0.3 product contract is now visible. SKU A is thirteen point eight kilovolts at five megawatts. SKU B is thirty-four point five kilovolts at five or ten megawatts. Both feed a regulated unipolar eight-hundred-volt DC interface. The document combines precise voltage-quality and efficiency targets with recommended fault behavior and explicit TBDs. Physical, environmental, auxiliary-power, communications, and compliance requirements are part of the product—not afterthoughts. Preserve every qualifier when moving a table entry into a requirement, test, or procurement document.

One final reading habit is to separate continuous capability from short-duration behavior. Nominal power, full-load efficiency, and acceptable frequency operation may be continuous. Overload, ride-through, and fault-current contribution have explicit or pending durations. Physical and environmental ratings may derate outside stated conditions. A product can satisfy each individual number yet fail the intended application if the durations and sequence are not coordinated. Requirements should therefore be tested as scenarios as well as isolated table rows.
