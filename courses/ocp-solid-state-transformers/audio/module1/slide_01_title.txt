Welcome to OCP Solid State Transformers: From Medium Voltage to 800 Volts DC. This course follows power conversion upstream—from rack-level high-voltage DC distribution to a medium-voltage-connected solid state transformer. Across five modules, we will connect the market pressure, the conversion technology, the exact OCP SST version zero point three design envelope, grid behavior, and system integration. The SST specification is the authority for product requirements. Analyst forecasts appear only as attributed context. By the end, you should be able to read the draft specification critically and place an SST inside a coordinated low-voltage DC data center architecture.

Think of the course as a boundary map. On the left is the utility or campus medium-voltage system. In the center is an active converter with isolation, firmware, cooling, and protection. On the right is a low-voltage DC distribution system serving extremely dense compute. The architecture can also include energy storage at several possible locations. We will keep asking the same three questions: what does the SST product promise, what must the surrounding system provide, and which claims come from a draft OCP requirement versus secondary market analysis? That discipline makes the material useful for operators, suppliers, integrators, and platform teams alike.
