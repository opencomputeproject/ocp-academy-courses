Three Academy courses cover different system boundaries. Diablo 400 is the rack-level course: it explains the power sidecar, plus-or-minus four-hundred-volt distribution, rack interfaces, safety, and adoption. The OCP Ready Requirements for Energy Storage Systems course goes deeper on storage placement, technology families, charging as additive site load, fire and electrical hazards, standards, testing, and OCP Ready assessment. This SST course starts at the medium-voltage boundary and follows conversion to a unipolar eight-hundred-volt DC output, including grid ride-through and coordinated storage behavior. These courses are companions, not prerequisites. Use each to zoom into the boundary you own.

Use the companion links as a learning handoff. If your next decision concerns cable assemblies, rack connectors, sidecar redundancy, or plus-or-minus four hundred volts, continue with Diablo 400. If the decision concerns battery chemistry, flywheels, facility versus row placement, fire hazards, charging policy, testing, or OCP Ready evidence, continue with the ESS course. Return to this course when the decision concerns thirteen point eight or thirty-four point five kilovolt input, five- or ten-megawatt conversion, eight-hundred-volt output quality, grid ride-through, or the SST state machine. Real projects cross all three boundaries, so teams should share assumptions and interface tests.
