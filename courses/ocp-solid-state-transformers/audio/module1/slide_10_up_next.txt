You now have the architectural context. In Module two, we will compare the conventional transformer and rectifier chain with an SST, map the five OCP product components, follow a representative three-stage conversion path, and examine why wide-bandgap devices and high-frequency magnetics enable the concept. The goal is a useful technology mental model without turning one illustrative topology into a specification requirement.

As you enter the next module, do not worry about memorizing semiconductor circuits. The learning objective is functional literacy. You should be able to look at a supplier block diagram and ask where isolation occurs, how the DC link is managed, what controls the input current, what regulates the output, which auxiliary source supports each control function, and which behavior is externally testable. That vocabulary will make the specification tables and system-integration discussions much more concrete.
