The course is organized as a progression. Module one establishes why the power chain is changing. Module two opens the technology black box without prescribing a vendor topology. Modules three and four are the specification centerpiece: ratings, interfaces, ride-through, and operating states. Module five moves beyond the product boundary into redundancy, protection, storage, and validation. Plan on roughly eighty minutes including the knowledge checks. Two companion Academy courses are linked where they add depth: Diablo 400 for the rack-level power sidecar, and OCP Ready Requirements for Energy Storage Systems for storage deployment, hazards, and compliance.

Each module contains an objectives or overview slide, five teaching slides, a summary, and a two-question knowledge check. The checks are formative: you must submit once to advance, but a perfect score is not required and retry remains available. Numerical requirements are traced to source pages in the editable course data. Terms that may be unfamiliar appear as glossary chips on their first substantive use within a module. Figures can be enlarged, and the silent animations can be paused. This structure lets a learner move quickly through familiar material while still supporting a careful engineering read of the draft.
