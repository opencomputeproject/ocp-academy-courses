# Module 3: Inside the Rack

In this module, you will delve into the essential components of data center infrastructure, focusing on the intricacies of server racks and their configurations. You will explore various rack standards, learn about the physical arrangements of IT equipment, and understand how these elements work together to optimize performance and security within a data center environment. By the end of this module, you will have a comprehensive understanding of how to effectively manage and organize rack systems to support diverse workloads.
