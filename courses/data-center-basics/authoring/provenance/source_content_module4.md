# Module 4: Management and Trends

In this module, you will explore the essential aspects of data center management and the latest trends shaping the industry. You will learn to describe common ownership and management models, distinguish between physical and virtual infrastructure, and recognize best practices for energy efficiency and sustainability. Additionally, you will identify key trends such as artificial intelligence, energy efficiency, and emerging technologies like quantum computing, all while applying your knowledge in a real-world interactive scenario.
