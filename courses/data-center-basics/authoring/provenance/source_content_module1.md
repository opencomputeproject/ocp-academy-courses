# Module 1: The Digital World

This course is designed for anyone new to the world of data centers, whether you're switching careers, fresh out of school, or simply exploring the field of data centers. It introduces the essential concepts of data centers and their role in the digital world. You will explore their function, layout, and architecture, and learn about key components and technologies shaping modern infrastructure. From power and cooling to networking and security, this foundational course offers a clear understanding of how data centers operate and why they matter more than ever.
