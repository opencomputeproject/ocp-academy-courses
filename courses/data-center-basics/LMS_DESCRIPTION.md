# LMS description and measured time

Data Centers: From Digital Services to Physical Infrastructure

Follow digital services through data-center power, cooling, racks and operations, then connect the basics to OCP.

Discover how everyday digital services depend on computing equipment, networks, electrical power, cooling and physical facilities. Four narrated modules introduce facility types, AI factories and workload placement, availability concepts, power and heat paths, rack DC busbars, rack dimensions and equipment, ownership responsibilities, and resource-efficiency measures. Ten instructional videos are complemented by diagrams, eight narration-timed teaching videos, thirteen knowledge checks with informative feedback, and a glossary. Relevant 2026 OCP publications connect these foundations to shared interfaces and chip-to-grid coordination, with a clear next step into the existing Intro to OCP course series.
