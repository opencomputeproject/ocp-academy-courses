**Title**\
OCP ESUN: Ethernet for Scale-Up Networks

**Short Description**\
Explore ESUN scale-up Ethernet, packet headers, fabric behavior, and endpoint and switch conformance requirements.

**Long Description**

Explore how OCP ESUN adapts Ethernet to accelerator scale-up networks. Position scale-up beside scale-out and front-end networking, interpret the ESUN packet header, and connect flow control, congestion handling, link retry, routing, and security to endpoint and switch requirements. The course supports technical review of the ESUN Base Specification Revision 1.0.

**Overall duration: 20 minutes**

**Module 1: OCP ESUN: Ethernet for Scale-Up Networks — 20 minutes.**\
Connect scale-up workload needs to ESUN's header and fabric behavior. Review endpoint and switch responsibilities, flow-control choices, reliability mechanisms, and conformance boundaries.
