**Title**\
Short-Reach Optical Interconnects for AI Scale-Up Fabrics

**Short Description**\
Evaluate short-reach optics for AI scale-up fabrics through reach, placement, reliability, diagnostics, and deployment tradeoffs.

**Long Description**

Evaluate Short-Reach Optical Interconnects for AI scale-up fabrics as accelerator systems extend across racks. Connect workload semantics, radix, copper reach, and optical-engine placement to power, latency, reliability, and repair constraints. Compare architecture choices and roadmap assumptions, then identify the technical and ecosystem evidence needed for deployment.

**Overall duration: 53 minutes**

**Module 1: Why Scale-Up Needs a New Optical Reach Class — 11 minutes.**\
Explain why scale-up communication needs a distinct optical reach class. Connect workload semantics, radix, pod size, and copper constraints to the SROI boundary.

**Module 2: Finding the Copper-to-Optics Crossover — 14 minutes.**\
Trace reach and loss budgets from package escape to adjacent racks. Compare retiming and optical placement against power, cost, latency, density, and reliability targets.

**Module 3: Engineering for Reliability at Pod Scale — 15 minutes.**\
Distinguish transient link errors from repair-required hardware failures. Connect temperature, laser strategy, diagnostics, redundancy, and placement to pod-level availability and service requirements.

**Module 4: Choosing an Optical Architecture and Roadmap — 13 minutes.**\
Compare optical integration, signaling, circuit switching, and near-term deployment choices. Evaluate roadmap boundaries and identify the coordinated ecosystem actions needed for adoption.
