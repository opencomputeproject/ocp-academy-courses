By the end of this module, you’ll be able to compare optical architectures with a whole-system decision model.

You’ll classify L P O, R T L R, N P O, C P O, and slow-and-wide options by power, serviceability, bandwidth density, reliability evidence, and ecosystem maturity.

You’ll explain the paper’s two-tier near-term path: use serviceable linear or half-retimed pluggables for initial multi-rack deployment, then move toward deeper integration as qualification data and open interfaces mature.

You’ll also examine O C S as a way to reconfigure the scale-up fabric at the fiber level.

For the signaling roadmap, you’ll explain why optics favors PAM four at four hundred gigabits per lane, while slow-and-wide W D M offers a different way to scale aggregate bandwidth.

Finally, you’ll identify the reach, qualification, interoperability, and protocol-alignment work that OCP, standards bodies, and suppliers must coordinate.
