Module four. Choosing an optical architecture and roadmap.

The SROI design space spans serviceable pluggables, N P O, C P O, slow-and-wide optics, and programmable optical switching. Each option moves power, density, reliability, repair, and supply-chain risk in a different direction. We’ll compare those tradeoffs, then examine optical signaling at four hundred gigabits per lane and the remaining role for the shortest copper tier. We’ll close with the ecosystem actions that turn system requirements into interoperable deployment.
