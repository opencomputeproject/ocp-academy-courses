The placement landscape begins with the system constraint a deployment values most.

L P O and half-retimed R T L R modules preserve front-panel replacement. The paper places linear pluggable optics around five to six picojoules per bit, and half-retimed approaches around eight to ten. These are directional optics-only values.

N P O moves the engine near the package. The electrical path becomes shorter, with power around five picojoules per bit in the paper’s comparison. Service can require board removal, but the balance of density, efficiency, and repair remains attractive.

C P O integrates the engine on the package or substrate. The paper assigns roughly four to five picojoules per bit and the strongest signal integrity. Repair and multi-sourcing become more difficult because the optical and compute assemblies are closely coupled.

Slow-and-wide optics uses many lower-rate lanes, sometimes combined through wavelengths. The optical portion may approach three picojoules per bit, but the reverse gearbox and connector ecosystem add their own cost.

Fully retimed pluggables remain serviceable and mature, but roughly thirteen to fifteen picojoules per bit makes them a poor match for massive scale-up lane populations.
