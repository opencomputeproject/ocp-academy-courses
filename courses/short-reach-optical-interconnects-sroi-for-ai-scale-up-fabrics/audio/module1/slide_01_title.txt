Module one. Why scale-up needs a new optical reach class.

AI compute is moving beyond one rack, but the scale-up fabric still has to make hundreds of accelerators behave like one tightly coupled system. That creates a reach problem copper can’t solve indefinitely. In this module, you’ll learn why workload semantics, raid-icks, lane rate, and Pod geometry define a new optical regime: S R O I, or short-reach optical interconnect. Narration starts automatically. Use the arrow keys or Space to advance.
