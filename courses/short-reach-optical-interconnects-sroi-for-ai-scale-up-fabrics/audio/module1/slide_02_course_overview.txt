# pronunciation: SROI -> "S R O I"; CPO -> "C P O"; NPO -> "N P O"; radix -> "raid-icks"
This course turns the S R O I whitepaper into four practical architecture decisions.

First, we’ll define the regime. You’ll connect scale-up traffic, raid-icks, and multi-rack geometry to the need for an optical layer up to thirty meters.

Second, we’ll locate the copper-to-optics crossover. That means looking at the full channel: packages, boards, connectors, cable routes, and retiming.

Third, we’ll treat reliability as a Pod-level property. We’ll separate transient link errors from hardware failures, then connect temperature, laser strategy, diagnostics, repair, and redundancy.

Finally, we’ll compare pluggable, N P O, C P O, slow-and-wide, and optical-switching approaches. The goal isn’t to crown one universal winner. It’s to give you a system model for choosing the right boundary and the right maturity level for a deployment.
