Module three. Engineering for reliability at Pod scale.

Scale-up replaces a small number of simple copper assemblies with a very large population of active optical elements. That multiplication changes the reliability problem. We’ll separate transient link errors from hardware failures, then follow the consequences into retries, fit targets, operating temperature, laser strategy, diagnostics, redundancy, qualification, and repair. The goal is not merely a reliable module. It is a Pod that remains useful when real components and links fail.
