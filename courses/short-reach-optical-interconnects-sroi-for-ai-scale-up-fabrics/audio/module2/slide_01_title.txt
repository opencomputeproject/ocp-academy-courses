Module two. Finding the copper-to-optics crossover.

The crossover is not a contest between one copper cable and one optical module. It is a whole-link calculation that includes package escape, board traces, connectors, cable routes, signal conditioning, cooling, and repair. We’ll trace that physical path, then compare retiming and optical-engine placement. By the end, you’ll be able to explain why the most efficient boundary can move from the faceplate toward N P O or C P O.
