# pronunciation: NPO -> "N P O"; CPO -> "C P O"; radix -> "raid-icks"
The copper-to-optics crossover is a boundary across the full system.

Passive copper remains the simplest and most efficient choice inside its natural reach. The challenge is that packages, boards, connectors, and cable assemblies spend one shared loss budget. As lane rate rises and the physical route grows, that budget disappears quickly.

Fly-over paths and active conditioning can extend copper, but they do not do so for free. Retiming adds power and latency, changes error behavior, and can increase the cooling burden.

Optics moves the boundary. Faceplate modules favor repair. N P O shortens the electrical path. C P O drives the path to its minimum and improves density, while increasing integration and repair scope.

The correct crossover must meet every whitepaper target at Pod scale. That includes power, cost, latency, reliability, raid-icks, and density.

In module three, we’ll examine how link errors, component failures, diagnostics, and repair strategies determine whether an optical scale-up Pod stays available during sustained operation.
