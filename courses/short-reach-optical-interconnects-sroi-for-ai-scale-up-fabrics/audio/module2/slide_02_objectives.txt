By the end of this module, you’ll be able to locate the architecture boundary rather than treating reach as one universal number.

You’ll trace the path from the chip package through a same-rack backplane and on to an adjacent rack. You’ll connect two hundred twelve point five gigabit PAM four signaling to the loss budget that path must share.

You’ll compare non-retimed, half-retimed, and fully retimed approaches, including what each one adds to power, latency, and repair flexibility.

You’ll also compare faceplate, connector-adjacent, N P O, and C P O placements as different answers to one question: how much lossy electrical distance can the system afford?

Finally, you’ll interpret the whitepaper’s power, cost, reliability, latency, raid-icks, and density targets as a system adoption bar. Those targets will carry directly into the reliability architecture in module three.
