# SROI course animation plan

These silent, looping motion graphics replace existing static figures at the points where sequence, timing, or changing physical boundaries are essential to the explanation. They use only concepts and claims already supported by the SROI white paper and the approved course narration.

| Course location | Animation | Learning purpose | Source anchors |
|---|---|---|---|
| Module 1, slide 3 | `scale_up_bottleneck.html` | Show parallel traffic flowing normally, one path falling behind, a queue forming, synchronized accelerators stalling, and the group recovering. | SROI white paper pp. 16–23, 33 |
| Module 2, slide 6 | `optical_placement.html` | Move the O/E boundary from a front-panel pluggable engine toward connector-adjacent, NPO, and CPO placement while the electrical channel shrinks and system tradeoffs change. | SROI white paper pp. 48–51 |
| Module 3, slide 5 | `retry_tax.html` | Follow a transfer through FEC and CRC, then contrast ACK with a NACK/retry loop and its visible pause in useful compute progress. | SROI white paper pp. 55–56, 60–61 |
| Module 4, slide 6 | `optical_circuit_switching.html` | Contrast O/E/O termination with a continuous optical circuit and animate an OCS route change from endpoint B to endpoint C. | SROI white paper pp. 34–38 |
| Module 4, slide 8 | `slow_wide_wdm.html` | Contrast one stressed high-rate lane with multiple slower NRZ lanes mapped to wavelengths and combined onto one fiber. | SROI white paper pp. 70–75, 80, 93–97 |

All outputs are 1280 × 720 H.264 MP4 files with no audio track. Course playback is configured as muted, looping, and autoplaying, with a static explanatory poster for reduced-motion and initial-load states.
