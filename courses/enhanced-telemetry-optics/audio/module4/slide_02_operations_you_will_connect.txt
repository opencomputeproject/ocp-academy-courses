By the end of this module, you will connect protocol behavior to repeatable operations.

First, you will distinguish the standard local C M I S firmware workflow from the remote eight thousand thirty hex command family. You will explain why downloading, running, and committing an image are separate steps.

Second, you will describe the host boundary. The host communicates with its local module over I squared C, while that module retrieves remote partner data in-band.

Third, you will identify the tool categories that operators need: register access, telemetry, event history, firmware maintenance, module time, hysteresis, and health rating.

Finally, you will connect module timestamps and host receiver measurements to the same evidence timeline. You will verify capability, partner identity, score configuration, shutdown defaults, and firmware results before relying on the deployment during an incident.

The goal is a dependable handoff from module measurements to host output, from host output to operator decisions, and from those decisions to documented action.
