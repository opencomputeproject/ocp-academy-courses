Deployment readiness is a behavioral test of the whole evidence path.

Discover the module's telemetry version, supported commands, and metric capabilities. Verify in-band support and lane establishment. In mixed deployments, a working data link with a third-party partner does not establish that remote enhanced telemetry is available.

Test local and remote queries, including the correct partner and datapath in breakout cases. Establish module time and check that events correlate with host measurements. Preserve history across reset, removal, and reinsertion.

Read back score masks and thresholds. Verify autonomous shutdown starts disabled after reset, and confirm the intended state after configuration. If it is enabled, test how fault and event evidence identify the response.

Exercise firmware discovery, transfer, completion, run, verification, commit, and abort handling. Keep running-image state separate from the default for future resets.

Finally, check that operators can retrieve this evidence through the actual host tools. A deployment is ready when the team can use the interface predictably before the first major incident.
