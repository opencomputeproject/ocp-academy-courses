Version one dot one dot zero adds a dedicated chapter on system operation. A sequence of operations explains what the system does in each state. It covers how the bus energizes, how it precharges before admitting loads, what isolates a fault, and when it is appropriate to restore service. It also makes responsibilities clear across converters, storage, rack controls, and cooling. Maintenance plans need a way to verify that stored energy has been discharged.

Another new chapter covers electromagnetic compatibility, or E M C. Converters share a D C network, so noise can travel across the bus and affect other equipment. Check emissions and immunity with representative loads and cabling, rather than relying on an isolated converter test to tell the whole story.

The white paper also distinguishes product standards, installation codes, and system verification. Work with the relevant authority on the actual installation and use risk assessment, tests, and modeling to establish the evidence. D C arc flash remains an area where the paper calls for more research and validation.
