Mount Diablo specifies a rack-level sidecar. The facility white paper uses three stages to describe how much of a data center might run on D C.

In stage one, D C supplies the I T racks and may also supply associated cooling. Other equipment stays on A C. In stage two, D C supplies a larger I T module, including its network equipment and cooling distribution units, while the building infrastructure still runs on A C. Stage three extends D C distribution to all facility loads. Treat the stages as architecture options, rather than a deployment schedule.

Version one dot one dot zero also looks inside the rack. It compares a power shelf that steps the incoming low voltage D C down to extra low voltage for the rack busbar, with a design that carries low voltage D C to the I T tray and converts it there. A facility layout alone doesn't tell you which rack design to choose. The paper's rack input voltage criteria are preliminary. Follow the linked companion course for more detail.
