By the end of this optional module, you'll be able to distinguish the Mount Diablo sidecar from facility-level low voltage D C distribution. You'll compare the three stages in the white paper and recognize two ways to bring low voltage D C into the rack.

You'll also see why matching a voltage label is only the start. Equipment ratings, operating limits, polarity, and earthing all affect compatibility. Finally, you'll know what to ask about coordinated protection, storage, operating sequences, and electromagnetic compatibility in a facility design review.
