Welcome to OCP Mount Diablo: Disaggregated Power for High-Density AI Racks.

This edition follows the final Mount Diablo Disaggregated Power Rack Specification, version one dot zero dot zero, effective October eighth, twenty twenty-six. Earlier project material called it Diablo four hundred.

You'll learn why high-density AI infrastructure needs a dedicated power rack, how its electrical path works, and which interfaces and protection requirements matter in an implementation review. We'll begin with the move from conventional in-rack power toward a sidecar beside the IT rack.
