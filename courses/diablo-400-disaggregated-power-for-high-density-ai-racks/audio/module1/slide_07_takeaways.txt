The key idea from this module is that Mount Diablo is a response to density.

AI racks are pushing power into hundreds of kilowatts and toward one megawatt class infrastructure. At that scale, current, copper, heat, and rack space become system-level constraints. Higher-voltage distribution reduces current for the same power.

The sidecar moves conversion, distribution, protection, and optional energy storage into a separate power rack. That frees the I T rack to focus on compute, networking, and cooling. Common OCP interfaces give suppliers a shared foundation to build around.
