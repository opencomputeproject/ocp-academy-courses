Think back through the Mount Diablo power path. The sidecar is a serviceable A C to D C power rack. Its input requirements cover redundant feeds, voltage, frequency, power factor, and total harmonic distortion.

Holdup bridges the first moments of an interruption. Ride-through covers defined input disturbances, and an optional battery or capacitor backup can provide additional support. On the output side, use the regulation and transient requirements for the selected configuration. Dynamic loading and end-to-end efficiency matter because an A I rack's demand can change rapidly.
