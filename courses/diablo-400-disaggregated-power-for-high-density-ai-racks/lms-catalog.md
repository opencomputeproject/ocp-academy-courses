**Title**\
OCP Diablo 400: Disaggregated Power for High-Density AI Racks

**Short Description**\
Explore Diablo 400 sidecar power, rack interfaces, protection, telemetry, and its connection to facility LVDC distribution.

**Long Description**

Examine how OCP Diablo 400 moves power conversion into a sidecar to support high-density AI racks. Trace the electrical architecture, review physical and management interfaces, and connect protection, grounding, safety, and qualification requirements to deployment decisions. An optional final module extends the discussion to facility-level low-voltage DC power distribution.

**Overall duration: 35 minutes**

The core path takes approximately 35 minutes. Optional Module 4 adds 15 minutes, for approximately 50 minutes including all modules. Estimates include narration, reading, and knowledge checks; external reference study adds time.

**Module 1: Why Diablo 400 Exists — 12 minutes.**\
Connect increasing AI rack density to current, copper, and conversion constraints. Explain the sidecar pattern and place Diablo 400 within OCP rack and power work.

**Module 2: Diablo 400 Architecture and Power Path — 12 minutes.**\
Trace AC input through sidecar building blocks to DC output. Review the physical envelope, power quality, ride-through, energy buffering, dynamic loads, and efficiency requirements.

**Module 3: Interfaces, Safety, Telemetry, and Adoption — 11 minutes.**\
Review distribution components, grounding choices, management and telemetry, environmental operation, and safety and compliance boundaries. Identify the evidence needed for an implementation review.

**Module 4: Optional: Future Work - Facility LVDC Power Distribution — 15 minutes.**\
Extend the rack-level view to facility LVDC architectures. Compare voltage and earthing choices, protection, conversion, energy storage, grid interaction, and open standards questions.
