**Title**\
Open Systems for AI

**Short Description**\
Connect AI clusters, physical infrastructure, IT building blocks and management through OCP open systems.

**Long Description**

This introductory six-module course explains how workload requirements, shared interfaces and cross-domain design shape deployable AI infrastructure. Learners trace cluster architecture, power and cooling paths, modular IT building blocks and management responsibilities, then apply the framework in a practical review.

**Overall duration: 99 minutes**

**Module 1: Introduction to OCP Open Systems for AI — 14 minutes.**\
Explain why open, coordinated design connects the workload to physical infrastructure, IT and management.

**Module 2: AI Cluster-Level Overview — 18 minutes.**\
Trace the component hierarchy and distinguish network domains, parallelism choices and cluster integration constraints.

**Module 3: Data Center Physical Infrastructure — 20 minutes.**\
Trace the power and heat paths and recognize facility, rack, cooling and service constraints without treating examples as design limits.

**Module 4: Data Center IT Infrastructure — 19 minutes.**\
Explain the roles and interface responsibilities of compute, switch, power and management building blocks, including the MGX 1.2 follow-on.

**Module 5: System Management — 16 minutes.**\
Explain management hierarchy, Redfish profiles, telemetry and controlled actions while recognizing implementation dependencies.

**Module 6: Putting Open Systems for AI into Practice — 12 minutes.**\
Apply the six-module framework to an integrated deployment review and select the right follow-on learning.
