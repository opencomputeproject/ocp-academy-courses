# pronunciation: MGX -> "M G X"; AI -> "A I"

Choose further learning according to the decision in front of you. Open Cluster Designs for A I is the next step for reading documented topology, equipment, cabling and deployment evidence. The facility course supports a detailed site-and-equipment compatibility review. The M G X course focuses on rack and tray interfaces, while Introduction to Liquid Cooling develops the cooling architectures and complete heat paths.

For A I beyond centralized hyperscale facilities, follow the A I Computing Continuum course and project. That work examines infrastructure across regional, enterprise, edge and user-adjacent environments. The scale and constraints can differ, while the need to coordinate equipment, site and connectivity remains.

You do not need to take every course at once. Start with the boundary you need to resolve, then return to the system view to check its effect on the rest of the deployment.
