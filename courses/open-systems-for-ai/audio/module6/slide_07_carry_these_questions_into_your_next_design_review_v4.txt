# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"

Carry four questions into your next design review. Which workload and operating conditions define the requirement? Which interfaces must agree? Can you trace the complete power, heat and communication paths? And how will the system be observed, controlled, serviced and recovered?

Keep the O C P tenets connected to evidence: efficiency, impact, openness, scalability and sustainability. The framework is useful when it helps a team expose a missing handoff and make a better-supported deployment decision.
