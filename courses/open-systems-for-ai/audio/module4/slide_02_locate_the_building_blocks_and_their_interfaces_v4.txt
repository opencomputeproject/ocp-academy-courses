# pronunciation: IT -> "I T"

By the end of this module, you should be able to explain the jobs of the main I T building blocks, distinguish their communication and memory boundaries, and identify the evidence needed for a multi-supplier implementation.

Keep our running example in mind: a team is changing an accelerator tray in a cluster design. The team needs more than a list of components. It needs agreement on mechanics, electrical delivery, heat removal, communication, management and the service process.
