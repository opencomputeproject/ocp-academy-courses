Return to the proposed accelerator-tray change. The supplier has named the platform family and provided an outline drawing. The integration review now needs the configuration-specific evidence.

Start with mechanics and service. Confirm the exact fit, alignment, handling and removal requirements. Check electrical delivery and the load conditions used for the design. Follow both the liquid and remaining air-cooling paths to their supporting systems.

Next, verify communication, memory and storage behavior for the workload. Confirm supported interface options, software versions and the evidence behind the expected performance. Include management capabilities, firmware update, fault reporting and recovery access.

Assign an owner to every unresolved handoff. A supplier can document a component capability while the integrator remains responsible for the combined configuration. Facility teams confirm site conditions, and operations teams confirm the service and response procedures. Close the review with evidence of the selected system's behavior, rather than a collection of unrelated product claims. That is how modular building blocks become an operable deployment.
