# pronunciation: IT -> "I T"

The I T building blocks have different jobs, but their interfaces connect them into one system. Compute trays execute the work. Switching connects resources. Memory and storage supply and retain data. Power and management support reliable operation.

Use shared specifications to organize those boundaries. Then request configuration-specific evidence for compatibility, performance, service and fault behavior. A modular design becomes useful when the selected parts can be integrated and operated together.
