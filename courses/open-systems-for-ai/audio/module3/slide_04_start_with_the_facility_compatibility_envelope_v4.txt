# pronunciation: AI -> "A I"

Begin a facility review before the rack reaches the row. Follow the equipment from delivery through unloading, movement, lifting and installation. Check doorways, turns, floor and structural loading, and any restrictions imposed by the handling plan. A cabinet that fits its final footprint may still be difficult to get there.

At the operating location, review utility connections, cable and pipe routes, maintenance clearances and how failed parts are removed. Include the equipment's electrical, thermal and environmental requirements. The facility team needs the actual configuration and operating assumptions, not only a nominal rack category.

Then consider service and change. Can a technician isolate equipment safely? Does a tray withdrawal interfere with hoses or cable reach? Can a cooling or power component be maintained without creating an unexpected limit elsewhere? These questions connect layout to availability.

The Open Data Centers for A I initiative addresses the facility side of these challenges. The Academy course on applying the specification provides the detailed follow-on. Here, the essential habit is to define a compatibility envelope: the conditions the site provides, the conditions the equipment needs, and the evidence showing that the two agree.
