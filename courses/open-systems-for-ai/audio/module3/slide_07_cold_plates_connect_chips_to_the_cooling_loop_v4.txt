Follow the heat from an operating chip. It crosses the thermal interface material into a cold plate, then transfers to the coolant moving through that plate. The thermal interface material helps bridge the physical contact between surfaces; the complete stack still has to meet its thermal and mechanical requirements.

The coolant path continues through hoses, connectors and manifolds. Those components must support the required flow, pressure, temperature and chemistry. Their arrangement also has to allow assembly, service and the intended response to a leak or connection problem.

Quick disconnects and blind-mate connections can improve serviceability. Blind mating means the connection engages as equipment is inserted, with alignment and engagement designed into the assembly. It still needs qualification. Confirm how tolerances, repeated service, seals and fault detection are handled in the selected implementation.

Keep the remaining heat path visible. Cold plates may remove heat from selected high-power components while memory, storage, power conversion or other equipment still depend partly on air cooling. A liquid-cooled tray therefore does not automatically remove the room's airflow responsibility. Trace both branches until their heat reaches the facility's rejection system.
