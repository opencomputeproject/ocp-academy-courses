# pronunciation: IT -> "I T"

Carry three ideas into the rest of the course. Workload requirements create connected demands across the infrastructure. Shared interfaces make collaboration and supplier choice more repeatable. And the selected configuration still needs evidence that its parts work together.

When you review a proposal, ask where a requirement enters, which boundaries it crosses, and who verifies each handoff. Keep operating and lifecycle assumptions visible. This system view will help you connect the cluster architecture to the physical, I T and management discussions that follow.
