# pronunciation: OCP -> "O C P"; AI -> quoted "AI" (user-requested M1S6 TTS input); IT -> "I T"

Open Systems for "AI" coordinates work across the O C P community. Its three work areas provide a useful map: physical infrastructure, I T infrastructure and systems management. A deployment crosses all three, so the initiative helps identify gaps and align work that cannot be solved within one discipline.

Two focused strategic initiatives help turn that broad coordination into practical designs. Open Data Centers for "AI" addresses the facility infrastructure needed to support these systems. Open Cluster Designs for "AI" develops documented pod-group and cluster designs, connecting equipment, topology and deployment requirements.

These efforts work with the established O C P projects. A rack interface, an interconnect or a management capability can belong to a specialist project while also supporting a larger "AI" deployment. The "AI" Clusters Project and its Summit track should not be confused with the separate Open Cluster Designs strategic initiative.

For this course, use the map to find the right conversation. A facility question, a component interface question and a complete cluster-design question may need different owners. The value of the umbrella is helping those owners coordinate around the same system requirements.
