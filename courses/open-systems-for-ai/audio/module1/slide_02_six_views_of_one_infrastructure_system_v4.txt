# pronunciation: 2025 -> "twenty twenty-five"; OCP -> "O C P"; AI -> "A I"; IT -> "I T"

This course follows a similar structure to the white paper authored in twenty twenty-five by the O C P Community within the Open Systems for A I Strategic Initiative. We begin with why open systems matter, then examine the cluster, physical infrastructure, I T equipment and systems management. The final module brings those views into a deployment review.

Since then, the course has continued to evolve with newer O C P contributions and developments. You do not need to be a specialist in every domain. Follow the relationships and handoffs. Allow about one hour and forty minutes for the course, including recordings, reading and questions. Linked resources offer optional depth.
