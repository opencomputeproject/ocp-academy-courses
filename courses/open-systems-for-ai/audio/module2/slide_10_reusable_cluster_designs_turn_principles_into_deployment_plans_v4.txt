# pronunciation: AI -> "A I"

A cluster drawing becomes more useful when it connects the topology to equipment and deployment evidence. Open Cluster Designs for A I develops that kind of documented system view. It brings together building blocks, connectivity and the infrastructure assumptions needed to interpret a design.

Open Pod Group and Open Cluster documents provide different levels of that architecture. The point for this introductory course is to recognize the hierarchy and know where the detailed design work belongs. You do not need to reproduce the topology calculations here.

The community's current-activities presentation also illustrates why publication status matters. A published baseline and a liquid-cooled design still being developed can both be valuable, but they support different decisions. An in-progress drawing is a direction for collaboration, not automatically an approved implementation requirement.

The companion course, Open Cluster Designs for A I, will examine the documents and deployment evidence in more detail. Use it when you need to move from recognizing the system boundaries to reading a specific architecture, preparing connectivity records or reviewing readiness. Keep those detailed tasks connected to the physical and management requirements.
