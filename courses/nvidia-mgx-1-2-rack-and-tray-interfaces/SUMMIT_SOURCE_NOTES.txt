FACTS AND LIMITS TO CARRY INTO AUTHORING

- Specification scope: MGX 1.2 p. 9 excludes power shelves and IO/power cabling.
  Its busbar is 48 VDC nominal, 40-59.5 VDC, with a conditioned 5000 A requirement
  (pp. 33-34). The Summit 800 VDC link is upstream of compute-rack DC/DC conversion.
- Clip rating: Summit p. 12 says 2036 A; the transcript says approximately 2000 A.
  Use the precise slide value only as an attributed component example. It does
  not replace the rating of the whole busbar assembly. The '+11% RMS heating'
  statement lacks enough stated comparison conditions for a general design rule.
- Connector rating: distinguish the speaker's 125 A continuous operating claim
  from 157 A connector/breaker labels. Do not turn the explanation into universal
  jurisdiction-specific installation or derating instructions.
- Cooling roadmap: the p. 10 'moving to' 100% liquid cooling language and p. 11
  'TBD' electrical/management labels must remain visible in their interpretation.
  These are not evidence of a released, dimensionally interoperable interface.
- Standards status: the p. 14 call to action asks the industry to evaluate
  connectors, block sizes, current shaping, and cooling. It does not establish
  that those proposals are adopted OCP requirements.
- Generation numbers: 660 kW / 60 seconds and 880 kW / 90 seconds are described
  configurations, not MGX 1.2's universal rack ratings. Keep generation, redundancy,
  BBU population, and voltage-conversion boundaries attached to any reuse.
- Transcript corrections: 'PowerShell' means power shelf; 'silicon' refers to
  silicone insulation on the slide; energy storage is 65 to 100 J/kW. The speaker
  corrects the power-floor/peak-limiting explanation at 08:52-09:01; use the
  corrected explanation and chart, not the earlier verbal reversal.
