The depicted fanless bay interfaces use M Q D zero-three quick disconnects together with the M G X input and output connector. The October sixth contribution records the M Q D companion specification as pending or in process. This course identifies the interfaces used within the M G X contribution; it does not assume an unpublished external requirement or teach an unverified companion revision.

Keep the thermal boundaries distinct as well. The fanless tray performs one hundred percent of its tray cooling through the liquid loop. Rack-level Table nine lists an eighty-five-percent liquid heat-capture ratio. Heat capture expresses how much heat is removed through the indicated liquid boundary. The tray and the complete rack are different boundaries.

That distinction prevents an incorrect system conclusion. One hundred percent liquid cooling within a selected tray does not convert the entire rack requirement to one hundred percent, and neither measure independently states facility energy efficiency. Record the connector family, location, revision, and thermal boundary before reviewing the operating conditions.
