Float is the controlled movement provided to accommodate that misalignment. It complements the rack, support, rail, and tray references; it does not remove the need to align those features. The assembled interface still needs the correct location and engagement state.

The source sectional views distinguish nominal overmate from allowed overdrive. Figure seventy-four identifies a nominal position at zero point five millimeters overmate and shows three millimeters allowed overdrive. They are not two descriptions of the same position.
