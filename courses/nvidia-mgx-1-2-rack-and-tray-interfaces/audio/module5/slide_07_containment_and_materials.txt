Liquid integrity includes the joint, the response to a leak, and the material exposed to the fluid. The busbar requires a base pan for containment. If it overflows, fluid should be directed toward the rack rear and away from the information technology equipment.

A leakage sensor is also required; its location and mechanism depend on the design. The hose joint needs its own compatibility review. Barbs must meet the selected hose specification under the required temperature and pressure conditions. Ear clamps are recommended with supplier-appropriate sizing and application.

The contribution states that worm-gear clamps should not be used. A clampless hose option needs supplier confirmation of geometry and testing. Preserve those differences in requirement strength. The wetted-material list identifies approved material families and an avoid list.

It requires peroxide-cured E P D M for hoses. Some lubricant alternatives depend on compatibility testing. The review therefore needs the actual fluid and actual exposed materials, not just a generic family name.

Aluminum on the wetted-material avoid list does not mean that every structural or chassis part must be aluminum free. Applicability depends on whether the material contacts the fluid.
