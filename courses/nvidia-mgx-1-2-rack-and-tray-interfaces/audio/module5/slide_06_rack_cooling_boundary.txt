Rack Table nine states a target sustained flow up to three hundred fifty liters per minute and maximum rack power dissipation of two hundred fifty kilowatts. It lists an eighty-five-percent rack liquid heat-capture ratio. Those values describe the indicated rack requirements, not the smaller busbar loop in Table four.

The differential-pressure entry is approximately ten pounds per square inch differential for a node at target flow, with one mated universal quick-disconnect pair. Preserve that node and connection boundary. Do not present it as the pressure drop of every component or the entire rack loop.

For a review exercise, separate the busbar, node, tray, and rack rows into their actual boundaries. Record the selected operating condition and connection configuration beside each requirement.
