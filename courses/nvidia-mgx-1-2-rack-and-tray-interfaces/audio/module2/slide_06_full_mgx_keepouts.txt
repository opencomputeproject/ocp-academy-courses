The full-width M G X board uses the available motherboard zone to provide a larger board envelope. It carries its own outline, required mounting locations, and maximum-height definitions. Do not derive those features by simply doubling the micro board dimensions. Use the full-width drawings.

As with the micro family, additional mounting holes may be introduced when needed. Mounting contact must remain within the designated ground-pad diameter. That rule keeps the fastening method connected to the intended mechanical and electrical contact region. Primary-side height options are shown for memory-module and non-memory-module layouts.

On the secondary side, the published component-height restrictions exclude supporting structures that may be required by the motherboard assembly. If a design adds a subpan or stiffener, it must add the necessary keep-in or keepout constraints for that hardware. Predefined zero-height regions still apply.

The supporting board pan is part of the overall assembly assumption used by the secondary-side height constraints. Review that pan together with board thickness, component height, mounting contact, and chassis location.

A board that meets a component-height figure can still produce an incompatible assembly if an added mechanical part occupies an uncontrolled region. For a substitution review, compare the primary and secondary zones separately. Identify the selected layout option and the mechanical structures added after board processing.
