The mechanical review connects requirements that are easy to separate accidentally. Begin with contribution scope and the revision boundary. Then identify the actual datum and tolerance that control each selected feature.

The nineteen-inch adaptation, optional extender, manifold supports, and cartridge structure serve different interface needs. Carry their relationships into the manufactured assembly. The rail and latch belong in the same alignment review as the rear connections. Their nominal locations and their supplier-specific capacities are different kinds of evidence.

Finally, connect stiffener placement to a defined cartridge implementation. Example mounting geometry is useful, but it is not a universal support rating. You should now be able to trace one tray-to-manifold reference chain and explain which drawing or requirement controls each step. That map becomes the starting point for reviewing a changed tray, rail, or support.
