Rack reinforcement is configurable because interconnect implementations do not all impose the same geometry or load. The cross-rack and front-to-back stiffeners can shift in one-rack-unit increments. Front-to-back stiffeners are two rack units high. Their spacing depends on the particular implementation and its loading requirements.

The contribution recommends cross-rack stiffeners in the spaces above and below the interconnect cartridge. These provide stiffness, mounting, and alignment features for the cartridge support brackets. Additional stiffeners, or closer spacing, may be appropriate for larger cartridges or higher loads. That is an implementation decision that needs evidence.
