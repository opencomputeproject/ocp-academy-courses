By the end of this module, you'll be able to interpret a connector-force diagram as a defined example case. You'll identify the tray type, mating or unmating condition, and the source units before performing arithmetic. You'll state any assumption that the listed forces act together.

You'll then connect the force locations to the mounting and reinforcement geometry. A calculated subtotal is useful, but it does not establish a support rating or a measured insertion force.

You'll also separate the evidence collected by manufacturing from the rack-level thermal, transport, and integration review. Finally, you'll assess a proposed fanless module, bay population, cartridge, grounding variant, and cooling boundary. The acceptance matrix records the requirement, source, implementation assumption, evidence, and unresolved item. That record turns a collection of familiar components into a reviewable engineering claim.
