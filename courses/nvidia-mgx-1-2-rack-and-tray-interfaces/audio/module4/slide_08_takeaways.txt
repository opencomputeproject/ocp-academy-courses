The busbar review connects capacity to its conditions. The five-thousand-ampere requirement belongs with the voltage range, ambient temperature, elevation, natural-convection condition, and temperature limits. It is not a universal rack-power rating.

Return bonding and chassis grounding are separate relationships. Draw the selected topology before interpreting the applicable test evidence. Keep the mechanical position, conductor materials, fitting extent, and connected hose space in the completed rack review.

Insulation and thermal transfer also have different roles. Verify the assembled compression and retention, then evaluate the relevant touch and clip temperatures. An example heat map does not predict every implementation.

Finally, acceptance records need the actual test conditions and configuration. Voltage drop, dielectric withstand, durability, and traceability evidence should identify the unit or variant to which they apply. You should now be able to compare the electrical evidence needed for the two return-bonding choices.
