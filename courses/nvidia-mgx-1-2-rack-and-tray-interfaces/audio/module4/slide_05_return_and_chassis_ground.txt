The contribution permits two busbar variants with different return-bonding arrangements. One isolates chassis and return. The other provides a hard tie between return and chassis. This allows different rack grounding schemes with limited hardware changes.

That choice is distinct from grounding the busbar chassis to the rack. The chassis or U-channel must connect electrically to the rack to maintain a common ground potential. The contribution specifies connections at both the top and bottom. The mounting interfaces use masking or serrated washers to establish the electrical contact, and the relevant rack contact region is masked to minimize impedance.

Do not describe an isolated-return variant as an ungrounded chassis. Isolation concerns the return-to-chassis relationship. The separate chassis-to-rack connection still applies. Likewise, selecting a bonded-return variant does not excuse an uncontrolled rack contact surface.
