The liquid-cooled busbar supports forty-eight volts direct current nominal, with a stated range of forty to fifty-nine point five volts, and current up to five thousand amperes. Preserve all three parts of that description. The nominal voltage is not the entire operating range, and the current number is not a universal rack-power label.

The electrical requirement states five thousand amperes at forty degrees Celsius ambient and three thousand fifty meters elevation, with natural convection only. The assembly must satisfy touch-temperature requirements and remain within the specified busbar clip's temperature rating. Those conditions belong beside the capability claim.

Both the power and return conductors must be electrically continuous through the assembly height to form a single power zone. A selected power shelf may have its own output voltage and compensation behavior. The contribution identifies typical nominal outputs, but directs the implementer to the actual power-shelf specification for the detailed range. Power shelves themselves remain outside the M G X contribution's scope.
