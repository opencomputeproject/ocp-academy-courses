Module four. Busbar electrical design and grounding. The five-thousand-ampere capability is meaningful only with its stated voltage and qualification conditions. We'll distinguish return bonding from chassis grounding, examine conductor and layered construction, and read the electrical test requirements. The goal is to connect a capacity claim with the correct temperature, interface, isolation, and acceptance evidence for the selected busbar implementation.
