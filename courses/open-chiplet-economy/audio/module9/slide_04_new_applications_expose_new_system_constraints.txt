The same modular approach can serve very different systems. Compute and memory designs may be dominated by bandwidth and thermal coupling. Optical chiplets add another boundary between electrical communication and optical transport. Systems that act in the physical world add deadlines and safety concerns.

The Summit discussions illustrate those different pressures. Do not assume that one interface or package is ideal for all of them. Start with the application's required behavior and trace it to the chiplet boundaries. In a robot or vehicle, for example, average throughput is insufficient if a late response can cause harm. The relevant contract must include the behavior that the application actually depends on.
