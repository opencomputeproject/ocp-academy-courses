Participation starts with a concrete problem. You might review a pad description, test an interface option, or explain an ambiguity in a startup sequence. Join the relevant project meetings and read the current work before proposing a change.

Then bring something that others can review: an implementation result, a model with stated assumptions, or a reproducible integration issue. The project discussion encourages both contributing to specifications and asking suppliers to implement them. Keep the feedback connected to the system outcome. A small, well-supported change can be more useful than a broad request for every component to become interchangeable at once.
