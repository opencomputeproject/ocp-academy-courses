Multiple interface choices can serve legitimate application needs. The challenge is that an integrator may have to bridge differences or qualify several combinations. Even within one standards family, version, profile, and optional features can affect compatibility.

The practical response is to make the supported contract precise and to demonstrate the combinations that work. Adapter cost, power, latency, and verification effort belong in the business case. Testing and packaging remain real barriers as well.
