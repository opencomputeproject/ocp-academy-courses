A useful product offer lets the integrator make a decision. The technical package should identify supported functions, interface options, physical constraints, and the models and tests that describe them. The commercial package should explain availability, support, and the permitted use of that information.

Responsibility needs to be equally clear. Who owns the package? Who investigates an intermittent failure? Who maintains the firmware and notifies customers of a change? These questions affect time to market and lifecycle cost. They are not administrative details to add after the architecture is fixed. A reusable chiplet becomes more valuable when the supplier can support its integration into more than one customer's system.
