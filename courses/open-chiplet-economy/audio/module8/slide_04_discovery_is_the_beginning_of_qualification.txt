# pronunciation: O C P; O C E; F C S A; P H Y; die; chiplet; Bunch of Wires; U C I Express; C D X M L; A D K; M D K; T D K; D F T; I triple E.

An open chiplet marketplace can connect a specialized supplier with an integrator that would not otherwise find that function. Discovery can reduce search effort and make the range of available capabilities more visible.

Qualification is a separate activity. The integrator must check whether the die meets the functional requirements, whether its interfaces and package constraints fit, and whether enough supporting information is available. Samples, models, test results, and lifecycle support may all be needed. A listing does not itself prove that arbitrary components are interchangeable or that an entire system has been validated. Treat the catalog as a route to evidence and a supplier relationship.
