Multi-vendor composition adds trust boundaries. The system needs to know which component is present, what firmware it is running, and what it is allowed to do. Update and recovery processes must work across the assembled product rather than stopping at one die.

Recent security discussions include roots of trust and the composition of security across chiplets. A security feature inside one component does not automatically secure the package. The system designer must identify assets, threats, and trust relationships. F C S A provides a more detailed place to study that system responsibility. For a marketplace decision, ask what evidence and lifecycle support are actually available.
