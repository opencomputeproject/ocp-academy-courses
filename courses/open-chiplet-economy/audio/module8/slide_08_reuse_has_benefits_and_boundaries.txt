A purchased chiplet can reduce the need to develop a function from scratch. Reuse can also lower uncertainty when the relevant behavior has been characterized in silicon. Those benefits depend on how closely the available part matches the intended product.

The same fixed implementation that enables reuse can constrain customization. A replacement from another supplier must satisfy the system contract, including physical, functional, software, and lifecycle requirements. Similar functionality is not enough. Evaluate the effort required to qualify an alternative before treating it as supply-chain protection. The strongest reuse plan combines a stable contract with enough evidence to support several intended integrations.
