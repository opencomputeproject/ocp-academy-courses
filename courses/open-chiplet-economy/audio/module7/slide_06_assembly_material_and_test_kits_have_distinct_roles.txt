The Assembly Design Kit, or A D K, describes assembly-related structure and rules, including geometries, layers, and tolerances. The Material Design Kit, or M D K, carries material properties and the conditions under which they apply. The Test Design Kit, or T D K, adds physical information needed for testing.

The kits have different roles. A thermal analysis needs suitable material data, while an assembly check needs the correct geometry and tolerances. A test plan needs to know which pads exist at the relevant stage. Keep their coordinate systems, units, revisions, and assumptions consistent. Standard structure reduces manual interpretation, but does not replace sign-off by the responsible teams.
