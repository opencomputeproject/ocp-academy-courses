A component can claim compliance with a specific interface and still require integration work. The other endpoint must implement compatible behavior. The package must meet the channel requirements. The complete system must then coordinate startup, power, errors, and software access.

Build the evidence chain explicitly. Record what was checked at the component level, what was tested between the selected pair, and what was validated in the complete package. Include the exact configuration and applicable limits. This is the move from a catalog claim to verifiable interoperability emphasized in the recent project discussion. A label helps find a candidate; evidence supports using it in a product.
