# pronunciation: 3DK = three D K; ADK = A D K; MDK = M D K; TDK = T D K; SI/PI = signal and power integrity.

Three-dimensional integrated circuit design kits, or three D K, organize the information needed from chiplet selection through design and sign-off. Different decisions use different parts of that information. Selecting chiplets needs component descriptions and assembly compatibility data. Package planning needs technology rules and material properties. Detailed design needs test connectivity and routing rules. Sign-off uses signal, power, thermal, and mechanical analysis. Assembly, Material, and Test Design Kits support different steps within this broader view. Ask which kit supplies the information for your current decision, and whether its conditions and revisions agree with the rest of the system.
