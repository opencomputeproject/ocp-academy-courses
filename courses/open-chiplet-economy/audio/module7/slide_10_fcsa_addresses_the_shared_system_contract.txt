Foundation Chiplet System Architecture, or F C S A, is one part of the broader interoperability effort. It defines shared system architecture expectations while leaving room for implementation choices. The published one point one edition includes security material and additional boot and implementation requirements.

This series establishes why those agreements are necessary. The companion F C S A course goes further into how the rules are organized and applied. Keep the distinction between a base architecture, an interface design, and a particular product. A product claim needs a stated scope and evidence. Following a physical interface specification alone does not establish compliance with every system-level rule.
