Different analyses require different evidence. A behavioral model helps explore function and traffic. A physical description provides dimensions and connection locations. Power and thermal models help predict whether the system can sustain its intended operating point. Signal and power integrity models address the actual electrical environment.

Test models and documentation explain how to validate and integrate the component. Do not replace one category with another. A bump map does not describe transaction behavior, and a functional simulation does not establish thermal margin. Agree on model quality, operating range, versions, and the relationship to measured silicon.
