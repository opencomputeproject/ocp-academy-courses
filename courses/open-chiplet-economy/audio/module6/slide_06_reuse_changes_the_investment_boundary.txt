A reusable die can serve more than one product or capacity point. That can spread its development investment and shorten the path to a new variant. Silicon-proven behavior can also reduce uncertainty compared with starting every function again.

The saving depends on a real reuse plan. A later product may need different bandwidth, power states, package geometry, or firmware behavior. If those requirements exceed the existing interface, the die may need redesign. Even an unchanged die needs validation in its new system context. Record which work is genuinely reused and which work remains. That makes the cost comparison more useful than a blanket percentage reduction.
