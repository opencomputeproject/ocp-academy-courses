An off-the-shelf chiplet can reduce development effort while limiting customization. Decide whether the supported options fit the product rather than assuming that silicon can be changed as easily as a licensed design block. Supply availability is equally important: the final package needs all required parts.

A second source is useful only if it is qualified for the actual system contract. Similar function or the same interface name does not make an immediate substitute. Include lifecycle support, change notifications, and integration assistance in the comparison. These commercial conditions can affect schedule and engineering effort even when the nominal die price looks attractive.
