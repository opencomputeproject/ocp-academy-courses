A simple business comparison divides the relevant engineering investment by the expected number of shipped products, then adds the recurring cost per good unit. This is an explanatory model, not a complete accounting method. It helps show why shipment volume matters.

At lower volume, avoiding a large development investment may dominate. At higher volume, small recurring differences can become more important. Explore a range rather than one optimistic point estimate. Package yield, test time, supplier pricing, and the number of reusable variants can move the result. If the preferred option changes under a small assumption change, the team needs better evidence before committing.
