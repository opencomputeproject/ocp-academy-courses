Non-recurring engineering, or N R E, is the investment required to create and qualify the product. It includes design, verification, masks, and integration work. Reusing a chiplet may avoid repeating some of that work, but does not eliminate package or system validation.

Recurring production costs include the good dies, package, assembly, and test. A purchased chiplet price may already include its supplier's engineering recovery and margin. Avoid counting those costs twice. Compare the alternatives over the same product lifetime and shipment volume. A design with lower initial investment can still have a higher unit cost, while a larger upfront investment can be worthwhile at sufficient volume.
