Moving components closer can make communication more efficient. A short package route can require less drive than a board-level path and can support a wider parallel connection. The package also sets constraints on pitch, routing loss, and placement.

The chiplet interface is not free. It occupies die edge and internal area, consumes power, and introduces clocking and control requirements. Compare the complete path rather than assigning one universal energy saving to all chiplets. Measure the interface under the intended load and package conditions. This is why architecture, packaging, and interface selection belong in the same discussion rather than three separate handoffs.
