Homogeneous disaggregation uses the same process node across chiplets. Heterogeneous disaggregation combines different nodes. These definitions help isolate the economic value of process choice.

They do not imply that every same-node die is identical, or that different nodes automatically create different functions. Repeated compute tiles are one example of a homogeneous design. A compute die and an input-output die made on different nodes are a heterogeneous example. In broader industry discussions, heterogeneous can also describe differences in function or technology.
