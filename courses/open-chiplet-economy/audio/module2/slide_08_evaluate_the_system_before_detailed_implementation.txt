Early system analysis can reveal a poor partition before detailed implementation locks it in. A workload drives activity. Activity changes power demand, and power delivery changes the voltage seen by the dies. Temperature can then change leakage and performance. These effects interact.

The Summit's virtual-prototyping and federated-simulation discussions explore how different suppliers can participate in that analysis.
