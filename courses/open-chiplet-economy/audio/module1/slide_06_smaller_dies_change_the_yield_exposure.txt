A manufacturing defect can make a die unusable. For a given defect environment, a larger die exposes more area to that risk. Splitting a large function into smaller dies can improve the yield of the individual pieces and allows defective pieces to be screened before assembly.

The final product still needs every required component and every required connection to work. Assembly yield, package cost, and test coverage can reduce or even eliminate the silicon saving. Treat improved die yield as one part of the calculation. Later in the series, we will compare the cost per shipped good system, including engineering effort and the number of products that can reuse a chiplet.
