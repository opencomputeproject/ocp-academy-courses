An open ecosystem allows different companies to specialize. One may supply a chiplet, another an interface, and another the packaging or test service. That specialization can widen the set of products an integrator can build.

Interchangeability is a stronger claim. Two chiplets can follow the same family of interface specifications yet differ in optional features, package requirements, or software behavior. The integrator must verify the specific combination. Ask what models are available, which versions are supported, and who owns an integration failure. The economic opportunity becomes more credible when technical evidence and business responsibilities are both clear.
