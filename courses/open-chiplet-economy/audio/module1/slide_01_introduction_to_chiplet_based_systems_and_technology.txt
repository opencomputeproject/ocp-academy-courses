Welcome to the Open Chiplet Economy series. A chiplet is useful because of what it enables in a system: a reusable function, a suitable manufacturing process, or a better route to a product. But a collection of good dies does not automatically make a good system.

We will connect the technology to the business decisions. We will also meet the community creating the interfaces, models, and system agreements needed for suppliers to work together.
