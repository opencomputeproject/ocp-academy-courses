Different functions scale differently. Dense digital logic may benefit from an advanced process, while analog or input-output functions can be better served by a mature or specialty process. Combining them can make more sense than forcing every function onto the same node.

Chiplets also provide a way around the size limit of a single lithographic exposure field, called the reticle limit. Several individually manufacturable dies can form a larger system. Reuse adds a separate benefit: a previously validated die may serve several product variants. Each benefit depends on a suitable boundary and compatible interfaces. A smaller die alone does not establish that reuse will be economical.
