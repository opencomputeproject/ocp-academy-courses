The Open Compute Project's Open Chiplet Economy project, or O C E, brings companies together around multi-vendor chiplet systems. Its work is broader than an electrical connection. Tools and flows help teams exchange design information. Ecosystem standards make expectations explicit. Open interfaces let components communicate.

Imagine buying a compute die from one supplier and an input-output die from another. Someone must still establish their package compatibility, test access, startup behavior, and commercial responsibilities. The project provides a place to develop those shared agreements. Participation can include reviewing a specification, supplying a model, or testing an implementation. No single contribution removes the integrator's responsibility for the complete product.
