We will follow a chiplet system from its business motivation to the evidence needed for integration. Start with the project and the architecture. Then examine packaging, die-to-die interfaces, testing, and cost. The final modules connect models and standards to a working marketplace. At the end, you will be ready to move into the Foundation Chiplet System Architecture course and investigate specific system rules.
