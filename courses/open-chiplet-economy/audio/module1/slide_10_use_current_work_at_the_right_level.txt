The ecosystem now includes several kinds of contribution. Foundation Chiplet System Architecture, or F C S A, describes shared system expectations. Bunch of Wires Flexi addresses a different problem: a configurable interface emphasizing low overhead and simplicity. Assembly, material, and test design kits support the exchange of integration data.

These contributions complement each other. They do not form one interchangeable checklist. We will name the relevant version and distinguish a published specification from a proposed direction. That distinction matters when a team commits to silicon. A useful presentation can motivate an investigation, but an implementation contract needs the actual applicable specification and evidence.
