Carry three ideas forward. A chiplet is part of a system, so its boundary needs a purpose. Potential benefits include suitable process choices, smaller-die yield, and reuse, but the package must preserve those benefits. Finally, open collaboration reduces the need to invent every agreement from scratch. Architecture is where these ideas become concrete: which functions should be separated, and which connections become expensive when they cross a boundary?
