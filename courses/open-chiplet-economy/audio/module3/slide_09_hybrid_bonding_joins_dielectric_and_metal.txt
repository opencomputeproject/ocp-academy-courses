Hybrid bonding combines a dielectric bond with direct metal connections. The surfaces must be prepared and accurately aligned. The dielectric surfaces bond, and thermal processing establishes the metal connection. Very fine connection pitch enables high signal density between the dies.

It does not make it the best choice for every product. Surface quality, alignment, thermal processing, yield, and test strategy all affect feasibility. A dense connection can be valuable for a tightly coupled stack, while a less demanding package may meet another product's requirements at lower cost.
