A redistribution layer, or R D L, moves connections from the die pads to a more useful arrangement. In fan-out packaging, that routing extends beyond the original die outline. Multiple dies can be incorporated into a reconstituted wafer or panel structure.

Fan-out on substrate uses dense local routing where it is needed, then attaches that structure to a larger substrate. Process order matters. In a chip-first flow, die placement precedes the redistribution processing. In a chip-last flow, the routing structure is prepared before die attachment. The flows have different yield, alignment, and known-good-component considerations. Compare them with the intended assembly and test plan.
