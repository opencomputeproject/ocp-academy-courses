A bridge creates a fine-pitch routing region between neighboring dies. It can be embedded in a substrate or combined with other package structures. The attachment tolerances and transitions between fine and coarse connections are part of its manufacturing challenge.

A silicon interposer provides an intermediate routing layer beneath the dies. Through-silicon vias can connect that layer vertically toward the package substrate. It offers dense routing over a broader region, but adds its own cost and process requirements. Neither approach removes the need for power delivery, thermal analysis, or test access. Select the structure by the required connections and the complete manufacturing flow.
