The package is an active part of the system design. It sets the routing environment, supports the dies, delivers power, and helps remove heat. We will compare the major packaging approaches and the interface requirements that drive their selection. The aim is to choose enough capability for the product, while accounting for assembly cost, test access, and manufacturing risk.
