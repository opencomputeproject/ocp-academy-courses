Design for testability, or D F T, adds structures that make faults easier to excite and observe. Boundary scan helps access connections. Built-in self-test generates and checks patterns internally. Automatic test-pattern generation creates patterns for a specified fault model.

Each technique has a purpose and a coverage limit. Adding a self-test block does not prove that every relevant failure is detectable. The package needs a way to reach the required test functions after assembly. Reusing die-level tests can reduce effort, but the access path, timing, and expected responses must still be valid in the composed system. Plan that retargeting rather than assuming it will work later.
