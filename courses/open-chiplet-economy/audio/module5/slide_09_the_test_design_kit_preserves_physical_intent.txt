The Test Design Kit, or T D K, addresses information that can be lost between planning, design, and manufacturing. A pad needs a clear physical description. The team also needs to distinguish the front and back of a die and understand which structures exist at each process stage.

Sacrificial pads are created for testing and are not part of the final product. A test plan that depends on them must account for when access is available and when it disappears. The contribution extends the chiplet data-exchange approach to carry such intent. It complements, rather than replaces, test patterns, access architecture, and agreed manufacturing responsibilities.
