Known good die, or K G D, is useful shorthand for a die that has passed a defined screening process. The phrase needs an attached test definition. Which faults are covered? At what operating conditions? What escapes remain possible?

Even well-screened dies can be damaged or connected incorrectly during assembly. Package testing must therefore address the connections and any assembly-induced faults. In a three-dimensional stack, access can become harder as layers are added. Agree on test coverage, result formats, and responsibilities before purchasing the components. A supplier's pass result is meaningful only when the integrator knows the limits behind it.
