Testing costs time and equipment capacity, while an escaped defect can waste an expensive assembly or fail in the field. The best flow balances those costs using expected defect rates and coverage. More testing is not automatically better if it duplicates a check without finding additional relevant faults.

Keep traceability across die, substrate, and assembly steps. When a package fails, the participants need enough evidence to diagnose whether the issue came from a component, an interconnect, or an operating condition. Agree on access to that evidence and on who performs the investigation. These responsibilities affect the business case as directly as the test equipment does.
