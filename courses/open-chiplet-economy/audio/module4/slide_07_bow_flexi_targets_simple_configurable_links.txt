Bunch of Wires Flexi is a published contribution aimed at communication where simplicity, efficiency, and cost are priorities. It uses single-data-rate signaling, a frame signal, and configurable data width. Its command set supports transfer and configuration without requiring a separate sideband for the basic setup.

This is a different design point from choosing the highest possible bandwidth. Optional features support different bus needs, so the two endpoints must agree on their configuration. Do not carry preliminary lane-count or frequency claims from older teaching material into an implementation. Use the published version one point zero specification for the supported behavior and electrical requirements.
