# pronunciation: O C P; O C E; F C S A; P H Y; die; chiplet; Bunch of Wires; U C I Express; C D X M L; A D K; M D K; T D K; D F T; I triple E.

Two chiplets must agree on more than the wires between them. They need compatible electrical behavior, a shared way to transfer information, and a shared meaning for that information. This module separates those responsibilities. It also shows how to compare interfaces without turning a peak bandwidth number or a standards name into a promise about the complete system.
