# pronunciation: O C P; O C E; F C S A; P H Y; die; chiplet; Bunch of Wires; U C I Express; C D X M L; A D K; M D K; T D K; D F T; I triple E.

The physical layer, often called the P H Y, defines the electrical connection. Pinout, voltages, timing, and the channel must be compatible. The link layer organizes transfer across that connection. Depending on the interface, it can include framing, error protection, and flow control.

The protocol gives the transferred information meaning. A request to read memory must be interpreted consistently by the sender and receiver. A protocol adapter can map an on-chip bus onto the die-to-die transport. A successful electrical link is therefore necessary but insufficient. The complete pair must agree on all applicable layers and options, including behavior during initialization and errors.
