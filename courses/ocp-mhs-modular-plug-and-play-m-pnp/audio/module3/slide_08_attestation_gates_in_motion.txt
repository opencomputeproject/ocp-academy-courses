The power-state mechanism lets the controller manage deliberate release points. Standby Hold-Off precedes Standby Run. S five Hold-Off precedes the S five operating state. The S zero sequence includes a SoC ramp and S zero Hold-Off before the system proceeds into Bios execution.

Verification at those points follows the platform security profile. Device power sequencing is implementation-specific, and the required checks depend on the applicable security requirements.

Run-Bios-Boot solves a different problem. Where no out-of-band connection exists, Bios progress and P C I E training can enable the in-band access needed for verification. After the applicable checks are complete, the platform can move to Run O S. The Hold-Off checkpoints serve a different purpose from the access-enabling Run-Bios-Boot state.
