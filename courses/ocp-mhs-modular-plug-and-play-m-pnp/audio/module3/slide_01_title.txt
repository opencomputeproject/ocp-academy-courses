Module three examines the H P M and Peripheral Programmable Device specification. A P D is a programmable device. An FPGA or C P L D can implement that role.

We'll separate the primary H P M device's required capabilities from optional blocks and peripheral use cases. Then we'll examine memory-map discovery, virtual wires, power-state coordination and the programmable-device JSON. The central question is what interface the management controller can depend on, not which vendor's logic implementation must be used.
