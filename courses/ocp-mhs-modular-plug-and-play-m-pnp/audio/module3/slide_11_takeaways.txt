The programmable-device specification gives firmware a common management surface while leaving implementation choices to the designer. The primary H P M and peripheral roles must remain distinct.

For the primary device, at least S G P I O or L T P I is required, together with a documented way to access the memory map. The catalog has nineteen entries, but the transport pair must not be counted as two independent mandatory capabilities. The directory can be optional under the stated JSON-addendum condition.

The power model has ten states and three named Hold-Offs. Required security checks follow the platform profile. The programmable-device JSON describes the implemented blocks, registers and mappings that make those behaviours discoverable. Keep every exception attached to the rule it qualifies.
