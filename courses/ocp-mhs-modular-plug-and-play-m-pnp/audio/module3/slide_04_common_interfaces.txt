The primary H P M programmable device must support at least S G P I O or L T P I. It may support both. That's an either-or requirement, not a requirement to implement both interfaces. L T P I is the Low Voltage Differential Signalling Tunneling Protocol and Interface; S G P I O is Serial General Purpose Input Output.

Memory-map access is another part of the contract. The implementor must provide random access through at least one illustrated interface, or through an interface documented in the self-describing JSON. The specification describes L T P I, S P I and I squared C or I cubed C mailbox approaches, but that list isn't exclusive.

Don't turn an example architecture into a fixed board design. The use of an S C M C P L D and the controls needed when multiple access paths exist are implementation-specific. Preserve the required behaviour while distinguishing it from the illustrated plumbing.
