The specification standardizes what the management controller can rely on, rather than prescribing one silicon implementation. The primary H P M P D exposes a management surface through IP blocks, control and status registers, a memory map, internal virtual wires and self-description. C S R means control and status register.

An FPGA is one way to implement that surface. A C P L D or another programmable device can also serve an applicable role. Vendor-specific sequencing logic and implementation details remain separate from the common contract.

The specification also covers peripheral programmable devices. Don't copy the primary H P M's mandatory block list onto every peripheral. The role and the applicable block requirements determine what a particular device needs. Vendor-defined blocks should follow the common register, virtual-wire and JSON guidance if they are to gain the framework's integration benefits.
