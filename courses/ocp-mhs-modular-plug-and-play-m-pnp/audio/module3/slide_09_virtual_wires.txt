A logical signal and the path carrying it are different things. The internal virtual-wire framework describes how a programmable device's signals connect to transports, memory-map interfaces or physical signals. S G P I O and L T P I are examples of external transports, not the names of the internal signal itself.

The current JSON keeps VirtualWires, Registers, PhysicalSignals and Buses as distinct structures. That separation lets firmware interpret the implementation instead of assuming a dedicated physical pin for every logical function.

Compare the primary H P M programmable-device example with the supplied peripheral C P L D example. The framework is shared, but their roles and implemented mappings differ. Don't infer that every signal in the host example must also exist in the peripheral.
