Table six lists nineteen common IP block entries. Seven rows carry a Y mark, and twelve carry an N mark. But the L T P I and S G P I O rows share the footnote requiring at least one, with both permitted. Don't count those as two independent mandatory capabilities.

Combining that pair into one group gives six required capability groups plus twelve optional entries, while the table has nineteen rows. The required groups cover power, reset, the internal virtual-wire framework, the chosen transport, M-PESTI, and flexible I O and platform sideband configuration.

The optional catalog includes Host Access, e S P I, M-PESTI Target, RAS and U S B alongside control and telemetry blocks. An optional block can have requirements when implemented. A Y mark also means at least some required functionality exists within the block, not that every feature is mandatory. Always read the relevant block section.
