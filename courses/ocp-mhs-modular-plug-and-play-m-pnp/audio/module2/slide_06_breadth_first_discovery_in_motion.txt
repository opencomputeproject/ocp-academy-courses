Discovery order only helps when its dependencies are available. If the B M C cannot retrieve the required local H P M JSON at this stage, the specification treats that as a discovery failure and requires the process to halt. Reporting and logging are recommended; remediation is vendor policy.

Optional extended H P M JSON has a different outcome. Failed retrieval is still a discovery failure and should be reported and logged, but the B M C may continue according to vendor policy. That doesn't make the missing data successful, and later steps that depend on an omitted optional result must be treated as failed.

The important distinction is the dependency. Missing required data stops the process; missing optional data allows only the continuation permitted by policy. A later step cannot rely on information the controller didn't obtain.
