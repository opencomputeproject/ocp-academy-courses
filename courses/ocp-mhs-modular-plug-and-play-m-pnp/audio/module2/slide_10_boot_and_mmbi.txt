Two boot states solve different access conditions. At S zero Hold-Off, devices can have main power while remaining in reset. Some verification can occur then. But a device without an out-of-band management connection may need P C I E training before the B M C can reach it in-band. S zero Run-Bios-Boot allows that additional progress. It is not a fourth named Hold-Off state.

The B M C and Bios must support the Memory-Mapped Buffer Interface, or M M B I. The specification also requires its M C T P transport binding and P L D M support for Redfish payloads and generalized file transfer.

The required payloads include Redfish inventory and the M-PNP JSON files collected during discovery, supplied on request. Inventory may be partial before the platform has booted once. The B M C acts as file host, representing the files with descriptor records, unique file names and identifiers, and a unique root directory.
