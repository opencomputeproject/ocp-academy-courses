Module two examines Fru, Discovery and Boot in version zero point seven. A Fru is a field replaceable unit, and the B M C is the management controller that retrieves its descriptive information.

We'll connect the data format to discovery order, power-state restrictions and failure handling. Then we'll follow the handoff to Bios. Pay particular attention to what the specification requires, what it makes optional, and which security actions depend on the platform's security profile. Those distinctions determine what firmware may do when data or a connection is unavailable.
