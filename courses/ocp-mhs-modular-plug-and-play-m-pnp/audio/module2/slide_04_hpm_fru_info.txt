Don't confuse a data format with a storage location. I P M I Fru records provide inventory and record structures. D M T F Fru files include descriptors that tell the B M C how to find the associated JSON. The Resident and Context fields help distinguish data in the information device from data elsewhere.

Local JSON contains attributes needed early in discovery. Extended attributes can be obtained through other locations or mechanisms. Both are JSON; local and extended describe their role and availability, not two competing formats.

The specification requires a method for cryptographically verifying Fru information authenticity. When a digital signature is supplied, it must cover all static data in the information device and be stored in a D M T F Fru file. The hash must be at least SHA three hundred eighty-four, the signature algorithm must be an industry standard, and the algorithm and hash size must be disclosed. Post-quantum signing support is recommended.
