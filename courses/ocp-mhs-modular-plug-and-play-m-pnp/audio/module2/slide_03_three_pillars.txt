The specification connects three responsibilities: Fru information, discovery, and boot. The H P M and peripherals expose identity and descriptive data. The B M C uses that data to expand its understanding from the S C M outward. Boot then proceeds with the needed platform context.

The important qualification is that discovery and boot are partially intertwined. Most discovery completes before system boot, but some devices need Bios execution and P C I E training before in-band access is possible.

Read the step qualifiers carefully. Required is the default when no applicability qualifier is present. An optional step can be omitted, but a later step depending on its result must then be treated as failed. A Security step becomes required when the platform security profile requires it. A step must also run only in its listed power states. These qualifiers determine which actions firmware may perform.
