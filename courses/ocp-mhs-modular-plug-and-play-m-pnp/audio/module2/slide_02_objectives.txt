By the end of this module, you'll be able to separate the Fru data layers from the locations where JSON is stored. You'll be able to read the process qualifiers, including a step's allowed power state and whether the platform security profile makes it required.

You'll trace discovery from the control module through the host module and peripherals, and identify when missing required data must halt the process. You'll also distinguish the preferred connectivity path from the different options for programmable-device self-description. Finally, you'll explain the defined boot-time transport and payload responsibilities between the B M C and Bios.
