Discovery is more than reading a file. It connects identity, location, topology, power state and policy. The B M C expands its understanding generally breadth-first, but it must have the required descriptions before taking dependent actions.

Missing required local H P M JSON halts discovery. Failed optional extended retrieval is still a failure, with continuation left to policy. Security actions depend on the platform profile, and the allowed power states constrain when each step may execute.

Finally, boot includes a defined data exchange. M M B I, its M C T P binding and P L D M support inventory and JSON transfer between the management controller and Bios. These distinctions keep a discovery implementation faithful to the specification when something doesn't follow the happy path.
