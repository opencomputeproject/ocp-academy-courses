The Interoperability specification is version zero point three point zero in the release package. Its fifteen pages define mechanical, electrical and logical interoperability and the three levels we introduced earlier.

M-PNP uses that cross-spec contract alongside the Fru, Discovery and Boot and programmable-device specifications. Interop uses FPGA terminology, while the P D specification covers programmable devices on both the H P M and its peripherals. Read the companion requirements with each device's role in mind.

The compliance checklist matters as much as the vision. A working JSON example, a successful discovery run, or a demonstration isn't by itself an OCP conformance determination. Read and satisfy the applicable mandatory requirements rather than using the existence of a mockup as evidence of recognition.
