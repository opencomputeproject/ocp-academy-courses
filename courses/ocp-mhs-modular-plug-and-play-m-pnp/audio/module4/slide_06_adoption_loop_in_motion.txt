A reproducible adoption path begins by pinning the exact inputs. Use the version zero point seven package, then record the specific hardware-component and programmable-device schema versions. Describe the implemented hardware and programmable functions, and implement the supporting firmware interfaces.

Packaging, schema checks and system behaviour are different checks. A Fru packaging result doesn't establish that the JSON semantics are correct. A JSON validation result doesn't establish that discovery, power sequencing and Bios exchange work on the assembled platform. Tool compatibility is itself something to verify.

Close the loop by contributing evidence: the input files, repository revisions, observed outcome and a focused issue or improvement. That record gives the community something concrete to reproduce and review.
