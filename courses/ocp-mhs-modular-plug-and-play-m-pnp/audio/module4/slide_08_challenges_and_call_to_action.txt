A useful contribution starts with something another engineer can reproduce. Record the package, schema, example and tool revisions. Then distinguish data checks from platform checks.

For data, examine compatibility, local and required attributes, connections and register mappings. For the platform, test what happens when required information is missing, how applicable security actions are enforced, how power transitions behave, and whether the B M C and Bios exchange the required files.

When something fails, report the expected behaviour, the observed behaviour and a minimal example. When something works, document enough of the setup for others to repeat it. The OCP MHS project page provides the community and contribution routes.
