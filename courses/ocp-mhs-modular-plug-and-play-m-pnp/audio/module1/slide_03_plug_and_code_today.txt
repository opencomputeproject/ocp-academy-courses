A server can use compatible connectors and form factors and still require substantial firmware integration. DC-MHS, the Data Center Modular Hardware System, addresses the hardware building blocks. M-PNP, Modular Plug-and-Play, adds the descriptions and processes needed for logical interoperability.

The H P M is the Host Processor Module. The S C M is the Secure Control Module, carrying the B M C, or Baseboard Management Controller. If that controller's firmware hard-codes every board and connection, changing the H P M can mean another integration project.

The M-PNP goal is to let the controlling entity discover conforming components with little or no advance knowledge of their model. That's a framework for reducing bespoke code, not a guarantee that schemas alone make every platform work. Firmware must implement the contract, and the assembled system must still be validated.
