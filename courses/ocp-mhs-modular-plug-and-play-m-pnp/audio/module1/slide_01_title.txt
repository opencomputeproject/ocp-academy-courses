Welcome to module one of the OCP Modular Plug-and-Play course. We'll begin with the problem that mechanical and electrical compatibility alone can't solve: firmware that already needs to know the exact hardware model.

This course is based on the version zero point seven release package. Its Fru, Discovery and Boot specification and its H P M and Peripheral Programmable Device specification are release candidate one documents. The Interoperability specification is version zero point three point zero. We'll connect those documents to a practical discovery and management model before going deeper into the implementation details.
