The course has four modules. First, we'll establish the interoperability goal and the documents that define it. Second, we'll follow how the management controller retrieves component descriptions, discovers the platform, and coordinates boot.

Third, we'll examine programmable devices on both the host processor module and its peripherals. Finally, we'll connect the specifications to schemas, examples, implementation checks, and contribution paths. Together, these modules take you from the M-PNP management model to the evidence needed to implement and test a design.
