The Interoperability specification defines three levels. At Plug and Code, the base specifications provide electrical and mechanical compatibility, while the integrator develops the higher-level enablement.

At Plug and Play Lite, the interoperability framework is defined, but significant code development is still needed to stitch its pieces together and reach baseline functionality. Baseline Plug and Play aims for minimal code and configuration to achieve that baseline. OEM extensions can still require additional work.

These levels describe interoperability and integration effort. A package version alone doesn't establish an implementation's level; that requires supporting evidence. The levels are also separate from the preferred and legacy methods used to discover programmable-device connectivity.
