The key idea is a management contract, not a new hardware form factor. DC-MHS supplies modular hardware interfaces. M-PNP adds a way to describe and discover those components with less advance, model-specific knowledge.

The version zero point seven package brings together Fru, Discovery and Boot, the H P M and Peripheral Programmable Device specification, and the Interoperability specification. Their schemas and examples make the data model concrete.

Treat self-description and standardized interfaces as tools for reducing bespoke integration. They require supporting firmware and a validated system. That's the mental model we'll carry into the detailed discovery process.
