Within the modular hardware family, Em P N P builds on the related specifications. The form-factor specifications define host processor modules. M-CRPS and M-PIC cover power and platform infrastructure. M-XIO defines the high-speed connector strategy.

M-PESTI, the Peripheral Sideband Tunneling Interface, supports subsystem discovery and sideband management. M-SIF addresses shared infrastructure. DC-SCM and the H P M Common Circuits specifications define the control-module connections and common circuits.

Em P N P doesn't replace those electrical or mechanical contracts. It supplies a framework for describing the resulting hardware, finding its connections, and managing it through standardized processes. When you review a design, keep the responsibilities of the related specifications separate rather than expecting one Em P N P document to define the whole server.
