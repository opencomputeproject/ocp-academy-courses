**Title**\
OCP MHS Modular Plug-and-Play (M-PNP)

**Short Description**\
Explore M-PNP discovery, boot, programmable-device interfaces, and evidence for interoperable modular hardware.

**Long Description**

Explore how OCP Modular Plug-and-Play adds discovery and logical interoperability to modular hardware systems. Use the M-PNP v0.7 release package to connect component descriptions, FRU data, boot coordination, programmable-device interfaces, and management processes. Follow the path from specifications and schemas to reproducible implementation checks and evidence-backed contributions.

**Overall duration: 45 minutes**

**Module 1: Introduction to MHS & the M-PNP Vision — 8 minutes.**\
Position M-PNP within DC-MHS and distinguish hardware, logical, and management interoperability. Identify the authoritative release package and the role of self-describing component data.

**Module 2: FRU, Discovery & Boot Specification — 13 minutes.**\
Trace FRU formats, local and extended JSON, breadth-first discovery, required-data failures, and allowed power states. Connect BMC-BIOS inventory exchange to coordinated boot decisions.

**Module 3: HPM & Peripheral Programmable Devices — 13 minutes.**\
Review primary and peripheral programmable-device roles, required interfaces, memory-map discovery, power states, Hold-Off behavior, and the JSON descriptions used by firmware.

**Module 4: Interop, Conformance, Tooling & Adoption — 11 minutes.**\
Connect interoperability requirements to schemas, guides, mockups, tools, and reference IP. Plan platform checks that distinguish available resources from verified release compatibility.
