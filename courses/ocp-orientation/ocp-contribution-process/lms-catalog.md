**Title**\
OCP Contribution Process

**Short Description**\
Follow an OCP specification from its first abstract through review, approval and publication.

**Long Description**

A contribution turns useful work into a shared public resource. Follow the specification route through the Contribution Hub, understand who acts at each stage, and learn how clear documents and timely communication help the review progress.

**Overall duration: 8 minutes**

**Module 1: OCP Contribution Process — 8 minutes.**\
Choose the contribution type, prepare an abstract, review the Contributor License Agreement and use the correct template. Watch upload demonstrations and follow project and committee review, presentations and revisions. Understand the optional exclusion-period waiver, the approval and acknowledgement needed before publication, and how to communicate a withdrawal or share the published contribution.
