# pronunciation: OCP, IDC, AMD and AI are spoken as letters.
Picture a camera above a moving production line. A bottle passes underneath. The camera spots a crooked cap. A machine downstream has a moment to divert the bottle. Where should the image go for an answer?

One path leads to a computer beside the line. The other leads to a remote data center. Give the remote path a little more distance and you can see why the team might want to try running the model on site. But who will service the local computer? And how much space and cooling will it need?

This is inference, putting a trained model to work on a new input. O C P’s A I Computing Continuum Project, or A I C C, works on open hardware for places like this factory. A computer beside the line has to fit the space, power and cooling the factory can spare.

At the Summit, O C P’s George Tchaparian asked for help on three fronts as the community looks to twenty twenty-seven. Give designers more choice of A I chips. Design the computing equipment and its facility together. And make open infrastructure useful wherever inference needs to happen, including places like this factory.
