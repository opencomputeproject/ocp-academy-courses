Picture a purchase order on a desk. A data center operator needs servers for a new hall. The operator pays an equipment supplier. A shared design helped shape those servers. Where, exactly, does that purchase show up?

O C P commissioned I D C to study what buyers around the world spend on equipment based on O C P-recognized designs. Servers account for the largest part. The study also covers equipment such as switches, racks, power systems and cooling. Think of the dollars as traveling from a buyer to a seller. That’s the transaction the study is trying to measure.

[thoughtful] Here’s an interesting wrinkle. A company can use an openly shared design even if it has never joined O C P. The design can travel farther than the membership list. That’s part of what makes the market worth measuring. And in twenty twenty-six, the people doing the measuring had quite an update.
