Come back to Prometheus. Inside one building, the computers already have to exchange information quickly enough to work as a team. Now put some of those computers in another building. The training job still needs them to act like one cluster.

Santosh Janardhan described how Meta brought connections from different buildings together in a regional network. Follow the light from one roof to the next. Along the way, somebody has to specify the fiber, choose the switches and figure out what happens if a path goes down.

Bloomberg Intelligence sees that change in the market around each A I rack. Adding computers also creates demand for the equipment that connects them. A network designer can look at a map like this and see a whole second shopping list.
