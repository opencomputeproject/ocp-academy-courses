Start with something you already know. If you buy or maintain equipment, the Rack and Power Project is a place to bring a detail you wish had been on a supplier’s drawing. If you connect buildings, the Networking Project is a place to compare notes on what changes when a cluster moves beyond one site.

If economics or policy is your field, take a look at Heat Reuse. A data center may have heat to offer. A neighbor may have a use for it. People still have to work out who pays for the connection and what happens when demand changes. There are workstreams for exactly those questions.

And if you run a factory, an enterprise site or a telecom facility, the A I Computing Continuum Project wants to understand where equipment will actually have to work.

[warmly] Pick the conversation you’d like to try. You’ll find a question to start with and a link to the people already working on it.
