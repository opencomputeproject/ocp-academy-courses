**Title**\
Understanding and Procuring Open Technologies and Products

**Short Description**\
Understand open technology, compare the full cost, and choose how to buy, support and contribute.

**Long Description**

A good purchase of open technology starts with what you can use and change, then follows through to the people and support that keep it working. Explore how to assess those choices and take part in the communities behind the technology.

**Overall duration: 7 minutes**

**Module 1: Understanding and Procuring Open Technologies and Products — 7 minutes.**\
Explore openness in software, hardware, data, AI models and standards. Learn how license terms, full costs, supplier choices and community activity shape a purchase. Use seven practical checks, plan the work across the life of a system, compare delivery and payment models, and consider five levels of community participation.
