**Title**\
Introduction to the OCP Solution Provider Program

**Short Description**\
Find the right OCP program for products, facilities and services, and understand the path to a Marketplace listing.

**Long Description**

The OCP Solution Provider Program connects products, facilities and services with people who can use them. Understand what each recognition means, what evidence supports it, and how participation leads to a clear, useful Marketplace listing.

**Overall duration: 7 minutes**

**Module 1: Introduction to the OCP Solution Provider Program — 7 minutes.**\
Explore Marketplace products, chiplets, integrated solutions, services, facilities and Experience Centers. Distinguish OCP Accepted product recognition, S.A.F.E. firmware security review and OCP Ready facility recognition. Follow the evidence, review and agreement steps, learn what Experience Centers offer, and understand membership, fees, authorized marks and keeping listing information current.
