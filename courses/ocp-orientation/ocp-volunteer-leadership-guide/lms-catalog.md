**Title**\
OCP's Volunteer Leadership Guide

**Short Description**\
Help people contribute through clear leadership roles, shared goals, dependable records and useful feedback.

**Long Description**

Volunteer leadership makes it easier for people to work together and create something others can use. Learn how to serve the OCP Community, organize its work and help contributors reach the right reviewers and support.

**Overall duration: 10 minutes**

**Module 1: OCP's Volunteer Leadership Guide — 10 minutes.**\
Understand the four leadership roles, selection processes, onboarding and shared tools. Connect project goals with meetings, records and useful contributions. Learn how contribution types, specification layers and supporting evidence shape review. Distinguish recognition programs, explore the Marketplace, and guide members to the project, Contribution Hub or Foundation team that can help.
