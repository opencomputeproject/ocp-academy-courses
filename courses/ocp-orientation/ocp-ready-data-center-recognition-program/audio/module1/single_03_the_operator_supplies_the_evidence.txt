# pronunciation: OCP -> O C P; AI -> A I; ID -> I D.
The assessment records practical details. Version one asks for the delivery route’s clear height and width, independent rack power feeds, and circuit capacity. Version two includes round-the-clock checkpoint staffing and pre-approved, I D checked, escorted visitors among its options. If supplemental cooling such as rear-door heat exchangers is used, add notes and make technical analysis available on request. These are examples, not the full checklist. The operator supplies the self-assessment, the Data Center Facility team reviews it, and the Foundation handles approval. This process does not include an O C P on-site audit.
