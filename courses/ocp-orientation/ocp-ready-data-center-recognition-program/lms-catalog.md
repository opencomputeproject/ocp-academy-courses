**Title**\
OCP Ready™ Data Center Recognition Program

**Short Description**\
Explore OCP Ready assessment paths, practical site questions, and the steps from evidence to facility recognition.

**Long Description**

Understand how facility operators prepare evidence for OCP Ready recognition, and how project review and Foundation approval fit together. Explore the two established assessment paths and the journey to a public facility listing. This is a self-assessment and review process without an OCP on-site audit.

**Overall duration: 6 minutes**

**Module 1: OCP Ready™ Data Center Recognition Program — 6 minutes.**\
See how v1 records delivery height and width, independent rack power feeds and circuit capacity. Explore v2 options for 24/7 security staffing and visitors approved in advance, with ID checks and escorts. Learn about notes and technical analysis when added cooling, such as rear-door heat exchangers, is used. Follow the nine assessment areas through review, agreements and publication. A linked course explains the Open Data Center for AI initiative’s facility specification, which could inform a future OCP Ready for AI self-assessment. That possible assessment remains undecided.
