**Title**\
Introduction to Emerging Optical Technologies

**Short Description**\
Compare emerging optics for AI infrastructure through power, serviceability, fiber density, latency, and readiness.

**Long Description**

Explore the optical technology shifts driven by AI infrastructure across scale-up, scale-out, and scale-across networks. Compare closer optical integration, expanded-beam and blind-mate connectivity, multicore fiber, hollow-core fiber, and quantum infrastructure considerations. Connect each approach to power, cooling, serviceability, density, testing, latency, and the ecosystem work needed for adoption.

**Overall duration: 60 minutes**

**Module 1: Why AI Infrastructure Is Changing Optics — 13 minutes.**\
Explain how AI workload and rack architecture change optical demands. Connect networking domains, bandwidth growth, and fiber density to infrastructure-level constraints.

**Module 2: Power, Thermal, and Serviceability Shifts — 17 minutes.**\
Compare pluggable, near-packaged, and co-packaged optics. Examine expanded-beam and rear blind-mate connectivity through electrical reach, power, cooling, and maintenance tradeoffs.

**Module 3: Density and Testability in the Optical Plant — 15 minutes.**\
Connect physical fiber-density limits to multicore fiber and space-division multiplexing. Review fan-in/fan-out transitions and testing implications for emerging fiber systems.

**Module 4: Latency, Quantum, and the Adoption Horizon — 15 minutes.**\
Explore hollow-core propagation latency and hybrid quantum-classical systems. Compare use cases, adoption horizons, and ecosystem readiness across the emerging optical approaches.
