The building columns stay fixed. What changes is the proposed row arrangement. Revision zero point seven replaces rigid row-length prescriptions with modular density in kilowatts per linear foot.

Use an illustrative calculation to see the units. At an assumed uniform twenty-five kilowatts per foot, forty usable feet correspond to one thousand kilowatts. If the available row is thirty-two feet, the same assumed density gives eight hundred kilowatts. Neither length is a specification requirement, and this simple arithmetic is not a complete blended-facility capacity model.

The second design accommodates a different equipment mix and site geometry. Shortening the row does not make the lost capacity disappear. Reconcile the actual length, equipment demand, power, cooling, access, and structural conditions before accepting the arrangement.
