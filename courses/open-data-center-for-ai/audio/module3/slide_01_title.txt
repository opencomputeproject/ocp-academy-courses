Module three examines Power and Cooling at A I Density. The central discipline is to use the right number at the right level.

A blended value describes a mixed facility. A peak value protects the most demanding local zone. Cluster megawatts, hall power blocks, row busways, cooling splits, pipe diameters, and quick connects are related, but they are not interchangeable. We will keep each target attached to its scope and assumptions.
