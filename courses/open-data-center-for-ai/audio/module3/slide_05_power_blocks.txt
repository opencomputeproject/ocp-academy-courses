Count the power blocks at hall level. Section five point two requires at least four. That sentence does not define a block's megawatt rating, independent feeds, redundancy, or a mandatory one-block-to-one-row mapping. Those details need the actual electrical design.

Now move to the separate row detail. Two busways and four busways are different distribution arrangements. Their number depends on the design and desired row power density. Adding busways does not create upstream power or prove independent supplies.

The Appendix drawings help visualize routing and equipment, but they are examples. Keep three records distinct: the hall's minimum block count, the available supply and topology, and the row's distribution and connection design. Component specifications can support that review; they do not remove the need to connect these levels.
