First compare cooling placement while the busway arrangement stays unchanged. A rear fanwall and in-row coolers use space differently. In-row units occupy real positions in the rack line; they cannot simply cover the rack fronts. Service access and air paths need to remain workable.

Then compare busway count separately. Additional busways and tray tiers can change the overhead coordination problem. Recheck the height; do not assume that different arrangements fit the same volume.

The Google and Nvidia Appendix concepts provide useful reference views for these questions. They are non-normative examples, not mandatory construction details. The course animation separates the design variables to make the tradeoffs visible; it does not certify a combined design.
