Different rack systems can be evaluated against the same facility targets without sharing an identical connector. Start with the physical boundary: weight, movement, envelope, and access. Then examine the electrical connection, rating, and supply arrangement. Next check the liquid connection, required flow, pressure, and temperature conditions.

The second system may need a different implementation or a documented adaptation. That is not automatic failure, but it is also not plug-and-play proof. Each boundary requires evidence and a responsible reviewer.

Finally, agree on service responsibilities and operating information. Telemetry and service-level agreements belong to the broader initiative and project coordination; Revision zero point seven does not fully specify universal interfaces for them. This is a conceptual review model, not a new standardized docking product.
