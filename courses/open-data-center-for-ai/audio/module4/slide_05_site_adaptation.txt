Consider a site with seventeen feet six inches from floor to beam underside. The proposed two-busway arrangement is below the eighteen-foot reference in Section four point two. That section explicitly calls for custom design variations where facilities cannot meet the standard.

The review record therefore names the source and datum, records the measured site condition, and describes a proposed custom overhead arrangement. The evidence must address clearances, the air plenum, thermal performance, structural implications, fire protection, and maintenance access. The M E P lead coordinates those reviews with the accountable specialists and operator.
