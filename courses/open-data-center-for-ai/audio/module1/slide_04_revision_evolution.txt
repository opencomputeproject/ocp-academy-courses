Revision zero point five was an important Google-authored reference. Revision zero point seven becomes a broader industry baseline, with AMD, Google, Meta, and Nvidia listed as authors. That added collaboration matters because the target is no longer one operator's near-term layout. It is a common envelope for multiple A I and M L systems expected across twenty twenty-seven and twenty twenty-eight.

The revision history shows the shift. Platform-specific tables, fixed row-length prescriptions, campus layouts, and several older appendices were removed. In their place, the document emphasizes first-principles targets for structure, space, power, cooling, and interfaces. The new revision also expresses row capacity in modular kilowatts per linear foot. The result is more concise, but it asks the reader to preserve context carefully: a stable requirement is not the same thing as a mandated physical solution.
