Welcome to Open Data Center for A I. The expensive question is no longer whether a facility can support today's rack. It is whether the next rack—heavier, denser, and possibly from a different supplier—can move from dock to row, land on the floor, connect to power and cooling, and operate without a redesign.

Revision zero point seven turns those risks into measurable facility interfaces. By the end of this course, you will know which constraints to test first, which capacity figures belong at rack, row, or hall level, and where the specification deliberately protects implementation choice. The specification is our technical center of gravity, with context from the O C P strategic initiative and its E M E A Summit discussion, including perspectives from AMD, Google, Meta, and Nvidia.
