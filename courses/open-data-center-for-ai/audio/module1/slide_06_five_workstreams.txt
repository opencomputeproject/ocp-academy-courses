The Open Data Center specification is one part of a wider O C P strategic initiative. The first workstream develops open facility reference designs for power, cooling, mechanical systems, and row infrastructure. That is where Revision zero point seven contributes most directly.

Four other workstreams extend the system view. Energy and grid work addresses connection requirements, flexibility, on-site generation, and storage. Open service-level agreements create shared engineering practices among facilities, equipment, and workload operators. Telemetry work seeks secure, harmonized observations across I T and operational technology. Power estimation improves planning when workload behavior and silicon roadmaps are uncertain. Together, the five streams connect the building, the grid, the hardware, and operations. A good facility envelope is necessary, but it creates full value only when these adjacent agreements and measurements mature with it.
