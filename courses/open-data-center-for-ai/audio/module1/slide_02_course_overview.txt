The course follows the logic of a real facility decision. In four modules, you will gain a review lens you can use before committing capital. You will learn which physical constraints can stop a dense rack before it reaches the row; where power and cooling numbers actually apply; why a full-row manifold and a rack connector are different decisions; and how to compare implementation options without mistaking an example for a requirement.

Allow about forty-five minutes for narration, study, and the scenario checks. This edition uses published Revision zero point seven, effective August twenty-seventh, twenty twenty-six. The specification link and a reusable review worksheet are included. The worksheet helps you record evidence and open questions; it is not an engineering approval.
