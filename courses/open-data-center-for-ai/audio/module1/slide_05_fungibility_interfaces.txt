These two rack implementations are deliberately different. Fungibility does not require matching internals. It means the facility can accommodate different systems and generations with limited retrofit.

Hold the review boundary steady: structural and moving loads, physical clearances, power delivery, liquid operating conditions, and service access. Then require evidence from each proposed implementation. A matching nominal envelope is useful, but it does not prove that plugs, fluid connections, or operating agreements match.

This is what makes late binding practical. The site can advance before the final accelerator decision, while unresolved interfaces remain visible. The wider strategic initiative also addresses telemetry and service-level agreements. These are continuing coordination needs, not a promise that Revision zero point seven already defines a universal connection for everything.
