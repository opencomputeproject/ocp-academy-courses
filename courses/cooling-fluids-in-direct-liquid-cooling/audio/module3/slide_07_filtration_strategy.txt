Filtration is the practical defense between routine particles and expensive cold plate cleaning, but filter fineness is not universal. The earlier water-based operating guideline identifies sidestream filtration below five microns, while Project Deschutes documents four twenty-five micron inline filters and one zero point two micron sidestream filter in one design. These values are source-specific examples, not requirements for every coolant.

P G twenty-five and other inhibited fluids can contain proprietary additives. Select filter media, micron rating, and sidestream flow with the coolant supplier and the C D U, cold plate, and filter manufacturers. The goal is to capture the intended particles or biofoulants without restricting primary flow or changing fluid performance. Inline mesh filters can protect heat exchangers and quick connectors, but they are not a substitute for a supplier-validated sidestream strategy.
