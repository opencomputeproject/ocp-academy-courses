Filter selection is more than choosing the smallest micron number. The P G twenty-five guideline recommends considering sidestream filtration because it avoids placing filter-clogging back pressure in the main cooling path. Select the rating, efficiency, flow, and media for the smallest sensitive passages and the actual coolant.

Overly fine filtration can remove antifoam additives. The guideline also warns that polypropylene media may adsorb siloxane antifoam. A nominal and an absolute filter with the same stated micron size do not have the same capture efficiency. Confirm performance with the fluid, filter, C D U, and cold plate suppliers.

Its example of five-to-ten micron absolute filtration during fill is not a universal sidestream requirement. Likewise, Project Deschutes filter ratings describe one design. Preserve cleanliness without stripping additives, restricting flow, or creating unserviceable equipment.
