Microbial guidance must match the fluid. For treated-water operation, the earlier water-based guideline recommends total viable bacteria below one hundred colony-forming units per milliliter and action above one thousand. Those operating thresholds differ from the water coolant base specification's fresh-product limit.

P G twenty-five has a biostatic character, but that does not make the loop sterile. Residual water or uncontrolled top-off can dilute glycol and inhibitors and increase the risk of biological growth. Maintain the exact supplier-approved concentration and chemistry, keep commissioning clean, and investigate abnormal fluid condition.

Do not transfer a water-treatment dosing recipe to P G twenty-five. Any biocide or corrective treatment needs formulation-specific supplier approval. Cleaning biocides must be removed before final fill so they do not interfere with the operating fluid's corrosion protection.
