Startup creates the baseline for later operating decisions. For P G twenty-five, the new guideline recommends bleeding air where necessary and checking for leaks while circulating for at least thirty minutes during final startup. That startup check is not a complete cleaning procedure or a universal mixing time.

Take a representative operating sample from each system within the first one to three days and retain it for the life of the fluid. Also retain an unexposed fresh-fluid sample. Label both with the loop or unit, fluid identity, and date.

Collect well-mixed circulating fluid, flush stagnant fluid from the sample port, and avoid reservoir corners, low-point drains, and dead legs. After sufficient circulation, verify glycol concentration with the supplier's refractometer correlation. Send the baseline sample for analysis so future changes have a trustworthy reference.
