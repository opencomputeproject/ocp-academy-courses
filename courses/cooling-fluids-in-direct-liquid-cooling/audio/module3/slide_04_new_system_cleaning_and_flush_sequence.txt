Start with a documented cleaning plan based on fabrication history, expected contaminants, metallurgy, and supplier guidance. Water can move loose debris, but it does not establish that oils, grease, flux, weld residues, or corrosion products have been removed. Qualified specialists should determine whether degreasing, chemical cleaning, or passivation is needed.

The P G twenty-five guideline does not prescribe one cleaning procedure. Choose a compatible cleaning solution, heat transfer fluid, or water as appropriate. Continue flushing until cleanliness is demonstrated through filter behavior, inspection, and representative testing, rather than a fixed duration or pass count.

The animation illustrates staged debris flushing only. Verify cleaner removal, drain residual rinse fluid, replace filters as needed, and fill with approved ready-to-use P G twenty-five. Residual water can dilute both glycol and inhibitors, so verify the final concentration.
