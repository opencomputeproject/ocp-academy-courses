Adjustments should be controlled because the cure can become the contaminant. The single-phase operating guidance says adjustments should only be made under guidance from the heat transfer fluid supplier and according to the approved treatment plan. For P G twenty-five, the published base specification also requires the supplier to support compatible restoration of glycol concentration and inhibitor levels after overdilution or depletion.

Before dosing, the system volume must be known. Add the predetermined amount while the fluid is circulating. Then continue circulation until the adjustment is uniformly mixed and the follow-up sample will be representative. The earlier guideline gives thirty minutes, but elapsed time alone does not account for system volume, circulating flow, bypasses, or dead legs. Establish the sampling delay from verified loop turnover and supplier guidance.

Documentation matters here. Record the date, system, additive type, amount added, and the analytical reports before and after the adjustment.
