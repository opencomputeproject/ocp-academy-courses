Validate an abnormal result before adjusting chemistry. Check calibration, confirm with suitable testing, and consult the fluid supplier. The P G twenty-five guideline recommends sending a sample to a qualified laboratory within one week after an on-site test indicates an out-of-limit result.

The system volume must be known before calculating a dose. Restore glycol or inhibitors only with compatible supplier-approved products. A concentrated formulation may correct overdilution; approved high-purity water is an exceptional correction for excessive concentration, not routine permission to dilute ready-to-use product.

Circulate until mixing is adequate and the follow-up sample is representative. Use verified loop circulation and supplier guidance rather than a universal timer. Record the reason, amount, batch, and before-and-after results. Once the correction is verified, establish the appropriate new baseline.
