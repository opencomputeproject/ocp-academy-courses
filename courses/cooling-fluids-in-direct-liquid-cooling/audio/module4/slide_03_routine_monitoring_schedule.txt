Monitoring frequency should match the fluid and the system's risk. The water-based operating guideline gives quarterly routine checks, more frequent checks when results are abnormal, and laboratory review quarterly in the first year, then every six months if stable.

The new P G twenty-five guideline uses different risk-based examples. Low risk combines inline monitoring, acceptable materials, and an experienced user: commissioning analysis and annual laboratory testing. Medium risk has no inline monitoring but acceptable materials and an unfamiliar user: laboratory testing every six months for the first one and a half years. High risk adds less-certain material compatibility: quarterly laboratory testing for that period.

These are planning examples, not one universal schedule. Follow warranty and supplier requirements, and increase testing after service events or unexplained anomalies. Even with inline monitoring, the guideline recommends annual laboratory confirmation.
