The P G twenty-five laboratory panel goes beyond water cleanliness. The guideline recommends checking glycol concentration, pH, reserve alkalinity, appearance, inhibitor concentrations, chloride, sulfate, hardness, degradation acids, and dissolved metals.

Reserve alkalinity depends on inhibitor technology. Conventional formulations and organic-acid formulations do not use one interchangeable interpretation, so a low number is not automatically a reason to dose. The supplier must interpret the actual formulation.

Use a laboratory managed or recommended by the fluid supplier and capable of comprehensive analysis and technical support. Trend metals relevant to the system's metallurgy, and compare the report with fresh-fluid and startup evidence. Field pH and refractometer checks, inline sensors, and laboratory chemistry are complementary; none replaces the entire monitoring plan.
