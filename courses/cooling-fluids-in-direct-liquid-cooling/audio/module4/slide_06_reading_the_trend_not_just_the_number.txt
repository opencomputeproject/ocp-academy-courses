A trend adds direction and context to a measurement. Compare pH, concentration, conductivity, metals, appearance, and filter loading with the baseline, instrument condition, temperature, and recent service events.

Distinguish new-fluid specification limits from operating guidance. The P G twenty-five guideline provides example in-use ranges, including formulation-dependent pH ranges. These do not replace the base specification's new-product requirements or automatically authorize widening the supplier's operating limits.

Confirm a sudden change with calibrated field measurements or laboratory analysis before dosing. In inhibited glycol, conductivity alone has limited diagnostic value. A rising copper or iron trend may warrant investigation even before an action limit is reached.

Place sensors in representative flowing fluid, maintain calibration, and compare them with laboratory results. Review limits after major system or fluid changes.
