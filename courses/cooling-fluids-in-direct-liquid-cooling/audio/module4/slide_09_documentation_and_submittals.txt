Keep a traceable file for each cooling loop. Record fluid identity, delivery and batch information, certificates of analysis, safety data sheets, system volume, wetted materials, cleaning and passivation history, and component replacements.

The P G twenty-five guideline also emphasizes consistent sample labels and submission paperwork so reports can be reliably trended. Preserve analytical reports, dosing records, and startup evidence. Maintain a sensor logbook with calibration results, cleaning, maintenance, replacements, and baseline changes.

Include handling and end-of-life decisions in the plan. Store fluid in compatible, protected containers under supplier guidance. Do not discharge used coolant to drains, soil, or surface water; follow local rules and approved recycling or disposal arrangements.

Documentation should let the next operator understand what changed, why it changed, and which evidence justified the decision.
