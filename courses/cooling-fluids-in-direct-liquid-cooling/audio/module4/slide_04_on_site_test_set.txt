Routine monitoring combines quick field checks with scheduled laboratory signals. Conductivity, pH, hardness, bacteria, azoles, turbidity, appearance, and filter loading each tell a different part of the story.

Dissolved metals belong in this monitoring set even though they are normally measured by laboratory I C P analysis rather than an on-site kit. Select copper, iron, aluminum, zinc, nickel, chromium, and other elements according to the system's wetted-material inventory. A detectable result alone is not conclusive. Compare it with the fresh-fluid and startup baselines, the laboratory method, and the direction of the trend. A sustained increase can indicate active corrosion or material release and should trigger investigation.
