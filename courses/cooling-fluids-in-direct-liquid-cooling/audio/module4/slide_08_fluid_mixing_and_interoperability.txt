Do not assume that all organic-acid coolants mix safely or that every inorganic and organic combination is incompatible. The P G twenty-five base specification says compliant products from different suppliers are not necessarily interchangeable. Evaluate the exact fluids, materials, and operating conditions.

The new guideline adds a practical equipment-introduction step. If new equipment contains water or another formulation, use a supplier-approved flushing protocol and fill it with the operating fluid before connection. Using the same fluid through manufacturing, testing, and operation reduces unintended chemistry changes.

Increase sampling after substantial service or equipment additions until fluid stability is confirmed. Even a tested mixture can complicate interpretation because inhibitor baselines and corrective actions may no longer be clear. Avoid unapproved top-off and document every intentional change.
