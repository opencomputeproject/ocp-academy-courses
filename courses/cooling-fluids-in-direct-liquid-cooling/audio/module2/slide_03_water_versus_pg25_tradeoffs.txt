Water is an excellent heat transfer fluid. If the only question were heat capacity, water would be hard to beat. But in a data center T C S loop, heat transfer is not the only question. Water must be managed for corrosion, biological growth, dissolved and suspended solids, pH, hardness, and additive balance.

The published OCP P G twenty-five specification defines a ready-to-use product containing twenty-five to twenty-eight percent propylene glycol by weight. Compared with water, P G twenty-five has different thermal properties, so the cooling design must use measured fluid data across its operating range. It also carries a proprietary inhibitor package, which means a compliant product still needs product-specific compatibility evidence and should not be treated as interchangeable with another supplier's P G twenty-five.
