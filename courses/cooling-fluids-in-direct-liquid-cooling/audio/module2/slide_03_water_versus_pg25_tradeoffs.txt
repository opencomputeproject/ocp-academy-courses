Water is an excellent heat transfer fluid, but heat capacity is not the only design concern. A data center T C S loop also needs corrosion protection, biological control, and management of particles, dissolved material, and additive balance.

The P G twenty-five base specification defines a ready-to-use product containing twenty-five to twenty-eight percent propylene glycol by weight. The new operating guideline describes glycol concentrations by volume. Weight and volume percentages are not interchangeable. Use the fluid supplier's formulation-specific data and identify the measurement basis in every record.

P G twenty-five has different thermal properties from water, so design with measured data across the operating range. Its glycol helps limit microbial growth, while its inhibitor package protects materials. Product compliance does not prove that different suppliers' formulations are interchangeable.
