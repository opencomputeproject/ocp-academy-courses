Compatibility follows every surface the fluid touches: cold plates, heat exchangers, pump seals, quick disconnects, hoses, filter media, reservoirs, thread sealants, brazing materials, and plated surfaces.

The P G twenty-five guideline's material lists are representative, not an exhaustive approval list. Verify the exact material grade and fluid formulation at the expected temperature, exposure duration, and operating conditions. Review manufacturer data and supplier recommendations, and arrange controlled testing when evidence is insufficient.

Filter cartridges and housing gaskets belong in that review. A filter can be chemically stable yet still affect an additive, and a compatible metal does not guarantee a compatible seal. Any change to the wetted-material inventory should trigger re-evaluation of the complete fluid and system combination.
