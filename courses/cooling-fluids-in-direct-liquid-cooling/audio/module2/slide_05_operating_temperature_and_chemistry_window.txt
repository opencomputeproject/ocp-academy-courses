Temperature changes chemistry and compatibility, and the two operating documents describe different typical ranges. The water-based operating guidance describes typical T C S operation below forty-nine degrees C and calls for additional review beyond sixty-six degrees C. The P G twenty-five guideline describes a typical closed-loop range of seventeen to sixty-six degrees C.

These are application guidance, not permission to operate every coolant or component anywhere within that range. Use the actual fluid and equipment suppliers' approved envelope. Above the P G twenty-five guideline's typical range, review the wetted materials, particularly elastomers and plastics.

Local cold plate surface temperatures and stagnant regions can be harsher than the bulk fluid. Include those conditions when validating compatibility and inhibitor performance.
