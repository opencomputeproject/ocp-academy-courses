For this course, the main single-phase fluid families are treated water and P G twenty-five. Treated water can deliver excellent heat transfer, but it needs active attention to corrosion, biological growth, conductivity, solids, hardness, and additives.

The published OCP P G twenty-five specification defines a ready-to-use fluid with twenty-five to twenty-eight percent propylene glycol by weight. It gives up some thermal performance compared with water and therefore needs measured property data for the operating range. The specification also requires corrosion inhibitors, a dye, controlled impurities, product filtration, and shipment evidence.

Two-phase dielectric refrigerants appear here only as a contrast, because their operating logic is different.
