# PG25 Operating-Guideline Refresh

English source refresh: October 7, 2026.

## Source Roles

- [Water-based coolant base specification V1.3](https://www.opencompute.org/documents/base-specification-for-single-phase-water-based-cold-plate-coolant-v1-3-final-pdf): ready-to-use water coolant requirements.
- [PG25 base specification V1.0.0](https://www.opencompute.org/documents/ocp-base-specification-pg25-v1-0-0-final-pdf): ready-to-use PG25 product requirements.
- [PG25 Guidelines V1.0.0](https://www.opencompute.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OCP-PG25-Guidelines-V1.0.0.pdf): lifecycle recommendations, including compatibility, commissioning, filtration, risk-based analysis, sensors, remediation, and disposal.

The guideline's typical properties and example in-use limits are not universal acceptance criteria and do not supersede either base specification or formulation-specific supplier limits. Weight and volume percentages must be identified, not interchanged.
