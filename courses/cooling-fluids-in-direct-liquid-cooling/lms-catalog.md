**Title**\
Cooling Fluids in Direct Liquid Cooling (DLC)

**Short Description**\
Compare water and PG25 coolants, then apply fluid compatibility, commissioning, monitoring, and maintenance practices.

**Long Description**

Build a practical understanding of single-phase technology cooling system fluids for direct liquid cooling. Compare treated water and PG25, connect coolant chemistry to wetted-material compatibility, and follow the fluid lifecycle from supplier qualification and commissioning through monitoring, controlled corrections, and operational records. Knowledge checks help learners apply the guidance to realistic fluid-management decisions.

**Overall duration: 35 minutes**

**Module 1: Why Single-Phase Fluids Matter in DLC — 7 minutes.**\
Locate the fluid within the cooling system, distinguish single-phase and two-phase approaches, and identify the recurring compatibility, monitoring, and documentation decisions.

**Module 2: Choosing and Specifying Single-Phase Fluids — 10 minutes.**\
Compare water and PG25 across performance and maintenance needs. Review coolant quality, inhibitor chemistry, operating conditions, wetted materials, and supplier qualification evidence.

**Module 3: Commissioning, Filling, and Protecting the Loop — 9 minutes.**\
Connect serviceable loop design to compatible cleaning, flushing, filling, filtration, and microbial control. Establish startup sampling and baseline records for later operation.

**Module 4: Monitoring, Maintenance, and Operational Judgment — 9 minutes.**\
Combine routine checks and laboratory analysis to interpret fluid trends. Apply supplier-guided adjustments, assess mixing and lifecycle questions, and maintain useful operational documentation.
