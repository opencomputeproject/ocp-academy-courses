**Title**\
Introduction to Liquid Cooling

**Short Description**\
Explore liquid cooling approaches, complete heat paths, fluid and interface choices, and deployment readiness.

**Long Description**

This introductory course connects direct liquid cooling and immersion to the complete facility heat path. Learners compare phase behavior, examine fluid and interface compatibility, and use practical evidence questions to review deployment readiness and value.

**Overall duration: 68 minutes**

**Module 1: Why Liquid Cooling Now? — 17 minutes.**\
Explain adoption drivers, distinguish cooling architectures and phases, and evaluate benefits at defined boundaries.

**Module 2: Direct Liquid Cooling (DLC) — 17 minutes.**\
Follow a cold-plate loop and examine CDU boundaries, fluids, liquid-to-air retrofits and specified connections.

**Module 3: Immersion Cooling Essentials — 17 minutes.**\
Compare single-phase and two-phase immersion, then review equipment qualification, service, coverage and lifecycle evidence.

**Module 4: Operations, Planning & Strategic Value — 17 minutes.**\
Review site readiness, supplier responsibilities, commissioning, monitoring and the evidence behind strategic value.
