# pronunciation: OCP -> O C P
By the end of this module, you'll be able to outline the main site and supplier questions, explain why commissioning must test the installation's handoffs, and distinguish different kinds of efficiency and lifecycle evidence. You'll also know where to continue learning in O C P Academy and the Cooling Environments community.

The course prepares you to contribute to a deployment review. Detailed design decisions and operating procedures still come from the qualified installation and its responsible teams. Our focus is recognizing what needs to be demonstrated and who must own the answer.
