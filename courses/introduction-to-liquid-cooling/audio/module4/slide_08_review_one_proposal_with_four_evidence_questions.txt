Return to the proposal to add liquid-cooled computing at an existing site. Use four evidence questions to organize the decision.

First, is the complete heat path supported? Follow the equipment load through acquisition, transport and final rejection. Check the conditions and capacity at each handoff, including any remaining air-cooled load.

Second, are the interfaces and materials qualified? Connect the specified mating parts, working fluid and wetted materials to the configuration that will actually operate. Similar product descriptions aren't a substitute for that evidence.

Third, do operations and fault responses have owners? Review commissioning, monitoring, maintenance, spares, training and recovery. A supplier's component responsibility needs to connect with the site's operating responsibility.

Fourth, does the claimed value have a measured basis? Identify the baseline, the service being delivered and the boundaries behind energy, water, carbon or heat-reuse claims.

These questions apply to facility-water integration, a liquid-to-air retrofit and immersion, even though their details differ. Missing evidence calls for a specific next step. The review should help the team resolve what is unknown and reach a supported decision, rather than automatically accepting or rejecting an entire technology category.
