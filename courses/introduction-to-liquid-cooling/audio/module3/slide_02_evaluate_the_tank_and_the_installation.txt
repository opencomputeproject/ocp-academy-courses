Evaluate both the tank and the installation around it. First, distinguish a bath that stays liquid from a system that boils and condenses its working fluid. Then follow the heat beyond the tank. Finally, examine equipment compatibility, service methods, warranty coverage and the evidence behind claimed benefits.

A dielectric fluid supports the intended electrical environment, but nonconductive doesn't mean that any hardware can be immersed safely. The selected equipment, fluid, operating conditions and procedures need to work together. That complete-system view will guide our comparisons throughout this module.
