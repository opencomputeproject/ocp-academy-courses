Immersion readiness extends beyond the bath. Single-phase systems keep their working fluid liquid; two-phase systems use boiling and condensation. Both require a supported path that carries heat onward from the tank.

Qualify the actual equipment, fluid and service process together. Distinguish technical testing and safety evaluation from written warranty coverage. Finally, assess benefits against the complete facility path and fluid lifecycle. These questions help you compare a proposed installation on its own evidence, while recognizing that different sites and workloads can need different cooling arrangements.
