# pronunciation: IT -> I T
This course follows four connected decisions. First, understand why a workload might need liquid cooling. Next, follow a cold-plate system and its interfaces. Then compare immersion approaches and their operating requirements. Finally, connect the technology to site readiness, procurement and measurable value.

Allow about an hour for the complete course, including narration, diagrams and knowledge checks. You don't need to perform engineering calculations. The aim is to help I T, facilities, procurement and sustainability colleagues ask informed questions together. Optional links take you to more detailed Academy courses when you need them.
