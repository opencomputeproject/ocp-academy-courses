Keep four ideas from this module. Heat concentration at a component and total rack heat are different parts of the cooling problem. Heat must be acquired, transported and eventually rejected or usefully reused. Cold plates and immersion describe physical arrangements, while single-phase and two-phase describe fluid behavior. And every benefit needs a clear measurement boundary.

Before choosing hardware, ask one facility question: where will its heat go next? Then identify any remaining air-cooled load and the evidence needed to support the complete path. Those questions provide the foundation for the next three modules.
