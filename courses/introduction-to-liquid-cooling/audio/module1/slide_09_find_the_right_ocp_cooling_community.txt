# pronunciation: OCP -> O C P
The O C P Cooling Environments Project provides a useful map of the community's work. Its five functional areas are cold plates, coolant distribution units, immersion, door heat exchangers and heat reuse. Together, they span much more than the cooling device inside a server.

Cold-plate work addresses components and interfaces along the technology cooling path. Coolant distribution units connect cooling responsibilities across system boundaries. Immersion work considers equipment in dielectric fluid. Door heat exchangers collect heat from rack airflow. Heat-reuse work examines how another application can make use of the energy leaving the data center.

Open specifications and shared guidance help people describe interfaces, compare requirements and request relevant qualification evidence. Their value comes from making those requirements visible. Publication doesn't mean that any pair of products will work together without checking the actual interface and application.

Use the project page to find the community that matches your question. The Global Summit Cooling Environments track offers optional exploration of current work. You don't need to watch those recordings to complete this introduction. The shared starting point remains the same: follow the heat and identify the responsibility at each boundary.
