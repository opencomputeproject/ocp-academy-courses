Start with four questions. Where does the heat go? Which fluid is qualified for the loop? How do the components connect? And who keeps the system within its operating requirements?

We'll answer them by following cold plates, manifolds, hoses, connectors and a coolant distribution unit. We'll also compare facility-water integration with a liquid-to-air retrofit path. You won't need to size a pump or interpret a mechanical tolerance. The goal is to recognize the components, their responsibilities and the evidence needed before an installation can be accepted.
