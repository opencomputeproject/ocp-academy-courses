# pronunciation: UQDs -> U Q Ds; MQDB -> M Q D B; MQDs -> M Q Ds; MQD -> M Q D; MGX -> M G X; OCP -> O C P
A quick disconnect is a specified fluid interface. It lets assemblies connect and separate using a defined mating arrangement. Hand-mate connections are engaged directly by the operator. Blind-mate arrangements engage as equipment moves into its intended installed position, with a mechanism designed to accommodate the permitted alignment conditions.

Universal quick disconnects, or U Q Ds, and M G X Quick Disconnects, or M Q Ds, are different named families. M Q D and M Q D B now have a published O C P companion specification. The M G X Academy course explains where the specified connector arrangements fit into rack and tray integration.

For an introductory review, keep the family, size, revision and mating method together. Then check the fluid, operating conditions and intended location. A similar appearance, a matching label or a nominal size doesn't establish that two products can mate or perform correctly together.

Blind mating also connects mechanical and fluid requirements. The rack, tray, supports and coupling mechanism all contribute to the installed interface. Qualification must address that arrangement rather than an isolated connector illustration.

You don't need to memorize dimensions here. The practical question is whether the selected mating parts and their application have the required compatibility and qualification evidence.
