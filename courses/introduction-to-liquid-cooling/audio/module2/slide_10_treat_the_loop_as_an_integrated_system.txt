Treat a direct liquid-cooling loop as an integrated system. Cold plates acquire heat, manifolds distribute fluid, and the supply and return paths carry the required conditions to the equipment. A heat exchanger transfers that heat to a supported facility path.

The fluid must be qualified with the actual wetted materials, and connections must match their specified interfaces and applications. Reliability then depends on preparation, monitoring, maintenance and clear ownership. Before accepting a proposal, follow the heat and check the handoffs. A successful component test doesn't establish the performance of the complete installation.
