**Title:** OCP Project Brazos: Liquid Cooling in Air-Cooled Data Centers

**Short description:** Evaluate Brazos liquid-to-air cooling, performance limits, rack integration, controls, and deployment readiness.

**Series placement:** Standalone course.

**Audience:** Intermediate data-center engineers, thermal engineers, rack integrators, and operations teams.

**Duration:** Approximately 53 minutes; target range 45–60 minutes. Optional external courses, reference reading, replays, and retries add time.

**Overview:** Learn how OCP Project Brazos supports liquid-cooled IT in an air-cooled facility, and how to assess whether a particular installation is ready. Follow the heat path, interpret performance conditions, examine integration and service requirements, and use control and validation evidence to make a qualified deployment decision. The course is based on specification v0.75.4, effective September 11, 2026, with Google's June 2026 announcement providing context.

- **Follow the Heat Through Brazos — approximately 12 minutes.** Trace the closed liquid loop and the facility air path. Distinguish chassis components, the three-chassis sidecar, and the site's continuing heat-removal responsibility.
- **Cooling Performance — approximately 14 minutes.** Interpret nominal thermal capacity, airflow, pump curves, approach temperature, and cooling electrical power with their published boundaries and conditions.
- **Integrate and Service the Rack — approximately 13 minutes.** Review installation geometry, liquid connections, compatible fluids, qualified parts, rack interlocks, leak detection, and conditional whole-chassis service continuity.
- **Control, Monitor, and Validate — approximately 14 minutes.** Explain default control loops, startup and supervisory handover, interface roles, fault behavior, and the evidence required to accept a deployment.

Four narrated knowledge checks provide two questions each. Motion explanations follow narration with synchronized playback controls. Optional links continue into the Academy's Cooling Fluids in DLC and Project Deschutes CDU courses.
