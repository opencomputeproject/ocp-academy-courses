**Title**\
OCP Project Brazos: Liquid Cooling in Air-Cooled Data Centers

**Short Description**\
Evaluate Brazos liquid-to-air cooling, performance limits, rack integration, controls, and deployment readiness.

**Long Description**

Learn how OCP Project Brazos supports liquid-cooled IT in an air-cooled facility, and how to assess whether a particular installation is ready. Follow the heat path, interpret performance conditions, examine integration and service requirements, and use control and validation evidence to make a qualified deployment decision. The course is based on specification v0.75.4, effective September 11, 2026, with Google's June 2026 announcement providing context.

**Overall duration: 53 minutes**

**Module 1: Follow the Heat Through Brazos — 12 minutes.**\
Trace the closed liquid loop and the facility air path. Distinguish chassis components, the three-chassis sidecar, and the site's continuing heat-removal responsibility.

**Module 2: Cooling Performance — 14 minutes.**\
Interpret nominal thermal capacity, airflow, pump curves, approach temperature, and cooling electrical power with their published boundaries and conditions.

**Module 3: Integrate and Service the Rack — 13 minutes.**\
Review installation geometry, liquid connections, compatible fluids, qualified parts, rack interlocks, leak detection, and conditional whole-chassis service continuity.

**Module 4: Control, Monitor, and Validate — 14 minutes.**\
Explain default control loops, startup and supervisory handover, interface roles, fault behavior, and the evidence required to accept a deployment.
